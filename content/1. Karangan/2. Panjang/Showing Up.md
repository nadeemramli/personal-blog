Recently, I started to realize that the matter of showing up is a by product of "dressing up".

It has been no-brainer nowadays that fortune favors those who are consistent. 

If you keep on showing up, you will end up lucky. 

But the matter of showing up, is depending on how you "dress up". I was looking at a content by the Futur, and they are so consistent and building their content and giving values, it become a brand. It's a brand cause people's expectation to towards them has been meet many times consistently. And it's great!

But I wonder why myself unable to show up everyday, I have too many things on my plate, and I love to share it, but I don't like to share anything about it. While I'm seeing a person shared simple things, but that's the beauty of it, it's simple and it's great, you should share it.

Until I realize that it's all about how you dress up. Your identity and how you perceive yourself has bigger impact of your showing up, rather than the act of showing up itself (the intent). 

If you look good everyday and walk everyday at NYC, most probably doing a vlog won't be a problem, cause there's nothing wrong with that. Same with showing up, in anything that we want to show up to, there's dress up to it, if you find that, and dress well, showing up won't be a problem.

Readiness comes with preparation, I think showing up required dressing up.
