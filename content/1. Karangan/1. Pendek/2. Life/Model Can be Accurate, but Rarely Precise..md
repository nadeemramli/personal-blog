The problem for model thinker is that they can mainly be accurate, don't talk about compass or pursuing "why" with them, cause you ultimately able to dig down root causes with model thinkers. 

However, model thinkers are rarely precise. Their ideas, plan or strategy can be seen as "logic" or simply ideal.

But the lack of nuances is what causing them to fails, and most of the time failure didn't cause them to reinvent the model, it instead reinforce them. 

So what do we need to become a model thinker? and what do we need to be precise?


Trial and error

Through selecting objective, decomposing objectives to quantifiable goal, suffer and stumble in the process of achieving the goal, iterate, learn, gain insights, course correct and scale. 

This is how you be precise, that insights is nuances.
