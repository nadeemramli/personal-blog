A big fat problem in my life is my lacking sense of urgency. I know that I want to do a lot, but I didn't do it. I plan it out, I read more books, and when every time that discomfort exist, I shove it off by distracting myself.

I started to aim lower, lowering my standard, so I could feel accomplish. I uses vanity metrics so I could be more comfortable, I could have the false confidence.  I start to work on other people businesses, hoping that one day I could learn how he did it so I can do it one day. I work my ass off as a distraction of my own will and dream. I uses excuses, such as bad luck, not my strength, I don't like it, cause the time has not yet come.

But your future and dream shouldn't waiting for that. Your life didn't begin later on, your life begins now. 

A quote from a dude that I recently read about and his life seems fun, not just fun but that peaceful fun stuff.

He started a company that introduces supersonic speed for commercial, he founded the company that build the first civil supersonic jet. 

![[Pasted image 20250131054613.png]]

He quote in his bio, "Life is short so if you want to do a lot, it helps to move fast".

I needed this, I needed this.

I need to understand why the rush, why urgency, and this is why. 

I want to do a lot, I have a lot of ideas, and dreams to achieve, I HAVE TO MOVE FAST.
