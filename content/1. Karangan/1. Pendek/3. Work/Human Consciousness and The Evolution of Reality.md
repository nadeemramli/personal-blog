Let me help break down and explore this interesting philosophical question. It touches on both human consciousness and the nature of progress. Here's my elaboration:

1. **The Child Analogy**
- Just as a child gradually builds their understanding of a relatively stable world around them, humanity has been expanding our collective understanding of reality
- The fundamental laws of nature (physics, chemistry, biology) remain constant, but our comprehension grows

2. **Two Perspectives**:

a) **Expanding Consciousness in a Stable World**
- Like your first suggestion, we could be simply getting better at understanding what was always there
- Examples:
  - Gravity existed before Newton named it
  - Quantum mechanics existed before we discovered it
  - The human brain worked the same way before we began understanding neuroscience

b) **World Evolution Driving New Understanding**
- The world (especially human society) is changing rapidly
- New technologies create new realities to understand
- Examples:
  - Digital technology creating virtual spaces
  - Social media changing human interaction
  - AI raising new questions about consciousness and intelligence

3. **The Reality: It's Both**
- We are simultaneously:
  - Discovering more about the unchanging natural world
  - Creating new complexities through human progress
  - Developing new tools that allow us to understand both better

4. **The Acceleration Factor**
- Unlike a child's linear learning, humanity's understanding is growing exponentially
- Each discovery enables more discoveries
- New technologies accelerate both our ability to understand and the complexity of what we need to understand

This creates a fascinating feedback loop: our expanding consciousness leads to new developments, which in turn create new aspects of reality for us to comprehend, driving further expansion of our consciousness.
