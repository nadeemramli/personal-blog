"A quote from Alex Hormozi. Give away your secrets because only 1% of people are willing to implement it themselves. Then sell to the people who would rather pay for the implementation. Because most people need help."

I've recently encounter a new discovery of how the future gonna looks like.

In the past, digital tools specifically (saas) have been helping people to be more productive and more effective in their work, the growth of tools has been slowly transfer into growth of services. 

More and more software services have grown due to the nature of growth of tools.

But now we're seeing more decentralization of services and tools, due to AI. Nowadays anyone can work on the full cycle of software development, which used to be 1 company work, can now turn to 1 person work. 

We're seeing people using Chatprd, for product management, figma AI for prototyping etc. 

The development of taste and standard also increases as what used to be hard and challenging, now have become basic and meh. 

I believe the majority of early innovators would quickly grasp the idea and become the new norms. This might take a while but it's changes that would happen. 

My question would be what would be the future looks like, and how can we be a part of it.

Well soon, the idea of implementation will definitely breach as the idea of "how" can be quickly figure out.

More and more people will fail trying, and more and more people would start doing.

People would look for exposure, ideas and ways to improve their life, but now can quickly achieve it.

The media would strengthen as exposure would be the limitation of human capability.

Slowest path is the shortest path.
