I love to think that to solves bigger problem, we need depth of the problem. 

This fallacy fundamentally come from the fact that one of the biggest problem solving methodologies, is Root Cause Analysis.

and one of the biggest solution principle there is First Principle. 

So we thought that by digging it deeper, we finally get to the answer. 

However going back to the root cause and aswell first principle, it might be that we're just forgotten. 

It's imperative for us to kept on looking back and learn, rather than looking forward and improve.
