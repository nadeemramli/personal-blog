## Approach
-----
The philosophy of our approach lies on the mental model of optionality. The idea is that, in life to achieve success and immense wealth, it requires "luck" factor. 

Luck can't be engineered, however we can increase the probability of getting lucky. This requires trial and error, and just Luck Surface Area. But working on a lot of things does increase your surface area, but it is not sustainable. 

What people don't realize is that, there's Ergodicity factor in betting. Which is time constraint.  

Imagine that, betting on something will produce two factor: Probability of Success, and Reward.
But to bet you will need: Resources/Loss.

Things we have to understand is that, we all have limited resources. So by just betting aggressively won't cut it, cause all that resources we have such as mental energy, attention space, money, and hope, all these scarce. 

So how do we manage our approach better?

Enter, Barbell Strategy and Kelly Criterion.

### The Compounding Engine (The 80-90% Barbell)

This is the safe, robust foundation of your strategy. The primary goal here is survival, steady growth, and the generation of resources for your bets.

1. Knowledge + Skills
	- **Function:** The safest, most reliable compounding investment you can make. The "return" is guaranteed, as the knowledge becomes part of you.
	- **Barbell Logic:** This is the bedrock of the "safe" end. Time spent learning is never a "loss."
	- **Systemic Impact:** Every skill you master (e.g., copywriting, video editing, coding) directly increases the estimated **probability of success (p)** for any future bet you make. A more skilled operator can turn a 5% chance into a 15% chance, making more bets viable under the Kelly logic.
2. Systems-to-Assets
	- **Function:** Turning your effort and knowledge into tangible or digital property that works for you. A system (e.g., your weekly content process) creates an asset (e.g., your YouTube channel).
	- **Barbell Logic:** These assets form the heavy, stable weight on the safe side of your barbell. They are designed for predictable, cumulative returns and resilience.
	- **Systemic Impact:** These assets generate the **"betting bankroll"**—the disposable resources (capital, attention, time) that you will allocate to the risky side of the barbell. The larger and more effective your assets, the more fuel you have for bets.
3. Trust-to-Relationships-to-Audience
	- **Function:** Building social capital, which is the ultimate multiplier. Trust is the core element, which solidifies into relationships, and scales into an audience.
	- **Barbell Logic:** This is a "compounding" force that enhances the stability of your safe assets (a loyal audience is resilient) and dramatically improves the potential of your risky bets.
	- **Systemic Impact:** A loyal audience massively increases the **payoff odds (b)** of any bet. Launching a product to 10,000 engaged followers has a vastly higher potential reward than launching to zero. It transforms the Kelly calculation, making marginal bets become highly attractive.

### The Bets Portfolio (The 10-20% Barbell)
This is the anti-fragile, high-upside part of your strategy. The goal is to take multiple, small, calculated risks with capped downside and explosive, uncapped upside. This is how you court "luck."

1. PRDs + MVPs + Traction/Launch
	- **Function:** This is the disciplined, operational process for executing a "bet." It's not random gambling; it's a structured experiment.
	- **Barbell Logic:** This process manages your 10-20% high-risk allocation. Each cycle is one small weight placed on the risky end of the barbell.
	- **Systemic Impact & Kelly Logic:**
	    - **PRD (Product Requirements Document) - The Hypothesis:** This is the **Kelly Criterion thought process** made manifest. Before writing a line of code or spending a dollar, you define the bet. You must honestly estimate the cost (**Loss**), the potential **Reward** (to find b), and the **Probability of success (p)**. If the resulting Kelly fraction (f∗) is too low or negative, you kill the idea here, at zero cost.
	    - **MVP (Minimum Viable Product) - The Capped Wager:** This is the physical act of placing the bet. It is engineered to be the smallest possible experiment to test your hypothesis, strictly **capping your downside**. You are deploying the small, calculated fraction of your bankroll that the Kelly logic deemed appropriate.
	    - **Traction/Launch - The Outcome & Feedback Loop:**
	        - **Success:** The bet pays off. The reward is a massive injection of resources (capital, data, credibility) that you feed **back into the Compounding Engine**, strengthening your foundation and enabling bigger, better bets in the future.
	        - **Failure:** The bet results in a zero. Because the loss was capped and small, your foundation is unharmed (you've beaten ergodicity). The failure itself is a resource: the knowledge gained is fed back into your **"Knowledge + Skills"** base, making your next PRD more accurate.

“Don’t worry about failure, you only have to be right once.” – Drew Houston
