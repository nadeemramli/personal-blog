The core principle here is that you can't keep playing after you've lost all your money. Even if the expected value of a game is positive, you might not be able to achieve a positive return unless you have infinitely deep pockets in order to keep playing. In the example he gives - with 100 players, if player 28 loses all their money it's fine because the others can keep playing. But, in the example with one player, if he loses all his money on Day 28, then he can no longer play on day 29, 30... etc.
