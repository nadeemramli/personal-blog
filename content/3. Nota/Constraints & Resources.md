# High Impact Question
---
Developing questions for better alignment:

1. Considering my _time_ and _physical energy_, what is the maximum sustained effort I can dedicate? 
	1. First, to building reliable, compounding results? 
	2. Second, for potentially intensive but shorter bursts often required for initial stages of a 'bet'?
2. Where should I strategically direct my limited _mental capacity_ and _attention span_ to gain the most significant long-term growth?
3. How much can I afford my _mental capacity_ to allocate to exploring high-risk, high-reward ventures without jeopardizing my core compounding efforts?
4. How can I best utilize my existing _social capital_ and _competency_ to both enhance my consistent compounding activities?
5. Have you identify and potentially succeed in carefully chosen 'bets' where these strengths might offer an advantage?
6. What portion of my available _capital_ am I realistically comfortable allocating to lower-risk, compounding assets versus a smaller, calculated amount for higher-risk 'bets' with significant potential upside, keeping in mind the possibility of losing the capital allocated to bets?
7. How do my current _reputation_ and accumulated _experiences_ influence my decisions for both steady compounding (building on established credibility) and new, potentially transformative 'bets' (applying past learnings)
	1. What are the potential impacts on my reputation in either scenario?
