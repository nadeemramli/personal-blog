## What this is

Everything else on this blog has a job. "If You're So Smart" is about getting your life in order. "Don't Be an Ambitious Bum" is about converting competency into equity. They are useful. They are supposed to be useful.

This space has no job.

**I'm Just Curious** is where I take something I kept wondering about and break it down until it actually makes sense to me. Not because it pays off, not because it optimises anything, but because the question wouldn't leave me alone. If a topic earns its place here, it is only because I found it genuinely interesting and wanted to see the machinery underneath.

So the rule is simple: the only thing a topic has to do to belong here is make me curious.

## How these breakdowns work

A few habits carry across everything in this folder:

- **First principles, not first results.** I start from "what is this thing, really?" and build up, rather than starting from the application. The goal is understanding the mechanism, not memorising the conclusion.
- **One layer beneath the obvious.** Most explanations stop at the level you already half-know. I try to go one floor down: not "steroids build muscle" but "what is a steroid, structurally, and why does that structure let it talk to your DNA at all?"
- **Honest about the edges.** Where the science is settled, I say so. Where it is contested or where I'm reasoning past my certainty, I flag it.
- **Curiosity-only.** These are not how-to guides. When a topic has a practical companion elsewhere on the blog, I link to it and stay in my lane (understanding the thing, not telling you what to do with it).

The voice is "here is what fascinated me and here is what I found," kept rigorous (real sources, real structure) but written like I'm explaining it to a friend who asked.
