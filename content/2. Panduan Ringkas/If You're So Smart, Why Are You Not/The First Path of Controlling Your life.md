The closest authentic quote comes from Xenophon’s Memorabilia (Book 3, Chapter 12), where Socrates discusses physical training and the potential of the human body.

Here’s the relevant passage, as translated by E.C. Marchant (1923):

"Besides, it is a disgrace to grow old through sheer carelessness before seeing what manner of man you may become by developing your bodily strength and beauty to their highest limit."

The full version often cited in modern contexts, including fitness circles, is:

"No man has the right to be an amateur in the matter of physical training. It is a shame for a man to grow old without seeing the beauty and strength of which his body is capable."
