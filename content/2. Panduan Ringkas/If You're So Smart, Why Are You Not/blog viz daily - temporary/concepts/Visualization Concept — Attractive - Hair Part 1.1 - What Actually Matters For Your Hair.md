## Series context & the key point _(why this concept, not a generic one)_

This is the **biology/diagnostic foundation of the 3-part Hair series**, and it has exactly one job: install the idea that **hair loss is the rare cosmetic problem whose master variable is _time_**, because a follicle slides through three states in **one direction only**, and one of the boundaries it crosses is **permanent**. The article's own opening line is the thesis: *"Every month a follicle spends miniaturized without intervention is a month closer to fibrosis — the point where it stops being a chemistry problem and becomes a surgery problem."*

The article makes four structural moves the poster must carry:

1. **THE central insight — the one-way slide and the irreversible line.** `[Healthy Terminal] → [Miniaturized] → [Fibrosis]`, mapped to `(Fully Recoverable) → (Partially Recoverable) → (Permanently Dead)`. State 2 is *the window where the protocol does its real work*; State 3 is *the point of no return* — once the pore seals over with scar tissue, *"no drug, peptide, or compound currently on the market or in clinical trials can rebuild a fibrosed follicle."* This boundary, the article says, *"determines everything downstream."* It is the single most important object on the poster.
2. **Follicles are a finite, non-renewable lot.** *"You are born with a fixed number of hair follicles, and that number only goes down."* ~100,000 at birth, no new ones ever made. This is the same finite-resource framing as Fit's satellite cells and Skin's dermal junction — the protocol is **preservation, not creation.**
3. **The stack assumes a living host.** Every compound rescues a State-2 follicle or protects a State-1 follicle; **none rebuild State 3.** *"Anything that claims otherwise is selling apple stem cells."* So the chemistry only operates on the **left of the line** — the right side is surgical (transplant = physically importing live follicles from a DHT-immune donor zone).
4. **You diagnose where you are with three KPIs, three measurement tiers, and one at-home tell.** The KPIs: **Terminal-to-Vellus ratio**, **Follicular Unit Density (hairs/cm²)**, **Average Shaft Diameter (μm)** — same zone, re-measured at 6 and 12 months. The tiers: smartphone dermatoscope (rough) → Hair Doc RM50 scan (moderate) → clinical trichoscopy (gold). And the **field test that triages a zone in five seconds: peach fuzz under bright light = still alive (left of the line); slick, shiny, pore-less skin = fibrosed (right of the line).**

**The single thing a reader must walk away owning:** *Your follicles are a fixed lot that only shrinks, and each one slides one way — terminal → miniaturized → fibrosed. There is a hard line in that slide. Left of it, chemistry can pull a starved follicle back; right of it, the structure is razed, the lot is sealed, and only surgery imports a new one. The entire game is measuring which side of the line each zone is on — and acting while it's still on the chemistry side.*

That is an **ordered, one-directional, asymmetric degradation across a finite parcel, split by a permanent boundary** — which is why the honest anchor is, again, an **architect's drawing**, but this time a **structural-condition survey**: the same structure shown in three states of dilapidation along a time axis, with a condemnation line you cannot redevelop across. The article literally hands us *"anchor,"* *"structure,"* *"stem-cell pocket,"* *"the pore seals over,"* *"point of no return,"* *"the line."* We render the survey it's describing.

## 1. The Big Idea

_One sentence: **A hair follicle is a structure on a one-way dilapidation timeline — sound → distressed-but-restorable → condemned-and-sealed — and there is a single permanent line in that slide past which chemistry cannot reach, so the only number that matters is how much salvageable structure is still standing on the left of it.** The poster must make the reader feel, instantly, the irreversibility (time runs one way, the line is final) and the agency (everything left of the line is rescuable right now) — and that the job is preservation of a finite lot, not the fantasy of "growing more hair."_

## 2. Visual Metaphor & Structure — the hero object

**A structural-condition survey of one follicle drawn in three states across a left-to-right time axis, cut by a hard vertical "CONDEMNATION LINE" — the fibrosis point of no return — past which the structure is razed and the lot is sealed.** This is the anchor; nothing replaces it. The follicle is drawn as what it literally is: a slender **structure sunk into the dermis** (a sectioned column / standpipe anchored in ground), so the article's own words — anchor, pocket, the pore sealing over — render as architecture without forcing.

Read left to right (this matters — the eye must travel *time's one-way arrow*, the way the argument insists):

- **State 1 — HEALTHY TERMINAL (left, drawn full and sound).** A thick, upright, fully-detailed column anchored deep, captioned `SOUND · fully recoverable`. Sub-label `2–4 hairs / unit · genetic max`. Rendered in the warm load-bearing register (terracotta), blueprint-crisp, with a small "preventive maintenance" tag: `keep DHT blocked · blood up · inflammation down`.

- **State 2 — MINIATURIZED (center, drawn distressed but standing, under scaffolding).** A visibly **thinner, shorter, leaning** column — alive but starved — wrapped in **restoration scaffolding**, captioned `DISTRESSED · partially recoverable`. This is the emotional and strategic heart: a bold bracket labels it **`← THE WINDOW →`** with the line *"where the protocol does its real work."* A **recovery arrow curves back toward State 1** (terracotta, solid) to show miniaturization is reversible — *and the depth of the lean encodes how complete the recovery is* (a small "recovery completeness" gradient: fresh distress comes back fully, decade-old distress only partially). This is the only place on the poster where motion points *backward against time* — chemistry buying back lost ground.

- **═══ THE CONDEMNATION LINE ═══** a single hard **vertical** rule between State 2 and State 3, the most important mark on the poster. Margin labels: on its left `CHEMISTRY REACHES HERE →` (in warm ink), on its right `← SURGERY ONLY` (in alarm red). A tiny crossing caption: `the pore seals. nothing crosses back.` **The recovery arrow from State 2 explicitly stops dead at this line** — it never crosses. This is the exact functional twin of Appearance 1.0's epistemic line, re-purposed: there, the line split *mechanism vs convention*; here it splits *recoverable vs permanent*.

- **State 3 — FIBROSIS (right, drawn razed and sealed).** The column is **gone**: a **paved-over, smooth, pore-less plot** with a faint ghost of where the structure stood and a small `CONDEMNED` survey-stamp. Captioned `SEALED · permanently dead`. Rendered in the condemnation register (flattened, hatched-out, the lone alarm red) — deliberately *low-detail, because there is nothing left to detail.* One small importing arrow comes **in from outside the frame** labeled `TRANSPLANT — live follicle imported from a DHT-immune donor zone`, the only thing that ever populates the right side.

**The two-register surface (the device, reused).** Everything **left of the line is the "restoration register"** — blueprint-precise, scaffolded, callout leaders, dimension ticks, drawn as *live and under-repair*. Everything **right of the line is the "condemnation register"** — sealed, hatched, stamped, no fine linework. The surface itself teaches reversible-vs-permanent before a single word is read. This is the single most distinctive move and the thing that makes the poster un-generic.

**The finite-lot foundation (below grade, the borrowed/fixed-parcel device).** Beneath all three states runs the **ground / parcel line**, captioned `THE LOT IS FIXED — ~100,000 follicles at birth. The number only goes down. No new lots are ever zoned.` Rendered in ghosted graphite (present, load-bearing, but un-creatable) — carrying the finite-resource thesis in one gesture, the same way Appearance carried its "borrowed foundation."

Why this metaphor fits and nothing else does: the article's literal claims are *one direction* (time's arrow), *three discrete states*, *a permanent boundary* (the point of no return), and *a finite non-renewable substrate*. A left-to-right structural-condition survey with a hard vertical condemnation line and a fixed parcel-line is the only object that holds all four at once — it is directional, staged, splittable by a permanent line, and seatable on a fixed lot. A "hair growth cycle" loop (anagen/catagen) would imply *reversibility everywhere* and bury the one irreversible fact that runs the whole series; a before/after head shot teaches nothing and hides the line.

## 3. Layout Description

**Landscape-leaning or square (~4:3 / 1:1)** is primary here — unlike Appearance's portrait elevation — because the hero is a **horizontal time axis** and needs left-to-right room for three states + the line. (A portrait variant works for the blog hero by stacking the spec-sheet rail beneath the survey; landscape is primary.)

- **Top band (~12%):** the headline (see §4), set small against the bone paper, kerned wide, thin drafting border beneath it, and a tiny `Part 1.1 of 3 · Hair · the Attractive pillar` slug. A faint **left-pointing-to-right time arrow** `TIME →` runs under the border to prime the reading direction.
- **Center stage (~58%) — the hero:** the three-state structural survey spans the full width, anchored on the **fixed-parcel ground line** at its base. State 1 (sound) left → State 2 (scaffolded, `THE WINDOW`, backward recovery arrow) center → **CONDEMNATION LINE** → State 3 (sealed, `CONDEMNED`, transplant import-arrow) right. The condemnation line is vertical, full-height of the survey, the highest-contrast object in the frame.
- **Right rail OR lower-third — the spec sheet (~18%):** docked as an instrument card, three pieces:
  - **Three KPI gauges** in the cool slate-blue measurement register: `T:V RATIO ▸ >85% terminal` / `DENSITY ▸ 180–250 hairs·cm⁻²` / `SHAFT Ø ▸ 60–80 μm`, each with a tiny up/down legend (`↑ reversing · ↓ active loss`) and the rule `same zone · re-measure 6 & 12 mo`.
  - **The measurement-tier ladder:** three rungs — `T1 phone dermatoscope · RM50–200 · rough` / `T2 Hair Doc scan · RM50 analysis-only · moderate` / `T3 clinical trichoscopy · RM300–800 · ±5% gold` — with the Malaysian budget grace note `T2: pay RM50, demand the images, skip the package`.
  - **The at-home tell (its own bordered callout, a magnifier lens):** split lens — left half `PEACH FUZZ → alive · left of the line` (warm), right half `SLICK SKIN → fibrosed · right of the line` (red). This is the reader's five-second field test and must read as a single memorable object.
- **Bottom band (~12%):** the thesis lockup (see §4) plus the **decision-flow micro-strip**: `SOUND → prevent · DISTRESSED → rescue · SEALED → accept (surgical)` — three chips color-coded to the three states.

Reading path is deliberate and matches the argument: **enter low-left on the fixed parcel → travel right through sound → distressed (notice `THE WINDOW` and the backward rescue arrow) → hit the condemnation line and feel it stop you → see the sealed lot → drop to the spec sheet to learn how you'd know which side you're on → exit on the decision strip.**

## 4. Text Elements _(keep minimal — every word earns its place)_

- **Headline (pick one):**
  - `THE LINE ONLY CROSSES ONE WAY.` (strongest — names the irreversibility)
  - `EVERY MONTH IS A MONTH CLOSER.` (straight from the text; urgency)
  - `YOU CAN'T REBUILD A SEALED LOT.` (concrete, memorable)
  - `FOLLICLES ARE FINITE. THE SLIDE IS ONE-WAY.` (titular, safe)
- **State labels (left→right):** `SOUND · fully recoverable` / `DISTRESSED · partially recoverable` / `SEALED · permanently dead`.
- **The window (center):** `← THE WINDOW →` · sub-line `where the protocol does its real work.`
- **The condemnation line (margins):** `CHEMISTRY REACHES HERE →` (left, warm) · `← SURGERY ONLY` (right, red). Crossing caption: `the pore seals. nothing crosses back.`
- **The fixed parcel (ground line):** `THE LOT IS FIXED — ~100,000 at birth, only ever fewer. No new lots are zoned.`
- **Transplant import-arrow:** `TRANSPLANT — a live follicle imported from a DHT-immune donor zone.`
- **Spec sheet KPIs:** `T:V RATIO >85%` · `DENSITY 180–250 /cm²` · `SHAFT Ø 60–80 μm` · legend `↑ reversing · ↓ active loss · same zone · 6 & 12 mo`.
- **The at-home tell (magnifier):** `PEACH FUZZ = ALIVE` (left) · `SLICK SKIN = SEALED` (right).
- **Thesis lockup (bottom):** `STAY ON THE CHEMISTRY SIDE OF THE LINE.`
- **Decision strip (bottom):** `SOUND → prevent · DISTRESSED → rescue · SEALED → accept.`

Nothing else. No paragraph of body copy, no compound list (that's Part 2.0's payload) — the survey *is* the argument.

## 5. Color & Mood

**Mood:** the same calm, exacting confidence of a drafting table as Appearance — precise, grown-up, unhurried — but with a thin current of *consequence* running under it, because this article's subject is an irreversible deadline. Not "hair-loss-ad anxiety," not before/after hope-selling. The feeling of a structural engineer quietly telling you which walls are still load-bearing and which are already gone. The hard vertical condemnation line is where the calm picks up its single note of alarm.

**Palette — the locked Attractive signature, carried over verbatim from Appearance 1.0:**
- **Background:** warm bone / drafting paper (`#F4F1EA`), faint blueprint grid or drafting-dot texture, very low contrast.
- **Ink / linework / type:** graphite near-black (`#1E2024`) for all structure and labels.
- **Accent 1 — LIVING / load-bearing warm (`#C2703D` terracotta):** the **sound and distressed follicles** (States 1 & 2 — the chemistry side, the recoverable structure) and the backward recovery arrow. Warm = alive, real, salvageable.
- **Accent 2 — MEASUREMENT / observation cool (`#5E7585` slate-blue):** the **spec-sheet instruments only** — the three KPI gauges, the tier ladder, dimension ticks. Cool = the observing/diagnostic layer, never the tissue itself. (In Appearance this cool encoded "convention"; here it encodes "the measuring instrument" — same role: the cooler, lighter register that observes rather than carries load.)
- **The fixed parcel:** ghosted graphite / grey only — present, essential, but un-creatable (exactly as Appearance's borrowed foundation).
- **One alarm color — fibrosis only (`#D9483B` signal red):** used *exclusively* for the **CONDEMNATION LINE, the SEALED State-3 lot, the `CONDEMNED` stamp, and the `SLICK SKIN = SEALED` half of the at-home tell.** Scarcity is what gives it force — red = permanent, irreversible, the failure state. This faithfully reuses the locked "red-for-failure only" rule (fibrosis *is* the irreversible failure).

The warm-left / red-right split does double duty: it's handsome *and* it encodes the recoverable→permanent crossing, so the color teaches the one-way line before a word is read.

## 6. Style Reference _(the locked Attractive-pillar style — embedded so the poster chat needs only [this concept + design.md])_

**"Architect's Elevation / Spec Sheet" — a precise structural drawing with editorial restraint.** (Locked for the Attractive pillar by Appearance 1.0; do not change.) Touchstones:

- The honesty and labeled-dimension discipline of a **real architectural section / condition survey** (callout leaders, dimension ticks, hatching for "material," a surveyor's `CONDEMNED` stamp) — keeps it credible, not decorative.
- The cross-section + annotation elegance of **editorial explainer graphics** (Monocle / Kinfolk typographic calm; a Wait-But-Why concept drafted by an architect, not sketched by hand).
- A clear **two-register surface** as the signature move: here, **restoration-blueprint left of the condemnation line, sealed/condemnation-hatch right of it** — the analogue of Appearance's mechanism/convention split, and the single thing that makes this article's poster un-generic.

It sits as a **Hair-track sub-archetype inside the locked Attractive style**, the way Fit has many sub-archetypes (gauge cluster, oscilloscope, assembly schematic) inside its "Engineering Diagnostic Instrument." Series family for reference: **Fit = the daylit engineering instrument on a workbench; Sleep = the same rigor after dark (clinical nocturne); Attractive = the same rigor on the drafting table (architect's elevation)** — Appearance rendered it as an *elevation built upward*; Hair renders it as a *condition survey read across time*. Same bone-paper palette, same warm-living / cool-instrument coding, same single-alarm-red discipline, same hard-line device — different drawing.

## 7. What to Avoid

- **The cliché of a balding scalp, a Norwood-scale head chart, a hairline-receding "before/after," or a hand running through thinning hair.** It says "hair-loss ad" and teaches nothing — worse, the before/after frame *hides the line*, which is the one idea the article exists to install. Never make a real head or a real hairline the hero. (A Norwood pattern may appear only as a tiny pictogram inside the Day-1 spec sheet, never as the anchor.)
- **A circular hair-growth-cycle loop (anagen → catagen → telogen) as the hero.** A loop implies *everything comes back around* — the exact opposite of the article's master fact (one direction, one permanent line). The cycle is real but it is *not this article's point*; at most it's a small linear grace note under State 1 (`structure breathes on a 2–7 yr cycle`), never the central object.
- (Bonus to avoid: softening or omitting the condemnation line, or rendering all three states in one uniform register — that collapses recoverable and permanent into the same visual claim, which is precisely the false hope ("apple stem cells") the article is built to refuse. The line must be the hardest mark on the page.)
