## Series context & the key point _(why this concept, not a generic one)_

Part 4.0 told the reader *you live on Level 1* and gestured at "the base" as a single always-on rail. **Part 4.1 is that base, fully wired.** It is the longest, most concrete article in the track: thirteen legal ingredients, dosed, timed, split into two tiers, and costed in ringgit. A generic "here are my supplements" graphic would betray it. The article makes four moves the poster has to carry, and they fight a specific instinct (that a stack is a *pile of pills you pick from*):

1. **A stack is an engine, not a pile.** The article's spine is a literal **six-act bio-circuit** where every part only makes sense in relation to the others: Fuel & Power (creatine / tyrosine / ALCAR) → Wiring & the learning network (the uridine + choline + DHA Kennedy trinity, Bacopa) → Logistics / blood supply (ginkgo) → Spark & alpha-wave regulator (caffeine + theanine) → Acetylcholine guard (alpha-GPC + huperzine) → Mood & neurogenesis (saffron, Lion's Mane). The wiring *is* the argument: you cannot stamp the accelerator with no fuel lines, no coolant, no chassis.

2. **Two tiers, two power rails.** The payoff structure is a **stimulant-free Daily Baseline that runs 7 days a week** (it builds physical brain *structure*: membranes, mood, new wiring) and a **Heavy Execution overlay that runs only on the 4–5 real output days** (the stimulants, the raw horsepower). The rest days are not laziness; they reset caffeine tolerance, clear the accumulating huperzine, and stretch the bottles. You build structure every day; you only spend the stimulants when there is a result to produce.

3. **The cost inversion (the load-bearing surprise).** The article's most counterintuitive, self-declared point, the same myth-buster the PE finale runs: ==the "smart drug" half is the *cheap* half.== The always-on **structural** stack (Lion's Mane, CDP-choline, uridine, fish oil) is ~RM 200/month and dominates the spend; the switched **stimulant** overlay is only ~RM 80/month and the capsules last most of a year. The brain-building half is where the money goes, not the glamorous "nootropic" half.

4. **The Golden Rule: never max a single compound.** A dozen modest inputs covering every pathway out-perform one heroic dose of a single hero compound, every time (the inverted-U from Part 1.1). Low-dose synergy beats max-dosing, always.

**The misconception the article exists to kill:** that a good stack is a *shopping list of strong single ingredients* you assemble by picking the best ones, and that the impressive stimulants are the expensive, important part. The truth is the opposite: it is **one integrated machine run off two power rails**, where the unglamorous always-on structural rail is both the bulk of the cost and the half that actually rebuilds the brain, and where every dial sits at a *modest* detent on purpose.

**The single thing a reader must walk away owning:** *The natural stack is a complete engine wired off two rails. The structural rail never switches off (7 days, it builds the hardware, and it is the expensive half). The stimulant rail is switched on only for output days and is the cheap, intermittent half. You don't max any one dial; you run a dozen modest ones across every pathway.*

That is a **fully-assembled, dual-rail engine schematic** — which is why the only honest anchor is *the complete wired board with two power rails*, not a grid of bottles. Part 4.0 drew the empty base rail and said "you live here." Part 4.1 shows the entire engine that rail powers.

## 1. The Big Idea

_One sentence: **The natural stack is not a pile of pills you pick from — it's a single neural engine wired off two power rails, where an always-on STRUCTURAL rail (7 days, the brain-building hardware, and quietly the expensive half) stays lit while a switched STIMULANT rail (output days only, the cheap half) powers the spark, and every dial on the board sits at a modest detent because a dozen small inputs beat one slammed fader.** The poster should make the reader feel, instantly, that they are looking at one designed machine with a permanently-glowing structural rail and a switch-gated stimulant rail — and feel the jolt that the always-on half is the big spend, inverting the assumption they walked in with._

## 2. Visual Metaphor & Structure — the hero object

**A complete, fully-wired "neural engine" board read top-to-bottom, powered by TWO vertical supply rails: a permanently-lit STRUCTURAL rail and a switch-gated STIMULANT rail.** This is the anchor. It is the assembled engine that Part 4.0's single empty base rail only promised — a deliberate intra-track callback (4.0's lone base rail, now built out into a full board).

- **The two power rails (the spine of the piece, running the full height):**
  - **Left margin — the STRUCTURAL RAIL:** a thick, steadily-glowing cyan bus labeled `STRUCTURAL RAIL · ALWAYS ON · 7 DAYS`, sub-captioned `builds the hardware: membranes · mood · new wiring`. It has **no switch** — it cannot be turned off. It is the direct descendant of 4.0's base rail and should visually rhyme with it. Fed from below by `SLEEP · FOOD · BEHAVIOUR` (the gate from 4.0, shown as a small passed-checkpoint, not re-litigated).
  - **Right margin — the STIMULANT RAIL:** a parallel bus carrying a prominent **master HEAVY-DAY switch** drawn mid-throw, labeled `STIMULANT RAIL · SWITCHED · 4–5 DAYS`. Show its two states honestly: the lit `OUTPUT DAY` position (cyan, energised) and a ghosted `REST DAY` position where the whole rail goes **dim grey** — the rest-day reset made literal. A small amber tag on the switch: `rest days reset tolerance · clear huperzine · stretch the bottles`.

- **The six subsystem blocks (center stage, wired in the article's six-act order, top→bottom):** each act is a labeled circuit block, and the wiring between them is the bio-circuit. Crucially, **each block is fed by whichever rail it belongs to** — that wiring is the two-tier split made visible:
  1. `ACT I — FUEL & POWER` (creatine · ALCAR on the structural side; **tyrosine taps the stimulant rail**) → the engine's resource allocation.
  2. `ACT II — WIRING / THE KENNEDY TRINITY` (uridine + CDP-choline + DHA, drawn as three lines converging into one `phosphatidylcholine → membrane` node; Bacopa as a slow-charging cell) → **all structural rail.** Label the trinity `ALL THREE REQUIRED`.
  3. `ACT III — LOGISTICS` (ginkgo as a widening pipe / vasodilator on the stimulant side) → "delivers everything else." Draw it modest; the article calls it a low-risk aid, not a hero.
  4. `ACT IV — SPARK & ALPHA-WAVE REGULATOR` (caffeine = the ignition coil; **L-theanine = a snubber / jitter-suppression filter clamped across it**). This is the signature small device — see below.
  5. `ACT V — ACETYLCHOLINE GUARD` (alpha-GPC = fast choline delivery on the stimulant rail; huperzine = a one-way enzyme valve that *holds* acetylcholine). This block carries **the one red object** — see below.
  6. `ACT VI — MOOD & NEUROGENESIS` (saffron = a reuptake clamp keeping monoamines in the synapse longer, structural rail; Lion's Mane = a slow `BDNF/NGF` growth node, "real extract only — not biomass").
  All six feed rightward/downward into a single labeled `OUTPUT BUS → THE PRODUCE WINDOW`.

- **THE SIGNATURE MYTH-BUSTER — the cost-inversion current meters (this is 4.1's load-bearing reveal, its equivalent of 3.1's equal-height nitrogen datum / 4.4's inverted float / 5.2's receipt-vs-meter):** put a small **current draw meter on each rail.** The **always-on STRUCTURAL rail reads HIGH draw** (`≈ RM 200/mo · the bulk`); the **switched STIMULANT rail reads LOW draw** (`≈ RM 80/mo · lasts most of a year`). Behind them, a **dim-grey ghost** of the reversed assumption (a fat stimulant meter, a thin structural one) labeled `WHAT YOU EXPECTED`, visibly inverted by the real readings. Caption: `THE SMART-DRUG HALF IS THE CHEAP HALF.` The whole point lands pre-verbally: the rail that never switches off, and that you'd never brag about, is the one drawing the current.

- **THE 2:1 SNUBBER (Act IV detail, small but load-bearing):** at the spark block, draw caffeine's raw output as a sharp spike, and L-theanine clamped across the line as a filter that **rounds the spike into a smooth wave**, stamped `2:1 THEANINE : CAFFEINE` and `the whole trick`. This is the article's cheapest, highest-impact fix; it earns a callout.

- **THE CHOLINE SPLIT (a small wiring detail across two acts):** choline enters at **two points** — a thin steady `CDP-choline` trickle on the structural rail (with a tiny feedback arc `→ uridine` showing the Kennedy synergy) and a switched `Alpha-GPC` burst on the stimulant rail feeding Act V. Micro-label: `low daily CDP · save Alpha-GPC for heavy days`.

- **THE GOLDEN-RULE BASE COURSE (a thin strip under the board):** a row of **a dozen small trimmer pots, every one set to the same modest ~⅓ detent**, beside a single lone fader **slammed to max throwing a clipped/jittery waveform.** Caption: `A DOZEN MODEST DIALS BEAT ONE SLAMMED FADER.` (Distinct from 4.2's single-channel amplitude version: here the point is *breadth across pathways*, many small inputs, not the height of any one channel.)

- **THE ONE RED OBJECT — the cholinergic-overload interlock (Act V):** the acetylcholine guard is where the only harm-on-overdrive lives. Draw a **red fault interlock** where the choline *fuel* (alpha-GPC) and the huperzine *brake* are both pushed hard **and a redundant `DMAE` module is plugged in on top** → the block trips red: `CHOLINERGIC OVERLOAD · jaw clench · fog · depressive dip`. A small note: `huperzine must be cycled — weekdays on, weekends off.` This is the on-thesis red for a "complete safe engine" poster: the one way to break an otherwise-clean board is to over-drive a single pathway from both ends at once. **Red appears nowhere else.** Every other caution is amber: the rest-day switch tag, the ginkgo "modest evidence" note, the nicotine "nitrous, never daily" tag, the pre-made-trap caffeine zone.

- **THE PRE-MADE TRAP (a small "rejected module" inset, off to one side):** a single `ONE-SCOOP PRE-BLEND` chip drawn as one block with its sliders **mechanically ganged** — dragging the `Bacopa / Tyrosine` slider up to a clinical dose **forces the linked `caffeine` slider into an amber over-dose zone** (`~300 mg · jitters + crash`). Caption: `LOCKED RATIO → over-caffeinated just to dose the herbs. the DIY board separates the rails.` It is the foil that justifies the whole two-rail architecture.

**Why this metaphor fits and nothing else does.** The article's literal language is "engine," "bio-circuit," "fuel lines," "wiring," "the spark," "the brake," "two tiers," "runs every day," "switched on." A complete dual-rail engine board is the only shape that is simultaneously (a) honest to the six-act wiring, (b) able to show the **two tiers as two physically separate power rails** (one un-switchable, one gated), (c) able to carry the **cost inversion as current draw** on those rails, and (d) a clean continuation of 4.0's base-rail vocabulary (it builds out the rail 4.0 drew empty). A bottle grid, a pill flat-lay, or a routine clock would all reduce the engine back to the *pile* the article is written to refute.

## 3. Layout Description

- **Top band (~10%):** the headline (see §4), kerned wide on the dark ground, thin rule beneath. Below it, a small `PASSED: SLEEP · FOOD · BEHAVIOUR` checkpoint chip (the 4.0 gate, acknowledged not re-argued — you only build this engine once the base is genuinely maxed).
- **Center stage (~76%) — the hero:** the engine board. - **Left/right rails:** structural rail (left) thick, steady, no switch. Stimulant rail (right) with the prominent HEAVY-DAY master switch near the top and the ghosted REST-DAY dim state shown.
- **Lower inset zone (within center):** the cost-inversion current meters (with the dim-grey "what you expected" ghost) on one side; the pre-made-trap rejected module on the other.
- **Bottom footer (~14%):** the Golden-Rule trimmer-pot strip, then the thesis lockup `BUILD THE ENGINE. KEEP THE STRUCTURAL RAIL LIT.`, then a thin line: `Part 4.1 of 4 · Cognitive Enhancement · the Productive pillar · the complete natural engine · most people never need Part 4.2`.

Reading path deliberately teaches the thesis in order: **two rails (one never off) → one wired machine → the always-on rail is the expensive one → don't max any single dial → the one way to break it is overloading a pathway from both ends.**

## 5. Color & Mood

**Mood:** the calm, lit-up seriousness of a clean schematic at night — a designed machine, disciplined, anti-hype. The opposite of biohacker neon or a glossy supplement ad. The feeling of being shown that the thing you thought was a shopping list is actually one engine with a power architecture, and that the unglamorous always-on rail is the one that matters.

**Palette (locked Productive / Cognitive Enhancement tokens, within `aether-call-to-action-DESIGN.md` — carried verbatim from 4.0/4.2/4.3):**
- **Background:** near-black ink (`#0B0F14`), barely-there gradient (a touch warmer at the bottom where the always-on structural rail lives).
- **Live signal — both energised rails, the lit subsystem wiring, the high structural-draw meter, the correctly-clamped spark:** electric **cyan / teal** (`#2DD4BF`). Note the deliberate move: the cost inversion is expressed *in cyan*, not in a separate money color (5.2 used its own ledger-green; the Cognitive palette stays locked) — the high-draw structural meter glows the same live cyan as the rail, so "the always-on healthy half is the expensive half" reads as one idea, not two.
- **Caution — the heavy-day switch tag, ginkgo's modest-evidence note, the pre-made-trap caffeine zone, the nicotine tag, the rest-day state badge, cyclical/huperzine reminders:** muted **amber** (`#E8A23D`).
- **One alarm color — used once:** restrained **signal red** (`#FF5C5C`), reserved *exclusively* for the Act V cholinergic-overload interlock. Scarcity is what gives it force.
- **Structure / text / ruler:** soft off-white (`#E6E8EE`) for primary labels, muted slate for secondary — never harsh pure white.
- **Dimming = inactive / off:** the **REST-DAY state of the stimulant rail**, the reversed "what you expected" cost ghost, and any un-energised trace are **dim grey**, not colored. The eye reads instantly: the left rail is always lit; the right rail can go dark; the structural draw is the bright one.

The composition does double duty: a permanently-glowing left rail next to a switch-darkened right rail encodes the two-tier thesis before a word is read, and the bright structural current meter quietly delivers the cost-inversion gut-punch.

## 7. What to Avoid

- **The "smart-drug" cliché kit — a glowing brain, a lightbulb head, gears in a skull, neurons as fireworks, or a flat-lay of supplement bottles / a pill grid / a pharmacy shelf.** It screams "nootropics," teaches nothing, and worst of all rebuilds the exact *pile of pills* the article is written to refute. The hero must read as one wired engine, not a product line-up.
- **Re-typesetting the article's dosage table or the cost table as an image.** Those become, respectively, the modest trimmer-pot detents (Golden Rule strip) and the two current-draw meters (cost inversion). A spreadsheet-as-hero kills the argument.
- **Drawing the stimulant rail as the big, bright, important half (or the structural rail as an afterthought).** That reproduces the precise expectation the article inverts. The always-on structural rail must be the one that is *thick, unbroken, and drawing the most current*; the stimulant rail must visibly *switch off* on rest days. If a viewer walks away thinking the stimulants are the expensive, central part, the poster has failed.
- **A "level-up" ladder, an ascending staircase, or any aspirational progression read** (the track-wide ban): this article is the *complete* base, not a step toward something better. And **flooding the board with red** — exactly one red interlock (cholinergic overload) is permitted; every other caution is amber, or the single-alarm discipline that the whole pillar depends on collapses.
