**3.0's new sub-archetype = a FAULT-ROUTED DIAGNOSTIC BOARD.** A horizontal signal flow that begins with a **symptom probe** (diagnose the failing lever), runs through **three parallel lever-lanes** (Drive / Memory / Flexibility), and ends in a **master verdict**. The defining difference from its closest siblings: 4.2 was about *how high you push one stack of dials and what it costs*; 4.3 was about *which cable to swap*; **3.0 is about matching a symptom to one of three signal paths, then taking the lowest tier that fixes that path.** No prior poster's hero event is *diagnosis-then-selection across a 3 lever × 3 tier territory*. Tier-coding glyphs (locked = Rx, hatched = experimental) are carried over deliberately as a **series convention**; the *composition* (lanes off a probe, not a fader rack) is what makes it a new instrument.

## 1. The Big Idea

**One sentence:** *Cognitive compounds are not a menu you climb from weakest to strongest — they are three separate repair lanes, and the whole skill is diagnosing which lever is actually failing and then reaching for the lowest tier that fixes that one lane, because the strongest compound on the wrong lever is worse than the right compound at its gentlest dose.*

The reader should feel the article's first instruction land physically: *stop scanning for the strongest thing.* The poster has to make three truths instantly obvious without a word of body copy. (a) There are **exactly three faults** a person actually has ("can't start," "can't retain," "driven but stuck"), and each routes to a different lane. (b) The honest home is the **lit middle tier** that runs down every lane (Premium OTC), with the prescription tier **locked** and the experimental tier **hatched and dim** — so "live in Tier 2, reach into Tier 1 only for high stakes, enter Tier 3 eyes open" is something you *see*, not read. (c) More is not better: the one lane that lets you push two controls at once (Memory's fuel and brake) has a **burn-out point** when you do. The register is a calm, lit clinical bench that respects the reader enough to show the locks and the one scorch mark honestly, the visual opposite of a hype nootropics carousel.

## 2. Visual Metaphor or Structure

**The anchor: a fault-routed diagnostic board — "PROBE → THREE LEVER-LANES → VERDICT."** Read left to right as a signal that enters as a *complaint* and leaves as a *correct, measured action.*

**A. The Symptom Probe (far left, the hero input).** A single three-way **fault selector** (drawn as a probe plugged into one of three labelled jacks, or a single-pole rotary with three hard detents — *not* a continuous knob, to stay clear of 1.1). The three positions are the article's exact decision flow, step 1:

- `CAN'T START / WON'T GRIND` → routes to the **DRIVE** lane (dopamine · norepinephrine)
- `CAN'T RETAIN / WON'T STICK` → routes to the **MEMORY** lane (acetylcholine)
- `DRIVEN BUT STUCK / OVERWHELMED` → routes to the **FLEXIBILITY** lane (GABA · serotonin)

Only **one** route lights at a time. This is the load-bearing idea of the whole article ("which lever is failing?") made into the first thing the eye does.

**B. The free Behavioural-Fix Gate (immediately after the probe, spanning all three lanes).** Before any lane reaches a compound, the signal passes a single **GATE** stamped `MOST 'DRIVE' FAULTS ARE BASELINE FAULTS — PART 2.0 SOLVES THEM FOR FREE`. Traces that the gate resolves **dead-end here, dimmed** (most of them). This is step 2 of the decision flow and a direct callback to 2.0's drained reservoir; it visually argues that the board is the *exception*, not the default.

**C. The three lever-lanes (the body).** Three horizontal lanes stacked top to bottom. The crucial anti-cliché move: each lane is **not a ladder you climb**. Each lane is a **bright, lit TIER-2 TRUNK** (the home base — Premium OTC, steady cyan) with two **taps branching off it**:

- a **LOCKED Tier-1 tap** above the trunk (padlock / keyhole glyph = prescription, controlled in Malaysia) — `HIGH-STAKES ONLY`
- a **HATCHED, translucent Tier-3 tap** below the trunk (dim, unproven) — `YOU ARE THE STUDY`

So tiers read as *branches off the trunk you live on*, never as rungs that get better as you climb. The compounds dock onto their tap/trunk as small spec chips (name · the one mechanism word · the one cost). Contents per lane, faithful to the article:

- **DRIVE** — Trunk (T2): `L-Tyrosine/NALT` (restock, not kick) · `Caffeine + L-Theanine 2:1` (the cheapest reliable combo) · `Rhodiola` (swing weapon). Locked T1 tap: `Modafinil/Armodafinil` (the *lower-tax* clinical choice) · `Methylphenidate` · `Amphetamines`. Hatched T3 tap: `Bromantane` · `Phenylpiracetam`.
- **MEMORY** — see device (E) below; this lane forks.
- **FLEXIBILITY** — Trunk (T2): `L-Theanine` · `Ashwagandha KSM-66` · `Saffron` · `5-HTP` (intermittent, care). Locked T1 tap: `Buspirone` only, with a **bolted-shut sub-gate** `BENZODIAZEPINES EXCLUDED ON PURPOSE`. Hatched T3 tap: `Selank` and `Phenibut` (the latter under an amber lockout, see §below).

**D. The Modafinil and amphetamine honesty marks on the DRIVE lane (amber).**

- A **reality-check tag** on Modafinil's locked tap: `RESCUES A DEPLETED BRAIN · MODEST ON A RESTED ONE → FIX SLEEP FIRST`, with a thin tie-line back to 2.0/the Sleep track.
- A **cross-lane BLEED meter** off the amphetamine chip: two leak arrows spilling out of the DRIVE lane into two small **external buses** drawn at the edge of the board, labelled `PEACEFUL ↓` (anxiety, jagged comedown) and `HEALTHY ↓` (heart rate, blood pressure). Caption: `GREAT FOR DRIVE · TAXES YOUR OTHER PILLARS · BAD TO STACK ON GEAR`. This is the "Adderall tax" rendered as current escaping the lane. Amber, not red.

**E. The MEMORY lane fork — fuel vs brake, and the one red.** This lane is the most mechanically distinct and gets the focal fault. Instead of one trunk it splits into **two parallel sub-trunks that re-combine at a summing junction:**

- **FUEL feed** (raw material for acetylcholine): `CDP-Choline (Cognizin)` drawn as the **preferred, clean** chip · `Alpha-GPC*` beside it carrying an **amber caveat tag** `*~46% higher 10-yr stroke signal (TMAO) · don't run high-dose daily` · plus `ALCAR` and `Bacopa` (slow, judge at 4–6 wks).
- **BRAKE-RELEASE** (stop acetylcholine breakdown): `Huperzine-A` on the trunk with `CYCLE IT — long half-life, it accumulates`; the **locked T1 tap** holds `Donepezil · Galantamine` stamped `SLEDGEHAMMER FOR A FINISHING NAIL`.
- **THE SUMMING JUNCTION = THE ONE RED OBJECT.** Where fuel and brake meet, a meter shows that driving **both** hard does not add memory — it **trips into overload**. This is the single alarm on the poster: a red fault lamp at the junction stamped `CHOLINERGIC OVERLOAD — MORE CHOLINE ≠ MORE MEMORY` with the symptom ribbon `jaw-clench · stiffness · mood dip · heavy fog`. It is this article's exact mechanism-specific inverted-U (the sibling of 1.1's clip and 4.0's dead-rail), and it is the only red anywhere.

**F. The Nicotine bridge (between the DRIVE and MEMORY lanes).** Nicotine is the multi-receptor wildcard, so it is a **jumper cable physically bridging the DRIVE and MEMORY lanes** (it releases dopamine/norepinephrine *and* acetylcholine at once). It carries a **dependence-ceiling limiter** (amber): `NRT only · 1–2 mg · tactical, never daily · or you rebuild the cheap-dopamine baseline 2.0 drained`, with a small habit-stamp glyph tying to 2.0. Its neuroprotection claim gets a faint `plausible, not proven` ghost tag, not a benefit chip.

**G. The Master Verdict (far right, the output).** The signal leaves as a **comparator** that states the article's thesis as geometry: a small **clean cyan output** labelled `RIGHT LANE · TIER 2` set beside a big **clipped/jagged output** labelled `WRONG LANE · TIER 1 MAXED`, with the clean one clearly winning. Law plate beneath: `THE STRONGEST COMPOUND FOR THE WRONG LEVER IS WORSE THAN THE RIGHT COMPOUND AT THE LOWEST TIER`. A thin **feedback loop** runs from the output back to the probe, stamped `CHANGE ONE VARIABLE · THEN MEASURE` (the single-variable rule from 1.0).

**Why this shape and nothing else.** The article's spine is a *decision flow* ("which lever is failing → exhaust the free fix → lowest tier that addresses it → change one thing and measure") laid over a *3 lever × 3 tier* space. A fault-routed board is the only instrument that makes the **diagnosis** the first act, makes **Tier 2 the literal trunk you live on**, isolates the **fuel-vs-brake duality** as a fork unique to one lane, and shows the **amphetamine tax as current leaking into other pillars** — none of which a menu, a ladder, or a re-typeset table can do. A gain console (4.2) would re-raise "how high can I push," the wrong question here; a ladder would re-encode "climb to stronger," the exact misconception the article opens by attacking.

## 3. Layout Description

**Orientation: landscape.** The argument is a left-to-right *flow* (complaint in → correct action out), and three stacked lanes need horizontal run. This also keeps it a sibling-but-distinct from the portrait gain console (4.2): same family, different instrument.

- **Far-left column — THE PROBE + GATE (entry).** The three-way symptom selector with its three labelled jacks, one route lit. Immediately right, the full-height **Behavioural-Fix Gate** spanning all three lanes, with most traces dead-ending dim against it.
- **Centre — THE THREE LANES (hero band, ~60% width).** DRIVE (top), MEMORY (middle, the visually busiest because it forks), FLEXIBILITY (bottom). Each lane: a bright cyan **Tier-2 trunk** running the lane's length, a **locked Tier-1 tap** rising above it, a **hatched Tier-3 tap** dropping below it. The **Nicotine jumper** bridges the gap between the DRIVE and MEMORY lanes. The **amphetamine bleed** arrows exit the DRIVE lane upward into the two small external pillar-buses pinned to the top edge. The **cholinergic-overload red lamp** sits at the MEMORY lane's summing junction — the lone warm point, roughly centre of mass, where the eye is meant to snag.
- **Far-right column — THE VERDICT (output).** The clean-vs-clipped comparator and the law plate, with the `change one variable → measure` loop arcing back across the top to the probe.
- **Foot ribbon (subordinate) — "THE STACK AT A GLANCE."** The article's 3×3 summary rendered NOT as a spreadsheet but as a tiny **3-lane × 3-tier status dot-map**: nine cells, the **Tier-2 row lit cyan**, Tier-1 row padlocked-amber, Tier-3 row hatched-grey — a thumbnail of the board above. One closing stamp: `DIAGNOSE THE LEVER · START LOW · CHANGE ONE THING`.

**Eye path (it argues the thesis by movement):** the hand goes first to the **probe** (you must diagnose, not shop) → through the **free gate** where most faults dissolve (you may not even need the board) → into the **one lit lane** the probe selected, resting on the **bright Tier-2 trunk** (this is home) → the eye notices the **locked** tap above and the **hatched** tap below (stronger is *fenced*, not better) → snags on the **single red overload lamp** on the Memory fork (more is not more) → exits to the **verdict** (right lane, lowest tier wins) → the loop pulls it back to *measure*. The reader leaves understanding the destination is the lit middle trunk, reached by diagnosis, not the locked top.

## 5. Color & Mood

**Mood:** a calm, lit clinical bench at night with exactly one fault lamp burning. Disciplined, anti-hype, quietly authoritative; an instrument that shows you the locks and the single scorch mark honestly. Not biohacker neon, not motivational.

**Palette — inherit the locked Productive tokens verbatim:**

- **Background:** near-black ink `#0B0F14`, generous negative space; a faint warmer glow only along the three lit **Tier-2 trunks** (home).
- **Live signal / Tier 2 / clean output:** electric teal-cyan `#2DD4BF`. The trunks, the lit probe route, the clean verdict output, the at-a-glance Tier-2 row.
- **Caution / structure / tiers:** amber `#E8A23D`. Everything cautionary-but-not-the-central-fault: the locked Tier-1 padlocks, the hatched Tier-3 framing, the Modafinil reality-check, the amphetamine bleed and the two pillar-buses, the alpha-GPC stroke caveat, the nicotine ceiling, the phenibut/benzo lockouts, the Donepezil "sledgehammer" tag. Amber does the heavy lifting.
- **The one alarm colour — used exactly once:** signal red `#FF5C5C`, reserved **solely** for the cholinergic-overload lamp at the Memory summing junction. Nowhere else.
- **Text:** soft off-white `#E6E8EE` primary, muted slate secondary. Never pure white.
- **Dim grey:** inactive / unproven — the Tier-3 hatched taps and the traces that dead-end at the free gate. The eye should read *a few bright trunks under a mostly fenced, dim surround*, which is the thesis.

The discipline is the message: one red, scarce, so the genuine inverted-U fault (overload) keeps its force; every other hazard is amber; **calm/Flexibility carries no alarm colour at all** (per 1.1, calm is the resolution, not the danger).

## 7. What to Avoid

1. **The drug-menu grid / pharmacy-shelf flat-lay / capsule photography.** The article's first line is *"don't read this as a shopping list."* A neat table of bottles is the exact cliché it is written against. The board must editorialise *diagnosis and tier*, not display product. Also ban the series cliché kit: glowing brain, lightbulb-head, gears-in-skull, floating molecules.
2. **Re-typesetting the 3×3 risk-and-tier table as the hero.** That table already exists in the article. The poster's job is the *relationships* the table can't show: fault → lane routing, the Tier-2 trunk you live on, the fuel-vs-brake fork and its overload, the amphetamine current leaking into other pillars, the nicotine bridge. The foot dot-map may *echo* the table at thumbnail scale, but it is subordinate, never the centre.
3. **A "level-up" ladder or any climb-to-stronger composition.** Drawing the tiers as ascending rungs (Tier 1 at the bottom climbing to Tier 3, or vice versa, with "better" up top) re-encodes the precise misconception the article destroys. Tiers must read as **taps branching off the lit Tier-2 trunk** — the prescription tap *locked*, the experimental tap *hatched and dim* — so "stronger is fenced, not better" is unmistakable.
4. **Red-flooding, or drawing Flexibility/calm as the alarm.** Only the cholinergic overload is red; the amphetamine tax, phenibut/benzo lockouts, alpha-GPC signal, Donepezil sledgehammer and nicotine ceiling are all amber. If every hazard is red the one real inverted-U fault loses its force. And the Flexibility lane is the *resolution* to "driven but stuck," so it must read clean and un-alarmed; never let calm look like danger.
