## 0. Continuity — the invisible-signal pivot, and why it teaches

Every prior sibling in this pillar drew something you can **see**: a building (1.0), a face (2.0), a head (3.0), a wardrobe (4.0), a carry laid on a table (5.0). Part 6.0 is the hinge where the pillar leaves the visual field entirely. ==This article's load-bearing fact is neuroanatomical: scent is the only signal that bypasses the visual cortex and routes through the amygdala and hippocampus *before* conscious processing.== So the poster's whole conceit — the thing a returning reader should feel in one glance — is **the pillar rendering, for the first time, two signals you can't see:** a *scent field* and a *social room*.

That gives the epistemic key a fresh, specific weighting for this poster:

- **Warm = MEASURABLE / GROUNDED, and it owns the *real levers*:** the scent **field tuned to the tropical climate** (apply 50%), the **two pulse points**, the **third *space* itself** (the doorway), and the **refresh kit**. These are mechanical, checkable, do-them-tonight real.
- **Cool = CONVENTION, and it shrinks to the *choices*:** which fragrance **family**, which occupant **prop**, which signature **pieces**. The article is explicit that the *which* barely matters once the *how* is right — "pick one, any one; defaults, not laws."
- **One scarce red = the cigarette.** The single thing in the whole article with a real, quantified cost. ==The leverage is the doorway (warm), never the cigarette (red).==

The single most distinctive move over a generic "perfume + networking" graphic: the poster argues, by composition, that **the two halves are one idea** — a non-visual signal layer that lets you walk into and out of rooms without resetting — and it proves it with **one shared object, the pocket atomiser / refresh kit, that physically hinges the two halves together** (it is the airlock). That hinge is what makes a two-topic article into one poster instead of two.

## 1. The Big Idea

_One sentence: **The two things most men either ignore or fumble — the scent they project and the informal "third space" where real relationships get built — are the same kind of thing: an invisible signal layer you tune and carry, so the poster should make the reader feel, instantly, that scent is a FIELD you shape (and the tropics shrink your dose, not your reach) and the doorway is a ROOM you occupy (and the leverage is the space, not the cigarette) — joined by the one refresh kit that lets you cross between them clean.** The feeling it should burn in: you don't need to spend more or smoke at all; you need to apply less, pick two, and walk out with the group._

## 2. Visual Metaphor & Structure — the two heroes and the hinge

**A vertical DIPTYCH on one drafting sheet: a radiated SCENT FIELD on top, a third-space FLOOR PLAN below, bolted together by a REFRESH-KIT AIRLOCK band across the middle.** Two drawing types the pillar has never used — a *radiated field* (isobars, like a pressure or sound-pressure map) and a *plan view* (top-down architecture) — chosen precisely because the subject is two signals that have **no visible object** to draw. You can't render a smell or a "third space" as a thing on a table (that was 5.0); you render them as a **field** and a **room**.

The sheet reads top → down in the article's own order (scent first, then the doorway, then the honest cost):

### TOP HERO — THE CLIMATE-DERATED SILLAGE FIELD (the invisible signal, tuned)

- A neutral **human bust/torso tech-flat** (the established graphite line-flat, never a glamour render), seen front-on, with exactly **TWO PULSE POINTS marked** — inner wrist + behind-ear/chest. A small stamp: `TWO SPRAYS · TWO PULSE POINTS · ONE REFRESH`.
- Radiating from the body, the hero object: a **SILLAGE FIELD drawn as concentric isobars** — an inner *top-note* ring, a middle *heart* ring, an outer *base/sillage* ring. This is scent rendered as the one honest thing it is: a projection field, not a bottle. The correct field is a calm, contained warm bloom (intimate-to-moderate radius), *not* a room-filling cloud.
- **THE TROPICAL DERATING — the killer, article-specific mechanic (the role 5.0's floor-seeking master readout played).** The same field is shown a second time, ghosted, under MY climate forces drawn as two opposing arrows:
  - **HEAT → burns off the top-note inner ring fast** (`citrus/aquatic tops: 30–60 min, not 2–3 hr` — the inner ring visibly evaporating).
  - **HUMIDITY → amplifies the base outer ring** (`"moderate sillage" in Paris = "loud sillage" in KL` — the outer ring ballooning past the body).
  - The net instruction rendered as a **DOSE DIAL turned DOWN to 50%** while the field still reaches the *correct* contained radius: `APPLY 50% OF THE EUROPEAN DOSE — the humidity does the projecting for you.` ==This is the article's single most counterintuitive line made geometric: less spray, same reach.==
  - A struck/ghosted **over-applied field** (a giant cloud swallowing the room) labelled in **amber**: `spray >4× = you ARE the scent` (caution, not the scarce red).
- **THE FAMILY SELECTOR — cool, "pick one, any one."** The five fragrance families as a compact wheel/strip — Fresh·Citrus·Aquatic / Aromatic·Fougère / Spicy·Oriental / Woody·Smoky·Oud / Leather·Animalic — but **collapsed into a two-detent DAY/EVENING kit**: `DAY = fresh/citrus (cool, "competent & clean")` + `EVENING = woody/spicy (warm, "weight in the room")`. A struck shelf of a dozen bottles, ghosted: `past 3 = collection, not signature.` Leather/animalic carries a small amber `polarising — read carefully`.
- **THE SKIN-CHEMISTRY GATE — warm, the borrowed-foundation device for this poster.** A small pass-gate: `the bottle does not predict the field — your skin renders it.` Rendered as a **5–10 ml DECANT worn for 5–7 days** before any RM 400–1,500 bottle; a struck paper blotter labelled `blotter = calibration only, never the verdict`. (Same "you can't buy the result, only earn/test it" device as 1.1's finite collagen and 5.0's foundation gate, here = skin chemistry.)

### THE HINGE — THE REFRESH-KIT AIRLOCK (the one shared object that fuses the two halves)

Across the middle of the sheet, a slim horizontal **AIRLOCK band**: a small chamber the figure passes through. Inside it sit the three kit items, drawn as neat flats: **pocket atomiser (5–10 ml decant, RM 30–80)** · **mints / Tic-Tacs** · **hand sanitiser**. Caption: `THE KIT THAT LETS YOU CROSS WITHOUT RESETTING.` A note ties it back up to the scent field and down to the plan: `walk out → talk → refresh here → walk back in clean.` The atomiser is the literal callback that proves "this is one article": it earns its keep in *both* halves. A thin leader links it to `[[Part 2.0]] breath-economics`.

### BOTTOM HERO — THE THIRD-SPACE FLOOR PLAN (the invisible room, occupied)

- A **top-down architectural PLAN VIEW** (orthogonal to 1.0's side-on elevation — different drawing type on purpose). Two zones:
  - **INSIDE — the FORMAL MEETING ROOM**, drawn cool/neutral, labelled `where decisions get RATIFIED.`
  - **OUTSIDE — the DOORWAY / THIRD SPACE**, drawn in warm terracotta as the high-leverage zone, labelled `where decisions get SOCIALISED.` Examples ringed around it: `office smoking area · conference lobby/bar · wedding courtyard · late-night mamak table.`
- A **figure-path** loops from inside, out through the airlock, into the doorway, and back — the **DOORWAY PROTOCOL** as five numbered waypoints along the path: `① walk out with the group ("come for some air") → ② occupant prop (water · coffee · phone face-down · pouch) → ③ stay 5–10 min ("leave when they leave") → ④ match the register (weather/sport/traffic; substance arrives sideways) → ⑤ REFRESH (back through the airlock) → re-enter clean.` A small amber tell on overstaying: `lingering after the group disperses = reads as dependence.`
- **THE ONE RED — the cigarette.** In the doorway, the **leverage is the SPACE itself** (the warm zone). A **single cigarette** sits in the scene as the lone **signal-red** object, with a red CV-cost micro-trace off it: `endothelial damage · plaque · raised hematocrit — even 1–3/day is non-zero.` The hero callout, the largest non-headline line: `THE LEVERAGE IS THE DOORWAY, NOT THE CIGARETTE.` Sub: `you can occupy the exact same space with none of the cost.` A clean **bypass arrow** routes the figure around the cigarette into the conversation. ==Red appears nowhere else on the entire sheet.==
- A **harm-reduction note in AMBER, not red** (the scarcity must hold): a nicotine **pouch** (ZYN/Velo) flat stamped `no combustion smoke = less unsafe — NOT safe.` The cross-reference to the PE series sits as a thin grounded leader: `→ [[Part 4.0 - Protection]] (PE): the same CV mechanism this site refuses for anabolics.`

**Why this metaphor fits and nothing else does:** the article's four literal moves are (1) **shape a scent's projection** (the isobar field), (2) **tune that projection to the tropics by applying *less*** (the heat/humidity derating + 50% dial), (3) **occupy an informal *space*** (the floor plan + protocol path), and (4) **access it without the cigarette** (the warm doorway + the one red + the bypass). A **radiated-field-over-floor-plan diptych hinged by the refresh kit** is the only construction that holds all four at once *and* renders the core feeling — two invisible signals, one carried kit. A perfume-bottle hero, a fragrance-wheel infographic, or a smoking-area photo would each destroy the one idea that matters: *the signal is the field and the space, never the product or the cigarette.*

**How it stays clear of its siblings:** 1.0 was a side-on **elevation**; this is a top-down **plan** + a **field** (orthogonal drawing types). 2.0's hydraulic section, 3.0's head survey, 4.0's loadout catalog, and 5.0's inspection-sheet-with-tolerance-band are all *object* drawings; 6.0 is the pillar's first **non-object** drawing (a field and a room). Same bone paper, graphite flats, warm=measurable / cool=convention, one scarce red — new geometry, new subject, new verb (*project & occupy*, not *choose* or *audit*).

## 5. Color & Mood

**Mood:** the calm, exacting confidence of a **perfumer's bench drafted by an engineer** crossed with an **architect's plan of a building's social flow** — never a fragrance advert, never a smoking PSA. Grown-up, un-anxious, slightly clinical: the feeling of being handed the *physics* of two things usually sold as mystique (scent) or vice (the smoking circle). The **warm-measurable / cool-convention split does the teaching**, and here warm owns the genuinely useful levers (the tuned field, the space, the kit) while cool shrinks to the barely-matters choices (which family, which prop).

**Palette (light-first — the established Attractive drafting-paper register):**
- **Background:** warm bone / drafting paper (`#F4F1EA`-ish), faint blueprint grid or drafting-dot texture, very low contrast.
- **Ink / linework / type:** graphite near-black (`#1E2024`) for all structure — the torso-flat, the isobar field lines, the floor-plan walls, the protocol path, dimension lines, labels.
- **Accent 1 — MEASURABLE / GROUNDED (the dominant accent):** structural **terracotta / ochre** (`#C2703D`-ish) for the **correct sillage field**, the **two pulse points**, the **50% dose dial**, the **third-space / doorway zone**, the **refresh-kit airlock**, and the **skin-chemistry gate**. Warm = the real, do-them-tonight levers.
- **Accent 2 — CONVENTION (shrunk):** muted **slate-blue / pewter** (`#5E7585`-ish) for the **which-one choices**: the fragrance-family selector labels, the occupant-prop options, the formal meeting room. Cooler, lighter, "defaults, not laws."
- **One alarm colour — the cigarette only:** restrained **signal-red / vermilion** (`#D9483B`), used **exclusively** for the cigarette and its CV micro-trace. Scarcity is the force; it is the exact moral spine of the article (`the leverage is the space, not the smoke`).
- **Dim grey** for the ghosted heat/humidity reshape of the field, the struck over-application cloud, the struck bottle-collection shelf, the blotter, and the pouch caveat — out-of-spec or secondary, but **amber-or-grey, never red**.

The warm-over-cool balance ties Part 6.0 to its thesis — the leverage is *attention applied* (field tuned, space occupied, kit carried), not *money spent or a habit taken on* — and the single red cigarette is the one true cost on an otherwise calm sheet.
