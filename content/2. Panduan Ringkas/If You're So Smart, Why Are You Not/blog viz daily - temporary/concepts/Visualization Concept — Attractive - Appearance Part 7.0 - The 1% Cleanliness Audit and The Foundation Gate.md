## 1. The Big Idea

**One sentence:** A six-part appearance machine isn't *running* just because you built it — it only counts if you do two unglamorous things forever: **keep the 1% maintained (the multiplier that stops the 99% from rotting within months) and pass the financial gate before you fuel it toward dating (the prerequisite) — because a complete, well-kept, mid-tier system fed only from surplus out-reads a flashy, neglected one bought on a stretched balance sheet.**

The feeling on first glance: *"Building it was the easy part. This is the standard of running it."* The poster is the most **system-level, least decorative** plate in the whole set — exactly what a capstone should be.

The two load-bearing lines, both from the article's own framing:
- *"Maintenance is the multiplier; the gate is the prerequisite."* (the `!important` thesis)
- *"Build the machine first; invite guests later."* (the tying phrase)

## 2. Visual Metaphor or Structure

**The anchor: a commissioning & run schematic of the assembled "Appearance machine."** Three operators act on one small assembled core:

1. **THE MACHINE (Parts 1–6)** — drawn small and *sealed*, as a callback emblem, **not re-detailed** (the way 2.0 held 1.0's emblem). A two-tier core matching the article's own 3-layer compression: a **SUBSTRATE slab** (Parts 2–3) under a **SIGNAL tier** (Parts 4–6). It is the thing every other operator acts upon — never the hero by itself.

2. **THE GATE — the prerequisite (upstream, left).** A literal **intake valve / sluice** the *fuel* must pass before the machine is allowed to run hot. It is opened or held shut by a **comparator** reading the **Surplus Equation** against the machine's **running cost**. This is the financial half rendered as flow control, not as a coins-and-piggy-bank illustration.

3. **THE FLOOR — the multiplier (wrapping the machine).** A **closed maintenance LOOP / recirculation orbit** that returns the machine to spec every week. A small **×** glyph on the loop makes "maintenance is the multiplier" literal. The same machine with the loop **severed** appears as a **ghosted decay-shadow** ("built-and-decaying within 6 months").

**Why this metaphor fits.** The article is explicitly *two things tied together*, and both are **operators on a system that already exists**, not new components. A gate (a prerequisite = a logical pre-condition) and a multiplier (maintenance = a recurring gain on the existing build) are precisely an intake valve and a recirculation loop. Rendering the six prior articles as one sealed unit also does the capstone's real job: it shows that Part 7 operates on the **whole series at once**, which no other article does.

## 3. Layout Description

**Orientation: LANDSCAPE** (~3:2 / 16:10). Rationale: the gate→machine→output story reads left-to-right, and the maintenance loop needs room to wrap the core. (6.0 was portrait; 7.0 flips back — and a flow needs width.) *Portrait fallback: gate as a top intake header, machine + loop centred, decay-shadow + thesis stacked below.*

Composition, left to right:

- **Far-left rail — THE EPISTEMIC KEY (locked device).** Two swatches: **warm = MEASURABLE & YOURS** (the maintenance cadence, *your* surplus number, coherence) over **cool = CONVENTION / BENCHMARK** (the ≤30% / 3–6mo / 10–15% rules-of-thumb, "what peers appear to do"). Plus the one-red legend. Mirrors every sibling's left rail.

- **Left third — THE GATE (prerequisite).** Top: the **SURPLUS EQUATION as a descending subtraction manifold** — an inflow pipe "MONTHLY INCOME" with mandatory tap-offs draining downward in order: −health protocols · −rent (≤30%) · −utilities/transport/insurance · −emergency fund (3–6 mo) · −retirement (≥10–15%). What survives collects in a measured **SURPLUS reservoir** at the bottom. A **comparator** weighs Surplus against the **MACHINE RUNNING COST (RM 500–1,500/mo)**, and sets the **GATE VALVE**: **OPEN** (terracotta, surplus ≥ cost → full fuel + the *dating-as-a-project* outlet unlocked) or **HELD SHUT** (calm grey → only a thin **SUBSTRATE BYPASS trickle**, Parts 2–3 "costs almost nothing," is let through; the heavy SIGNAL feed and the DATING outlet stay locked). A small numbered **commissioning-order ladder** runs beside the gate: 1 income → 2 debt control → 3 emergency fund → 4 retirement → 5 substrate → 6 signal → 7 dating.

- **Centre — THE MACHINE + THE FLOOR.** The sealed two-tier core sits on a **baseplate labelled THE FLOOR**, wrapped by the **maintenance LOOP** carrying the Sunday-reset's six service stations as nodes — ① shoes ② wardrobe scan ③ vehicle/bag ④ wallet ⑤ nails / grooming borders ⑥ screens & crystals — stamped **"30 MIN · SAME DAY EACH WEEK."** A **LEVELLING BAR** runs across the machine's components = the **"upgrade evenly"** rule (all held at one moderate tier, one maintenance level). The detailed **1% checklist** hangs off the loop as a thin rail in four zones (FACE · HAND · CLOTHING · METAL & LEATHER).

- **Lower band — THE GHOSTED DECAY-SHADOW.** The *same* machine with the loop severed, faded to **grey within "6 MONTHS"**: scuffed shoe, frayed strap, grey-white sneaker, stained lapel, overgrown neckline. Caption **"BUILT-AND-DECAYING."** Grey, never red — neglect is dim, not alarming.

- **Right edge — THE OUTPUT.** A clean output terminal: **"RUNS FROM STRENGTH, NOT COMPENSATION"** — lit only when the gate is open *and* the loop is intact (fundamental-before-multiplier, the series' locked logic).

- **Bottom — headline + thesis lockup**, spanning full width.

**Eye path:** key → headline → gate + equation (left) → fuel crosses into the machine (centre) → loop wrapping it → output (right) → drop to the grey decay-shadow → snag on the ONE red gate-force → land on the thesis.

## 5. Color and Mood

Locked palette, verbatim. Mapping is the teaching:

- **Warm terracotta `#C2703D` = measurable & yours:** the live maintenance loop + × multiplier, the SURPLUS reservoir, the machine's RUNNING/lit state, the output. (The article's "run *your* numbers, not theirs.")
- **Cool slate `#5E7585` = convention / benchmark:** the ≤30% / 3–6mo / 10–15% **rules-of-thumb** (the article's own disclaimer flags these as convention, "will not apply identically to every individual" — so they must read as defaults, **not law**), the peer-facade gauge.
- **Dim grey ghosting:** the decay-shadow, the **eroded financial footing** under the failure, the corrected "more signal = more solvent" instinct.
- **ONE red `#D9483B`:** a single alarm only (§5 below).
- **Bone `#F4F1EA`** paper, **graphite `#1E2024`** linework.

**Mood:** an engineer's **operations-&-maintenance / commissioning page** — calm, authoritative, clinical, the most *finished and system-level* drawing in the set. A capstone should feel like the manual's last page: "here is the standard for running everything you built."

**The ONE red object — the only alarm:** a hand **cranking the GATE open against an insufficient-surplus reading**, blowing out the red **DATING-AS-A-PROJECT** outlet downstream while the **financial footing erodes grey** beneath it — **"RUNNING THE MACHINE ON A STRETCHED BALANCE SHEET."** It is located **at the gate** (the prerequisite half) because it is the exact inversion of *"build the machine first; invite guests later,"* and it subsumes the debt hard-stop (debt = negative surplus). **Everything else stays calm:** the *closed* gate is neutral grey (a held gate is correct, not a failure); the decay-shadow is grey (neglect, not alarm); the uneven-upgrade tell and the peer-facade warning are **amber**. Exactly one red, and the maintenance half deliberately has **no** red — its failure is the grey decay-shadow, keeping palette discipline.

## 7. What to Avoid

1. **The lifestyle / grooming-blog still life.** No "tidy man," no before/after, and **no literal Sunday-cleaning flat-lay** (shoe-polish-cloth-and-steamer product shot). Maintenance must read as a **recurring run-loop operator on the assembled machine**, not a cleaning-supplies photo.
2. **Redrawing 5.0's QC audit-sheet** (pins + tolerance band + master needle) — the single biggest trap, because the "1% Audit" is *literally* an audit. 5.0 owns the static inspection-sheet; 7.0 must be the **dynamic run/commission diagram** (gate + recirculation loop + decay-shadow, verb = RUN). Likewise do **not** redraw 1.0's building, 2.0's hydraulic body-section, 3.0's head survey, 4.0's catalog, or 6.0's diptych.
3. **The finance cliché.** No piggy-bank, no stacked coins, no ascending-bar "growth" chart, no padlock-on-wallet. The gate is an **intake valve governed by a surplus comparator**; the equation is a **subtraction manifold/ledger**; the %-targets must read as **cool convention**, never hard law.
4. **Red-flooding or mislocated red.** Exactly **one** red (the stretched-balance-sheet gate-force). Never draw the *closed* gate or the *decay-shadow* in red — a held gate and visible neglect are calm grey; only forcing the machine open on an empty/borrowed footing is the alarm.
