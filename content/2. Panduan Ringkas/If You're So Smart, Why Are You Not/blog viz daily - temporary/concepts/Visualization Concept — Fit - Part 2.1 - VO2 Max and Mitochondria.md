## Series context & the key point _(why this concept, not a generic one)_

Part 2.1 is the **mechanistic "why" chapter behind Part 2.0**. Part 2.0 gave the protocol (Zone 2 most days, hard intervals once or twice). Part 2.1 proves those two prescriptions, in those exact proportions, are not arbitrary folk tradition — they are a **direct readout of cellular biology**. The article's whole intellectual move is a *split*: VO2 max is not one thing, it is a delivery chain with **two independently-trainable levers**, and each of the two protocols preferentially trains one of them.

The spine the article hangs everything on is the **Fick equation**:

`VO2max = (HRmax × SVmax) × (a-vO2 diff)max`
`         └─── cardiac output (Q) ───┘   └── extraction ──┘`

Read it as three terms and you can see the entire article inside it:

1. **HRmax** — *fixed*. Genetics and age. Barely moves. (The non-trainable term — important to show as locked.)
2. **Stroke volume (SV)** — the **CENTRAL** lever. Trainable. A bigger pump. Built by **intervals** (the 4×4): sustained near-maximal cardiac output remodels the left ventricle (eccentric chamber expansion) + plasma volume, so each beat ejects more blood. This is "the ceiling."
3. **a-vO2 difference** — the **PERIPHERAL** lever. Trainable. Better extraction. Built by **Zone 2**: hours of low-to-moderate calcium/AMPK signalling drive **mitochondrial biogenesis** via the **PGC-1α → NRF-1/2 → TFAM** cascade (more and better factories) plus capillary density. This is "the floor."

So the geometry the reader must *feel*: **two organs, two machineries, two protocols.** Intervals → heart → stroke volume → ceiling. Zone 2 → muscle cell → mitochondria/capillaries → floor. The "80/20 polarized" split isn't a training fad; it's what the cell biology prescribes when you aim each stimulus at the organ it actually remodels.

Four supporting ideas ride alongside the central split and each deserves a small instrument readout, not a paragraph:

- **Lactate is fuel, not waste** (the lactate shuttle: glycolytic fibres export lactate via MCTs → oxidative tissue burns it; LT2 rises because *clearance* improves, not because you produce less). A myth-correction worth one callout.
- **Genetics writes the slope** (HERITAGE: ~47% heritability of trainability, ~50–60% of baseline; huge spread of response; ~21 SNPs). The honest-but-empowering caveat: track *your own* line.
- **Aging hits central harder than peripheral** (~10%/decade untrained; the heart's max stroke volume fades faster than mitochondrial capacity → intervals become *more* important with age, not less).
- **Why VO2 max predicts mortality** (Mandsager 2018, 122,007 patients, no upper limit of benefit): it's a *composite* — heart + lungs + blood + mitochondria + lean muscle. You can't fake any of them, so the number indexes whole-system integrity.

Prior Fit posters used distinct sub-archetypes inside the locked style: lookup matrices (1.1/1.2), a signal-trace strip (2.0), an operating-band curve dashboard (3.1), a build-up assembly schematic (3.2). This article needs a *new* sub-archetype that is unmistakably "Engineering Diagnostic Instrument" but visually its own: a **process & instrumentation diagram (P&ID) of the oxygen delivery chain**, with the Fick equation as the master bus and two labeled "tuning gains" wired to their protocols. The body is drawn as a piped flow system you instrument and tune — which is exactly the article's framing of VO2 max as "whatever number falls out of that whole chain when you run it as hard as it can go."

**The single key point to make a reader feel:** *VO2 max is one number coming out of a two-lever machine. One lever is your heart's stroke volume (built by intervals — the ceiling); the other is your muscles' oxygen extraction, i.e. mitochondria and capillaries (built by Zone 2 — the floor). HRmax is bolted shut. Train both levers, in the 80/20 split, because they live in different organs and answer to different stimuli.* A second free lesson the diagram teaches: *the number is a composite of system integrity — that's why it predicts how long you live.*

## 1. The Big Idea

_VO2 max is the output of a single oxygen-delivery pipeline (intake → pump → transport → extraction → combustion). The Fick equation splits it into three terms: one bolted shut (HRmax), and two trainable gains — CENTRAL stroke volume (a bigger pump, built by intervals) and PERIPHERAL extraction (more/better mitochondria + capillaries, built by Zone 2). Two organs, two stimuli, one polarized 80/20 protocol that falls straight out of the biology._ The poster is a process-and-instrumentation diagram an engineer would tape inside a control panel: the pipeline across the middle, the Fick equation as the master readout up top, two glowing "tuning gain" knobs wired down to their protocols, and four small auxiliary gauges (lactate, genetics, aging, mortality) around the edges.

## 2. Visual Metaphor & Structure

A **process & instrumentation diagram (P&ID) of the aerobic engine** — the kind of piped-system schematic with inline sensors, gauges and tuning valves you'd find on a control-room reference card. The body's oxygen chain is drawn as a left-to-right flow line; the two trainable terms of the Fick equation are rendered as two illuminated **gain stages** on that line, each wired down to the protocol that drives it; the fixed term is drawn as a **locked valve**. Supporting ideas are auxiliary instruments docked around the main schematic.

The dominant zone (center band, ~58% of the height) is the **delivery-chain flow line**, read left → right exactly in the article's order ("oxygen taken in by the lungs, pumped by the heart, carried in the blood, extracted by the working muscles, and burned by the mitochondria"):

- **Stage 1 — INTAKE (lungs):** a small inlet node `LUNGS · O₂ in`. Quiet — the article notes healthy lungs are a *requirement* but not the trainable lever for most people.
- **Stage 2 — PUMP (heart) — CENTRAL GAIN [trainable]:** the first hero element. A pump node split into its two Fick sub-terms drawn as two inline dials:
  - `HRmax` rendered as a **LOCKED dial** — needle pinned, a small padlock/`FIXED — genetics + age` tag. This is the visual punch: one term is bolted shut.
  - `STROKE VOLUME ↑` rendered as an **adjustable gain dial** turned up, glowing with the success/active accent, tagged `TRAINABLE · the ceiling`. A small inset cross-section shows the **left ventricle chamber expanding** (eccentric hypertrophy) + a `+plasma volume` note for the fast 2–4 week effect.
  - Bracket beneath both dials: `= CARDIAC OUTPUT (Q)`.
- **Stage 3 — TRANSPORT (blood):** a connector pipe node `BLOOD · O₂ carried` with a small `plasma volume ↑` tick linking back to the early-weeks stroke-volume bump. Quiet.
- **Stage 4 — EXTRACTION (working muscle) — PERIPHERAL GAIN [trainable]:** the second hero element. An extraction node = the `a-vO₂ difference ↑` gain dial, turned up, glowing, tagged `TRAINABLE · the floor`. Wired into it, a small **capillary-density** icon (more vessels per fibre) and the master biogenesis readout (see Stage 5).
- **Stage 5 — COMBUSTION (mitochondria):** the terminal node, drawn as a small **biogenesis cascade chip**: `Ca²⁺ / AMPK → PGC-1α → NRF-1/2 · TFAM → +mitochondria (more & better)`. PGC-1α labeled `THE CONDUCTOR`. This is the molecular floor of the whole chain.

**The master bus (top of the schematic, full width):** the **Fick equation rendered as the headline instrument readout**, with each term colour-/weight-coded to its stage below and a thin leader line dropping from each term to its node:

`VO₂max = ( HRmax × SVmax ) × (a-vO₂ diff)max`
`            [LOCKED]  [CENTRAL↑]      [PERIPHERAL↑]`

This is the spine. A reader who reads only the master bus + the two gain dials gets the entire thesis.

**The two protocol rails (bottom of the schematic, two wired feeds):** beneath the flow line, two distinct "supply lines" wire *up* into their respective gain dials — this is the part that makes the protocol feel inevitable:

- **LEFT FEED → CENTRAL GAIN:** `INTERVALS · 4×4 @ 90–95% HRmax · 1–2×/wk` with the one-line mechanism `sustained near-max cardiac output → LV chamber + autonomic remodel → bigger stroke`. A tiny note: `4-min bout = the sweet spot: long enough to stress, short enough to repeat.`
- **RIGHT FEED → PERIPHERAL GAIN:** `ZONE 2 · ≥3 h/wk · most days` with the one-line mechanism `hours of moderate Ca²⁺/AMPK signal → PGC-1α → biogenesis; recruits Type I fibres`.
- Where the two feeds meet under the chain, a small junction labels the payoff: `80 / 20 POLARIZED — not a tradition; a readout of the biology. Easy builds the floor, hard builds the ceiling.`

**Auxiliary instruments (docked around the main schematic — small, clearly secondary):**

1. **LACTATE SHUTTLE gauge** (myth-correction callout): a small two-node loop `GLYCOLYTIC FIBRE —MCT→ OXIDATIVE TISSUE (burns it)` with the strike-through myth and the correction: `LACTATE ≠ waste. It's fuel. LT2 rises because clearance improves — not because you make less.`
2. **RESPONDER SLOPE plot** (genetics): a tiny multi-line chart — several lines fanning upward at different slopes from a shared origin — labeled `HERITAGE: ~47% of trainability is heritable · spread is huge · low-responder ≠ no-responder`. Verdict chip: `Track YOUR slope, not anyone else's.`
3. **AGE-DECLINE strip** (the central/peripheral asymmetry): a small descending-bands readout `~10%/decade untrained · CENTRAL fades faster than PERIPHERAL → intervals matter MORE with age, not less.` With the one-liner: `Trained 60yo out-scores sedentary 25yo.`
4. **MORTALITY COMPOSITE readout** (the payoff): a small "integrity panel" listing the five subsystems a high VO2 max *requires* — `HEART · LUNGS · BLOOD · MITOCHONDRIA · LEAN MUSCLE — all-or-nothing` — with: `Mandsager 2018 · n=122,007 · no upper limit of benefit. You can't fake any of them — that's why it predicts how long you live.`

This sub-archetype — **P&ID flow schematic + Fick master bus + two wired protocol feeds + auxiliary gauges** — is deliberately different from Part 2.0's signal-trace strip and the other Fit posters, while remaining unmistakably "Engineering Diagnostic Instrument."

## 5. UX / visual encoding expectations _(semantic — colors come from design.md, not from here)_

- **The Fick bus and the two gain dials are the hero. Everything else is support.** The reader's takeaway is the *split into two trainable levers plus one locked term*. The equation up top and the two glowing dials on the flow line must dominate; the auxiliary gauges must be visibly quieter (smaller, lower-contrast chrome). If the four side gauges compete with the dials, the thesis blurs.
- **Locked vs. trainable is the single most important visual contrast.** Render `HRmax` as a genuinely *locked* instrument — pinned needle, padlock glyph, dimmed/desaturated surface — and the two trainable gains (`SV`, `a-vO₂`) as *active* dials turned up and carrying the success/active accent. This one contrast carries the article's core honesty ("don't train what's bolted shut; pour everything into the two terms that move").
- **The two protocol feeds must visibly wire to different nodes.** Intervals → the heart's SV dial; Zone 2 → the muscle's a-vO₂ dial. Draw these as two distinct supply lines that physically connect protocol to organ. The whole "two organs, two protocols" point lives in those two wires landing in two different places. Don't let them merge into one ambiguous arrow.
- **Semantic color, rationed (three states max):**
  - **Success / active** role — *only* on the two trainable gain dials (SV, a-vO₂) and their protocol feeds, to mark "this is the lever you pull." Ration tight.
  - **Neutral / locked** role — the HRmax term and dial, deliberately desaturated (the "you can't move this" signal). Not a warning color — just inert.
  - **Warning / danger** roles — *not used as alarms here.* This is a "how the engine works" schematic, not a fault panel. Reserve any single accent on the **MORTALITY COMPOSITE** gauge for gravity, not alarm — keep it sober.
- **The biogenesis cascade is a chain, not a cloud.** `Ca²⁺/AMPK → PGC-1α → NRF-1/2·TFAM → +mitochondria` must read as a directed left-to-right signal chain with PGC-1α visibly flagged as the hub ("the conductor"). Don't render it as a soft biological blob; render it as signal flow through a controller.
- **The flow line is a pipe, not a body.** Keep the delivery chain abstract — inlet, pump, transport, extraction, combustion — as engineering nodes on a pipe. **No anatomical hearts-and-lungs illustration.** A small LV *cross-section schematic* (chamber expanding) and a small *capillary-density* icon are allowed because they're diagrammatic, but the overall read is a P&ID, not a biology-textbook torso.
- **Hierarchy of attention:** headline → Fick master bus → the flow schematic with its two glowing gain dials (hero) → the two protocol feeds + 80/20 junction → the four auxiliary gauges (tertiary) → footer chrome.
- **Saveability:** the locked-vs-trainable contrast and the two-feeds-to-two-organs wiring must survive grayscale and a small phone screenshot. Carry the lesson with dial state (pinned vs. open), padlock glyph, line weight and labels — not hue alone. A lifter should be able to glance at the saved card and recall "intervals→heart→ceiling, Zone 2→mito→floor, HRmax is locked."

## 7. What to Avoid

1. **An anatomical heart-and-lungs illustration.** The obvious move is a red-and-blue circulatory diagram — a literal heart, literal lungs, blood-flow arrows through a torso. That betrays the pillar style (this is an *instrument schematic*, not a biology poster) and, worse, it buries the article's actual thesis (two *trainable terms* of an equation) under decorative anatomy. Keep the chain abstract — pipe, pump, gains, combustion node. The only anatomy allowed is the tiny diagrammatic LV cross-section and the capillary icon.
2. **A simple Zone-2-vs-intervals two-column comparison.** It is tempting to drop the equation and the chain and just draw a left/right "Zone 2 | Intervals" table. That loses the *why* — that the two protocols win because they wire to two different terms of the Fick equation living in two different organs. The comparison is the *output* of the schematic, not a replacement for it. The equation and the chain have to be present so the protocol split looks *inevitable*, not asserted.
3. **Giving all five auxiliary topics equal weight with the core split.** The article has one central idea (two levers + one lock) and four supporting riffs (lactate, genetics, aging, mortality). If the four side gauges are sized like the main schematic, the poster becomes a flat "7 facts about VO2 max" listicle and the thesis dissolves. Ration size and contrast hard: the Fick bus + flow line + two feeds are the poster; the four gauges are marginalia.
4. **Coloring the pipeline stages each their own hue (rainbow plumbing).** Tempting to give lungs / heart / blood / muscle / mitochondria each a distinct color. This saturates the schematic, destroys the rationed-semantic-color policy, and—most damaging—kills the locked-vs-trainable contrast that is the whole point. Keep the pipe and nodes in neutral instrument ink; spend the *only* active accent on the two trainable gain dials and their feeds, and the desaturated/locked treatment on HRmax.
