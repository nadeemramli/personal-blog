## 7. What to Avoid

- **A flat "menu of treatments" grid or a clinic price-list / before-after gallery.** A grid flattens the article's whole point — the *ladder* and the *gate*. The risk order and the bloodwork chokepoint are the argument; a menu hides both. Treatments appear only as rungs on the graded plant or lines on the riser, never as equal tiles.
- **A glowing / dewy / before-after face, or a syringe / laser-on-skin "beauty clinic" photo.** Says "med-spa ad," teaches nothing, and buries the one rule that matters (bloodwork). Never make a real face or a glamour shot the hero. (A small Fitzpatrick III–V swatch may sit *inside* the laser caution band if needed, never as the anchor.)
