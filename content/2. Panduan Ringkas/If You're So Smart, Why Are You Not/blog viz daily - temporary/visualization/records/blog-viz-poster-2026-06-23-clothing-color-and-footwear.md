## Visual concept used

The locked Attractive-pillar style ("Architect's Elevation / Spec Sheet") in its **STANDARD-ISSUE LOADOUT / parts-catalog variant** — the first *catalog/matrix* drawing in the pillar and the **series hinge poster**, deliberately tilted cool to mark the pivot from biology to convention. Landscape 1680×1120 (3:2) on bone drafting-paper with a faint dot grid, drafting border and corner ticks. A **DEMAND SELECTOR** rail (`INDEX BY DEMAND, NOT BY MOOD`) carries four stamped keys — `TRAIN · LOUNGE · EXECUTE · HIGH-STAKES` — each wired by a dashed leader down into exactly one of **FOUR MATCHED BAYS**, drawn as tech-pack garment flats: Bay 1 Gym (fitted tee + tapered jogger), Bay 2 Casual Lounge (crew/henley + straight leg, with an amber struck-out oversized tee `casual ≠ unkempt`), **Bay 3 Daily Casual — "THE GO-TO"**, drawn largest and pushed forward with a terracotta `60–70% OF DAYS · BUILD THIS FIRST` plate (OCBD/tee + chino + overshirt), and Bay 4 Formal (tailored two-piece + dress shirt, `can't be faked → see a tailor`). Each bay carries a `5–7 PIECES / SET` capacity plate; a full-width `BUILD ORDER → 1 DAILY · 2 FORMAL · 3 GYM · 4 LOUNGE` stamp runs beneath. The warm hero is the **GSM GAUGE** — a fabric-weight scale `<140 → 140–180 → 180–240 → [240–300 sweet spot, terracotta] → 300+` whose profile rises from a limp drooping line to a square-shouldered structured block, under the biggest non-headline line on the board: **`SHAPE COMES FROM THE FABRIC, NOT THE BODY.`** plus `280 GSM @ RM 80 > 130 GSM @ RM 200`. Docked at the right in a cooler slate register is **THE COLOUR CALIBRATION CARD** (`classify once`): undertone chips (warm/cool/neutral + vein/metal/paper tests, `most MY/SE-Asian men warm-to-neutral → soft white beats stark white`), a grayscale contrast strip (`OUTFIT CONTRAST = FACE CONTRAST · most Asian men MEDIUM`), a worked-default palette, and the tag `USEFUL DEFAULT, NOT LAW`. The bottom **FOOTWEAR FOUNDATION** rail shows four shoe flats (white-leather sneaker `build first`, premium athletic, loafer/chelsea, oxford/derby) under a warm coherence note (`LEATHER → LEATHER · METAL → METAL · mismatch = a tell`) with an amber `CLEAN RM 400 > SCUFFED RM 1,500` maintenance plate. The **single restrained red** is reserved for the ghosted **REJECTED** logo-tee in the lower-left (`<140 GSM · you paid for the logo, not the fabric`) — the exact inversion of the warm GSM hero. A top **hinge banner** reads `FROM HERE ON: SIGNALING, NOT BIOLOGY — a system to remove the decision, not a law of nature`, and the left margin carries the pillar's epistemic key **reweighted small-warm / large-cool** so the series pivot is felt structurally. Headline **"GET DRESSED ONCE."**; thesis lockup **"FOUR DEMANDS. FOUR SETS. ZERO CYCLES."**

Sits as the **fourth sibling** in the pillar family (1.0 = elevation · 2.0 = hydraulic section · 3.0 = survey/spec drawing · 4.0 = standard-issue loadout) — same bone palette, same warm-measurable / cool-convention coding, same single-alarm-red discipline, a new drawing type. All §7 traps avoided: no lookbook/OOTD/model, no generic capsule-wardrobe hanger rack (garments are technical flats with spec ticks), no colour-season wheel (undertone+contrast is a classify-once card), GSM is the visible hero rather than an afterthought, and the cool-over-warm tilt + "signaling, not biology" banner make the hinge explicit.

## Render notes

- **Renderer:** CairoSVG 2.9.0 (Playwright + rsvg-convert unavailable in sandbox). Generator: `gen_poster_clothing.py` (code → SVG → PNG, no external design tools).
- **Dimensions:** landscape viewBox 1680×1120 (3:2), rendered @2× → **3360×2240**, ~505 KB. Verified non-empty, correct dimensions; visually inspected full poster + zoomed crops of bays, calibration card, and footwear/rejected-tee.
- **Fonts:** Inter / JetBrains-Mono stacks with DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Mono fallback (only DejaVu present in sandbox).
- **Build fixes after first render:** (1) canvas moved from 1200×1500 portrait to 1680×1120 landscape (3:2) — the four-bay rack + footwear rail + side card want width, and the first portrait pass collided the GSM hero callout, footwear ties and rejected-tee in the lower third; (2) the `5–7 PIECES/SET` capacity plate was colliding with the long bay-header text, so it was moved to a centred stamp under each subtag and the garment flats nudged down; (3) calibration-card bottom dead space filled with a worked-default palette row.
- **Deviations / fallbacks:** none. Fresh build — no prior poster existed for this concept (no deletes needed; exactly one PNG + one SVG kept). The `aether-call-to-action-DESIGN.md` token file is a generic dark-UI CTA template, so the brief's own §5 palette was used as the authoritative source (bone `#F4F1EA`, ink `#1E2024`, terracotta `#C2703D` = measurable, slate `#5E7585` = convention, red `#D9483B` = the rejected logo-tee only, amber `#B5862E` = calm cautions).
