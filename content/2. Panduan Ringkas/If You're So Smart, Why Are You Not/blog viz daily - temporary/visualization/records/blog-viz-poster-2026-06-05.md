## Visual concept used
A landscape **structural-condition survey** of one hair follicle drawn in three states across a left-to-right time axis, cut by a hard vertical **CONDEMNATION LINE** (the fibrosis point of no return). State 1 **SOUND** is a thick upright terracotta column with a stem-cell pocket and a preventive-maintenance tag; State 2 **DISTRESSED** is a thinner, leaning column wrapped in restoration scaffolding under a `← THE WINDOW →` bracket, with a backward-curving terracotta recovery arrow ("chemistry buys back lost ground") — the only motion that points against time, and it stops dead at the line. The red condemnation line carries margin labels `CHEMISTRY REACHES HERE →` (warm) / `← SURGERY ONLY` (red) and a vertical crossing caption ("the pore seals. nothing crosses back."). State 3 **SEALED** is a paved-over, pore-less plot with a ghost of the razed structure, a rotated `CONDEMNED` survey-stamp, and a transplant import-arrow entering from outside the frame. A ghost-grey **fixed-parcel line** runs beneath all three ("THE LOT IS FIXED — ~100,000 follicles at birth…"). The lower third is the three-piece spec sheet in cool slate: three KPI gauges (T:V ratio, density, shaft Ø), the RM-priced measurement-tier ladder with the Malaysian grace note, and the split-lens 5-second field test (PEACH FUZZ = ALIVE / SLICK SKIN = SEALED). Bottom lockup: `STAY ON THE CHEMISTRY SIDE OF THE LINE.` + the SOUND→prevent / DISTRESSED→rescue / SEALED→accept decision strip.

Locked Attractive signature reused verbatim (carried from Appearance 1.0): bone-paper drafting surface (`#F4F1EA`), graphite ink (`#1E2024`), terracotta = living/recoverable (`#C2703D`), slate-blue = measuring instruments only (`#5E7585`), single alarm red reserved for fibrosis/condemnation (`#D9483B`), ghost-grey for the un-creatable fixed lot (`#9A958C`). Two-register surface (restoration-blueprint left of the line, sealed/condemnation-hatch right) preserved as the signature move. Sub-archetype: condition survey read across time (vs. Appearance's elevation built upward).
