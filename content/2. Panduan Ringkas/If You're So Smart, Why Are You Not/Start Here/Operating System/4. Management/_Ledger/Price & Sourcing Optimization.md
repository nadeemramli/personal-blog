## 4. Sourcing structure & the INDEXA-style play

**Vendors today:** kohohpharma (PEDs, ~RM149–200/vial — bulk/multi-vial to amortize crypto+ship), iHerb (supplements — RM22/RM450 shipping, so batch), Shopee/Lazada (pharma + hair + protein), shopee-china (cheap generics: aspirin, irbesartan, acarbose).

**The structural lever (how you funded peptides via INDEXA):** the cheapest ringgit is one you don't pay. Beyond per-item deals, the durable move is **tying an income stream to a cost category** so it self-funds:
- **Peptides → INDEXA** (already done — RM0 personal).
- **Supplement stack → affiliate/content.** You're writing the *If You're So Smart* series; iHerb/Shopee affiliate links on the Healthy/Fit articles could offset a chunk of the RM6–7k/yr supplement spend.
- **PEDs → group-buy / split orders** with trusted others to hit bulk pricing and split shipping/crypto fees.
- **Hair stack (~RM2,800/yr)** is the most "pure cost" category with no funding tie — the best candidate to either re-source hard (Oral Minox) or attach to content (a hair-protocol post).

> [!note] The principle
> Per-item deals trim ~RM2k/yr. The INDEXA model removes whole *categories* from the personal bill. Rank effort accordingly: re-source the 2–3 priciest pure-cost lines now (Oral Minox, protein), then think structurally about funding the Foundational layer the way INDEXA funds peptides.
