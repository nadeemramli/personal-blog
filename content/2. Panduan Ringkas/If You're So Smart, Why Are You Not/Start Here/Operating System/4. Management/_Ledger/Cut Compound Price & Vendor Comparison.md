## 3. Market reference points (for the generics, from web)

- **Tadalafil:** MY retail RM40–80 per 20mg tab; online generics from ~RM12. **Your shopee-china = ~RM5.64 / 20mg-equiv — already the cheapest channel.** Lever is *split a 20mg* (you dose 5mg) → ~RM0.71/dose, not switch vendor.
- **Empagliflozin:** brand Jardiance 25mg is expensive in MY; generic per-tab pricing not transparently listed online — **confirm shopee-china/Lazada actual before trusting the ~RM60/30 placeholder.** Check the KKM FUKKM formulary.
- **Rosuvastatin / Irbesartan / Aspirin:** already at/below MY generic floor via shopee-china; no vendor switch needed (split the 20mg statin per the deals note).

1. **P4 shield + generics:** keep shopee-china; only optimization is **tablet-splitting** (Tadalafil, Rosuvastatin) — already in [[Price & Sourcing Optimization]]. No vendor decision needed.
