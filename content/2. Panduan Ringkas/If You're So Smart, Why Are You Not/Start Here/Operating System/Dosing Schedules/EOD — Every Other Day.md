# EOD — Every Other Day

Every other day (interval 2 days = 7/2 administrations/wk); per-dose = weekly dose x 2/7.

> [!important] Forecast factor
> Effective daily burn = `daily_dose × days_per_week / 7` = daily_dose × 3.5/7.
