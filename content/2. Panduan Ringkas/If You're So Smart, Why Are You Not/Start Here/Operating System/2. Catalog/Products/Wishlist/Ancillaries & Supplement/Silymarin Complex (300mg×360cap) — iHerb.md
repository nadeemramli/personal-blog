## How I use it
- **Role:** hepatocyte recovery and antioxidant support after the liver-taxing window has ended.
- **Do not use as the main intra-cycle oral-AAS support.** During oral-heavy blocks, [[TUDCA (250mg×60tab) — iHerb|TUDCA]] and [[NAC (600mg×180tab) — iHerb|NAC]] own the active stress window.
- **Best timing:** with a fat-containing meal after oral AAS or other liver-taxing compounds have cleared.
- **Quality note:** this is a standardized extract product, not raw cheap milk thistle powder. It is still not a phytosome/Siliphos-style phospholipid-bound silybin product.
