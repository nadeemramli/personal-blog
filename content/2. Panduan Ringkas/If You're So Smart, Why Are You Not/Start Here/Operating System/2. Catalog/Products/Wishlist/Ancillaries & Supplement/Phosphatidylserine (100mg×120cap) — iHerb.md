## How I use it
- **Pathway:** P7 - the [[Fat Loss Pathways (Theory)#pathway-7--the-counter-regulatory-wall-cortisol--hpa-axis|cortisol / HPA wall]]. Evidence is modest but plausible for stress/cortisol support under acute physical stress.
- **Slot:** **Post-heavy workout** or **21:30 pre-bed** in [[Fat Loss Daily Schedule (Cut)]]. Use it on the days the nervous system feels over-revved, not as an automatic daily baseline.
- **Why it matters on a cut:** chronic cortisol can worsen sleep, appetite, water retention, and recovery. PS is a supplement-level recovery lever; [[Oxandrolone (Anavar) (10mg×100tab) — kohohpharma|Oxandrolone]] is the pharmacological glucocorticoid block.
- **Stack fit:** complementary to [[Uridine Monophosphate (300mg×60tab) — iHerb|Uridine]] + [[CDP-Choline (300mg×120tab) — iHerb|CDP-Choline]] rather than redundant. Uridine/CDP/Fish Oil build the phospholipid/choline lane; PS gives a direct membrane phospholipid plus stress-response effect.
- **MB fit:** compatible with [[Methylene Blue Protocol|Methylene Blue]] days. This is not the same risk class as saffron, tyrosine, caffeine, or Huperzine-A on MB days.
- **Substitute:** [[Ashwagandha (600mg×120tab) — iHerb|Ashwagandha]] (already in stock) covers the same stress/sleep slot more cheaply; PS is cleaner and more targeted when budget allows. Usually run **one**, not both.
