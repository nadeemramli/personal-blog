The blood panel (Hematocrit `<52%`, Sensitive E₂, lipids, renal) decides the cut's endocrine base:
- **Markers clean →** hold **Test 350mg** straight into the 12-week cut.
- **Markers stressed →** drop to a **cruise** (~200mg) to recover *before* starting the 12-week cut.
