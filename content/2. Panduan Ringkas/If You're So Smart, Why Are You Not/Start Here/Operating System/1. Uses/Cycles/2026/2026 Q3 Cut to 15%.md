# 2026 Cut to 15%

> [!abstract] The cut campaign — a **12-week aggressive cut to ~18% BF @ 91 kg**, the first block toward a 12–15% maintenance physique.
> Architecture: absolute **anti-catabolic insurance** (Trenbolone + HMB + BHB) layered with **aggressive metabolic + mitochondrial accelerators** — muscle loss biologically blocked while fat oxidation is maximized. Mapped onto the [[Pipelines|4-pipeline framework]]: [[Endocrine Design Rules|P1]] base, [[Cut Protocol|P2]] fuel, [[Mini-Cut & Accelerator Tiers|P3]] deployment, [[Year-Round Shield|P4]] shield. Quantities/cost → [[Cut Campaign Stock & Cost]].

> [!important] Start is GATED on the 29 Jun 2026 blood panel.
> Current cycle [[2026 Q2 Cycle]] is a **bulk** finishing ~wk 10 (panel **29 Jun**). That panel decides the cut's base.
> `start_date 2026-07-06` is provisional pending that decision. 91 kg · ~23–25% BF · target ~18% in 12 wks.

> [!info] ⭐ ESTER SWITCH — Test Cypionate → Test Enanthate (decided 2026-06-08)
> **No cruise/bridge** (decided). Testosterone runs **continuous 350 mg/wk** straight through; the ester just changes mid-stream when the last Test C is used up. Cyp (~8-day half-life) → Enan (~7-day) at the same dose needs **no re-stabilization** — blood levels stay flat.
> - **Test C on hand (8 Jun):** ~1,800 mg (8 prefilled syringes @40u/100mg = 800 mg + 4 ml vial = 1,000 mg) → ~5 wks left.
> - **Burn-down:** the Q2 bulk tail (8 Jun→6 Jul, ~4 wk) eats ~1,400 mg → **only ~400 mg Test C into the cut (~wk 1)**, then **Test E 450 from ~wk 2 to wk 12** (~11 wk × 350 = ~3,850 mg ≈ <1 vial).
> - **Test E = [[Testosterone Enanthate Etho 450 (450mg-ml ×10ml) — beligaspharmacy Intl|Beligas Etho 450]]** (450mg/ml). At 350mg/wk pin **0.78 ml/wk** (0.39 ml ×2), vs 1.4 ml on the old 250 Test C.
> - **From Q4 forever: Test E only.** ~4 vials/yr at 350mg/wk.

## Nutrition (the 12-week plan)

**Maintenance:** ~2,100–2,300 kcal base; **+250 kcal** from 8k steps EOD → effective TDEE **~2,650**.
**Strategy:** eat a **consistent target every day** within each week-type — don't toggle by walking day; it averages out.

| Week type | Weeks | kcal/day | Macro split |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase A — Low** | 1-2, 4-5, 7-8, 10-11 | **1,450** | P 200g (800) · F 45g (405) · C 60g (240) — ~40g clean carbs around the workout |
| **Phase B — Refeed** | **3, 6, 9, 12** | **2,650** | high-carb (~370g+) glycogen refill |

> [!note] Low weeks at 1,450 are aggressive (modified-fast style) but offset by full-maintenance refeed weeks every 3rd week — the average lands the ~18% target in 12 wks. Protein stays the 200g muscle anchor throughout; the +250 step-calories let fats rise to 45g (joint/hormone support on 350mg Test).

**Refeed weeks double as the safe window for Empagliflozin** (high carbs = glucose to dump; never dose SGLT2 on the lowest-carb days).
