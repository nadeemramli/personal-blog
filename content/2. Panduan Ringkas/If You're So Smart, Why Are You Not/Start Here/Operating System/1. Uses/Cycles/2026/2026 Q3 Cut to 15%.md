# 2026 Cut to 15%

> [!abstract] The cut campaign — a **12-week aggressive cut to ~18% BF @ 91 kg**, the first block toward a 12–15% maintenance physique.
> Architecture: absolute **anti-catabolic insurance** (Trenbolone + BHB) layered with **aggressive metabolic + mitochondrial accelerators** — muscle loss biologically blocked while fat oxidation is maximized. Mapped onto the [[Pipelines|4-pipeline framework]]: [[Endocrine Design Rules|P1]] base, [[Cut Protocol|P2]] fuel, [[Off-Season Mini-Cut & Accelerator Tiers|P3]] deployment, [[Year-Round Shield|P4]] shield. Quantities/cost → [[Cut Campaign Stock & Cost]].

## Cut Control Model

The protocol is run as a **feedback system**, not a suffering contest. Fat loss is driven by the boring levers first; pharmacology only earns its place if it makes those levers easier to execute or protects output while the deficit is active.

| Priority | Lever | How it is judged |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calorie target + protein | 7-day weight average drops while **200g protein/day** stays automatic. |
| 2 | Steps + post-meal movement | **10k steps/day** and **15 min air squats/walk after meals** happen before adding more intensity. |
| 3 | Resistance training performance | Historical benchmarks are held within acceptable drift; lifting stays heavy enough to defend lean mass. |
| 4 | Appetite control | [[Retatrutide (60mg×10vial) — BFF-AMO\|Retatrutide]] is used to make the diet executable, not to justify chaotic eating. |
| 5 | Targeted mobilization | Yohimbine/caffeine/stimulant work is only useful when paired with fasted light movement. |
| 6 | Helper layers | Thyroid, glucose flushing, mitochondrial agents, and pump support are secondary amplifiers; they do not replace the deficit. |

**Operating rule:** adjust **one lever at a time**, then wait 7-14 days before judging. If the scale trend, waist, performance, and sleep disagree, the trend wins only after two full weekly averages.

## Pipeline 2 — Bio-Feedback (The Controlled Deficit)

- **Controlled deficit** baseline; executed as **low weeks + short refeed blocks** (see Nutrition below).
- **Target:** strip the final 3–4% stubborn fat to ~18% while holding **historical strength benchmarks** in the logbook.
- **10k steps/day** + **15 min air squats after meals** → daily expenditure floor and post-meal glucose sink (see Nutrition).
- **No intensity creep:** if fat loss stalls, adjust calories/steps first; do not turn lifting into cardio.

**Glucose disposal** (the glycogen-depletion layer)
- [[Metformin-Empagliflozin FDC (500-5mg×300tab) — shopee-china|Metformin]] **500 mg** + [[Metformin-Empagliflozin FDC (500-5mg×300tab) — shopee-china|Empagliflozin]] **5 mg** — **run 4 weeks straight** to flash-deplete liver/muscle glycogen (≈72h) and force deep fat oxidation, then drop.

**Beta-3 activation**
- [[Mirabegron (50mg×30tab) — shopee-china|Mirabegron]] **50 mg to test tolerance → 100 mg** only if resting HR stays stable · RM184/30×50mg (shopee-china).

**Beta-2 tolerance-support layer**
- [[Ketotifen (1mg×120tab) — Lazada|Ketotifen]] is included as the intended β2-receptor resensitization/upregulation support alongside [[Clenbuterol (40mcg×100tab) — kohohpharma|Clenbuterol]].
- This is a **tolerance hypothesis, not safety clearance**. Ketotifen does not cancel clenbuterol-associated tachycardia, arrhythmia, blood-pressure, myocardial-injury, or hypokalaemia risk.
- The 12-week calendar is a planning horizon only. Continuation requires acceptable symptoms, resting HR/BP, clinician-reviewed ECG and electrolytes; the presence of Ketotifen is never a reason to override a stop signal.

> [!warning] Glycogen-depletion side effect (expected, not muscle loss): Metformin + SGLT2 + deep deficit empties glycogen in ~72h → muscles look **flat/deflated**. Trenbolone is preventing catabolism; the Glycerol + C8 MCT + Creatine pre-workout restores the pump. Don't panic-eat.

## Nutrition (the 12-week plan)

**Maintenance:** ~2,100–2,300 kcal base; **10k steps/day** + post-meal air squats create the movement floor → effective TDEE target **~2,650**.
**Strategy:** low days do the fat loss; short refeed blocks protect training, glycogen, sleep, and compliance. Refeed weeks are no longer full maintenance weeks by default.

| Day type | Used in | kcal/day | Macro split | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| **Low day** | Default day | **1,450** | P 200g (800) · F 45g (405) · C 60g (240) | Main fat-loss engine. Keep ~40g clean carbs around training. |
| **Refeed day** | Weeks 3, 6, 9, 12 | **2,650** | P 200g · high-carb (~350-370g+) · low/moderate fat | Glycogen refill and performance rescue. Place around hardest training. |

| Week type | Weeks | Structure | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low week** | 1-2, 4-5, 7-8, 10-11 | 7 low days | Stay boring and consistent. |
| **Controlled refeed week** | 3, 6, 9, 12 | 4-5 low days + 2-3 refeed days | Default **2 refeed days**; add the 3rd only if training output, sleep, or mood clearly need it. |

> [!note] This keeps the original low/refeed rhythm but removes the automatic 7-day maintenance block. The cut stays aggressive, but the high-carb days are still there when they actually protect performance.

**Protein distribution:** 200g/day is the anchor. Inside the eating window, hit **3 protein feedings** if possible (for example 60-70g / 60-70g / 60-70g). If the 18-hour fast makes 200g protein unreliable, widen the eating window before cutting protein.

**Refeed placement:** put refeed days on the hardest training day and the day after, not randomly. If using a 3rd refeed day, place it before the next heavy lower/body benchmark session.

**Retatrutide role:** use appetite control to make the planned calories easy, not to skip protein or under-eat so hard that training collapses.

**Empagliflozin placement:** refeed days are the only window where the glucose-dump logic fits the food plan. Do not turn the lowest-carb days into extra SGLT2 days.

## Adjustment Rules

| Signal | Rule | First adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| 7-day weight average not dropping for 14 days | Confirm adherence before changing the plan. | Add +2k steps/day or remove 100-150 kcal from low days. |
| Strength drops hard for 2 sessions | Treat it as a fuel/recovery problem first. | Move carbs closer to training or add the 3rd refeed day that week. |
| Hunger is already controlled | Do not add more appetite/stim layers. | Keep the plan boring; let Retatrutide do its job. |
| Sleep worsens | Protect sleep before adding more fat-loss pressure. | Remove the latest stimulant/yohimbine pulse first. |
| Palpitations, chest pain, fainting, marked breathlessness, persistent racing/irregular heartbeat, or severe tremor | Treat as a clenbuterol stop signal, even if Ketotifen is being used. | Stop the stimulant layer and seek urgent medical assessment. |
| Resting HR/BP, ECG, potassium, or magnesium becomes abnormal | Ketotifen does not override cardiovascular or electrolyte toxicity. | Hold clenbuterol and obtain clinician review before any continuation. |
| Scale drops too fast and performance collapses | The deficit is too expensive. | Add 1 refeed day or raise low days by 100-150 kcal. |
| Waist drops but scale stalls | Likely water/glycogen noise. | Hold for another week; do not panic-adjust. |
