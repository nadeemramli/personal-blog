> [!abstract] Nadeem's training rhythm, for any calc that depends on training days or injection cadence.

## The rhythm
**3 days train → 1 day rest → repeat.** A rolling **4-day microcycle**, deliberately *not* locked to the 7-day calendar week. Nadeem's note: inconsistent against the week, but it's the best fit so far — and it **aligns with EOD injections**.

> [!important] **Frequency is constant across every cycle** (bulk, cut, cruise) — the 3-on-1-off rhythm never changes. Only *which compounds* are pinned changes (see [[Pinning Schedule]]).

## What this means for the numbers
- **Training days ≈ 5.25 per week** (21 training days per 28-day block). Use this, not "3–4×/week", for any training-day-dosed item.
- **Injections are EOD** (every other day, ~3.5×/week), riding the same rhythm — steroids + most peptides pin on this cadence.
- **Training-day-only compounds burn faster than a weekly average suggests.** Example: MOTS-C at 2mg/training-day = ~10.5mg/week during a cut, not 4–5mg.
- Pre-/Intra-workout, protein timing, and stimulants key off training days (5.25/wk), not all 7.

## Forecast hook
The stock engine maps dosing codes to days/week (`ED 7, EOD 3.5, TD 6, ITD 4, OCC 4`). For **training-day items**, treat `TD` as ~5.25/week effective under this rhythm (the 3-on-1-off pattern), and confirm the cut's training split when forecasting cut-only peptides.
