# Training Nutrition & Pre-Intra Protocol

> [!abstract] Owns food and training-support execution.
> Product owners include [[Whole Eggs (30eggs) — Grocer]], [[On The Go Protein Isolate (OTG) (1000g) — Shopee]], [[SPI (Soy Protein Isolate, HK) (1000g) — Shopee]], [[Pre-Workout (1014g) — iHerb]], [[Intra-Workout (651g) — iHerb]], [[Beta-Alanine (1000g) — Shopee]], [[L-Citrulline (1000g) — Shopee]], [[L-Carnitine (1000g) — Shopee]], [[Glutamine (1000g) — Shopee]], [[Glycerol (1000g) — Shopee]], [[Creatine Monohydrate (1000g) — Shopee]], [[BHB Salts (442g) — iHerb]], and [[C8 MCT Powder (907g) — iHerb]].

> [!important] Hierarchy
> Total daily calories and protein beat supplement timing. Pre/intra products only matter after the food target, hydration, sleep, and training plan are written.

## What This Protocol Owns

- Protein target and protein inventory.
- Training-day carb, hydration, electrolyte, pump, and intra-workout support.
- Cut/bulk differences for nutrition execution.
- Collagen substrate support for [[Recovery & Repair Peptide Protocol]].
- Stock math for food, powders, pre-workout, intra-workout, and repeatable training support.

- Protein/food: [[Whole Eggs (30eggs) — Grocer]], [[On The Go Protein Isolate (OTG) (1000g) — Shopee]], [[SPI (Soy Protein Isolate, HK) (1000g) — Shopee]]

## Entry Criteria

Use this protocol when:

- A cut, bulk, lean bulk, mini-cut, final shred, recovery block, or PCT block starts.
- Protein target, food stock, or training support needs forecasting.
- Training performance is dropping and the question is nutrition/support rather than chemistry.
- Collagen/tissue repair is the target and [[Recovery & Repair Peptide Protocol]] needs a substrate layer.

## Phase Logic

- **Bulk / lean bulk:** calories and carbs support progressive training. Pre/intra is used to improve session quality and digestion, not to hide dirty food structure.
- **Cut:** protein stays high, carbs are targeted around training, pump/hydration support protects performance, and stimulant overlap is watched.
- **Mini-cut:** keep it simple. Protein, steps, hydration, electrolytes, and enough carbs to keep training useful.
- **Final shred:** support hydration, electrolytes, and training quality. Do not let pre-workout stack on top of already-high stimulant load.
- **PCT/recovery:** training deload, sleep, protein consistency, and micronutrient coverage matter more than maximal performance.

## Decision Framework

1. **Set the target.** Phase goal decides calories, protein, carbs, fats, sodium, and hydration.
2. **Choose the protein lane.** Eggs, whey/isolate, soy isolate, and normal meals all count; the goal is consistency and digestion.
3. **Add carbs around performance.** Intra-workout belongs when sessions are long, hard, pump-limited, or performance is declining.
4. **Add pump/hydration tools only when useful.** Citrulline, glycerol, creatine, and electrolytes support output; they are not substitutes for carbs, sodium, or sleep.
5. **Use EAAs only when they solve an amino-acid timing gap.** A complete EAA formula can be useful when training is fasted, meals are far apart, appetite is suppressed, or the session is long enough that intra-workout amino acids improve adherence/recovery. If daily protein and per-meal leucine/EAA targets are already hit, EAAs are optional, not magic.
6. **Separate stimulant pre-workout from fat-loss stimulants.** If caffeine/yohimbine/clen/thyroid are active, pre-workout is reviewed through [[Blood Pressure & Fluid Escalation Protocol]] and [[Final Shred Deployment Protocol]].

## Collagen / Repair Add-On

When [[Recovery & Repair Peptide Protocol]] is active, this protocol owns:

- Total daily protein target.
- Glycine/collagen-rich food or collagen/gelatin add-on if chosen.
- Vitamin C pairing through [[Renal & Hepatic Support Protocol]] / [[Vitamin C (1000mg×60tab) — iHerb]].
- Calorie sufficiency and load management so repair is not sabotaged by a deep deficit.

## EAA Decision Rule

EAAs are a **conditional yes**, especially in PED-supported phases, but only as an amino-acid availability tool. PEDs raise the ceiling for nitrogen retention and muscle protein synthesis, which makes missed protein feedings more expensive. They do **not** make EAAs superior to hitting the total daily protein target with complete protein.

Current stock note:

- [[Intra-Workout (651g) — iHerb]] is **BCAA-based**, not a complete EAA formula. It gives BCAA 2:1:1 5g per 22g serving, but it does not provide the full essential amino acid spectrum needed to replace a complete EAA feed.

Use EAAs when:

| Situation | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fasted lifting or long gap since last protein meal | **Good use** | Provides leucine + the other essential amino acids needed for MPS substrate. |
| Appetite suppression from cut / retatrutide / stimulants | **Good use** | Low-calorie way to protect amino-acid pulses when food is hard. |
| Long high-volume session | **Good use** | Can improve adherence, perceived recovery, and amino-acid availability. |
| Already eating 20-40g high-quality protein within 1-2h pre-workout | **Low priority** | Whole protein already supplies EAAs and leucine. |
| Already hitting 1.6-2.4g/kg/day protein with good distribution | **Optional** | Extra EAAs mostly become expensive insurance. |
| Replacing meals with EAAs | **No** | EAAs are not full nutrition; they do not replace total protein, calories, micronutrients, or carbs. |

Quality target for a future EAA product:

- Full 9-EAA blend, not just BCAA.
- At least ~2-3g leucine per effective serving, or enough total EAA to reach that leucine pulse.
- Low/no stimulant if used intra-workout, because current pre/intra stack already contains caffeine and Huperzine-A.
- Third-party tested if possible.

Default protocol if added:

- **Training days only:** 1 serving intra-workout or immediately pre-workout.
- **Best slot:** fasted training, low-appetite cut, or long hard sessions.
- **Do not stack blindly** with current Intra-Workout if stimulant load is already high; either use a stimulant-free EAA or reduce the caffeinated intra.

## Monitoring

- Daily: calories, protein, hydration, digestion, sleep, training readiness.
- Training days: pump, endurance, GI comfort, cramps, stimulant tolerance, and session performance.
- Weekly: bodyweight average, waist/photos if relevant, lifts, appetite, food adherence, stock burn.
- Phase handoff: if performance is failing despite nutrition adherence, review the relevant phase protocol.

## Red Flags

Review if there is persistent GI distress, dehydration, severe cramping, stimulant intolerance, rapid performance collapse, disordered eating patterns, or electrolyte issues during aggressive cuts.

## Exit Criteria

Exit or rewrite this protocol layer when:

- The phase changes from cut to bulk, bulk to mini-cut, PCT to normal training, or final shred to recovery.
- Protein/calorie targets change materially.
- Pre/intra products are no longer improving training quality or digestion.
- Stimulant load changes enough that pre-workout needs a fresh BP/HR review.
- Stock math no longer matches actual intake.

## Stock Math

- **Protein powder:** `grams_per_day x phase_days / grams_per_container`, with reserve.
- **Eggs/food:** weekly unit count tied to meal plan, not vibes.
- **Pre/intra:** `training_days x servings_per_session`.
- **Creatine/beta-alanine/citrulline/glycerol:** calculate by active days and grams/day.
- **Cut fuel products:** BHB/MCT are phase-specific; do not project them across bulk unless explicitly used.

## C8 MCT / BHB Synergy Map

[[C8 MCT Powder (907g) — iHerb]] and [[BHB Salts (442g) — iHerb]] are not foundational daily nutrition. They are situational fuel tools for phases where glucose availability, appetite, or training flatness is a problem.

| Stack lane | Synergy | Use case | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fasted cardio / early cut window | C8 provides fast hepatic fat-derived fuel; BHB provides direct ketone substrate | Morning cardio or depleted sessions where performance/cognition drops | Start low; GI tolerance is the limiter. |
| Pre-workout volumizer stack | Pairs with [[Glycerol (1000g) — Shopee|Glycerol]], [[L-Citrulline (1000g) — Shopee|L-Citrulline]], creatine, sodium/electrolytes | Flat/depleted cut workouts where pump and output are falling | BHB salts add mineral load; include them in sodium/potassium/magnesium math. |
| Cognitive / productivity stack | Ketone substrate can smooth cognition when calories/carbs are low; compatible with uridine/fish oil/creatine/l-theanine | Low-carb, fasting, or aggressive deficit work blocks | Do not use it to mask under-eating or sleep debt. |
| Methylene Blue / mitochondrial lane | Complementary fuel logic: MB supports electron transport, C8/BHB provide alternate substrate | MB days during cut or fasted/low-calorie blocks | Keep MB interaction rules unchanged; C8/BHB do not make unsafe MB stacks safe. |
| SGLT2 / glucose-disposal lane | Can reduce perceived low-fuel feeling when glucose is being pushed down | Carefully planned cut blocks with [[Empagliflozin]] / metformin logic | Highest caution: SGLT2 + fasting + low carb + ketones can confuse DKA risk. Hydration, carbs, and symptoms matter. |
| Bulk / high-carb performance | Usually low value | Rarely needed unless appetite/digestion makes carbs hard | Carbs outperform ketone salts for glycolytic lifting. |

Default use:

- C8 MCT: start 5g, move toward 10g if GI is clean.
- BHB salts: 0.5 serving first; only increase if mineral load and GI are clean.
- Best slot: pre-workout or fasted/low-calorie work block.
- Avoid making it a daily bulk habit unless there is a written reason.
