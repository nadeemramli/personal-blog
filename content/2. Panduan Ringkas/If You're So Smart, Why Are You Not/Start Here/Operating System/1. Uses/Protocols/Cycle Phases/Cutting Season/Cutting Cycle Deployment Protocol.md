# Cutting Cycle Deployment Protocol

> [!abstract] Owns the long-cut calendar.
> [[Cut Protocol]] says what the cutting stack contains. [[Fat Loss Daily Schedule (Cut)]] says how the day is arranged. This note decides when the cut starts, when it escalates, when it refeds, when it diet-breaks, and when it stops.

> [!danger] Boundary
> This is a deployment governor, not a prescription. Thyroid drugs, clenbuterol, SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1/GIP/GCGR agonists, peptides, and stimulants require owner-protocol safety gates.

## Deployment Clock

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Planned longer cut, usually moving from high body-fat toward the mid-teen range. |
| Duration logic | Campaign length, not panic dieting. Use low/refeed/diet-break structure to preserve training. |
| Deployment logic | Start conservative. Escalate by body-fat range, adherence, bloodwork, BP/HR, sleep, and glucose response. |
| Stock math | Campaign demand plus reserve; keep final-shred reserve separate. |

## Entry Criteria

Start a long cut only when:

- The current body-fat estimate and timeline justify a longer campaign rather than a mini-cut.
- Baseline photos, bodyweight average, waist, calories, steps/cardio, and training plan are defined.
- Blood pressure, lipids, renal/hepatic markers, and glucose risks have owner-protocol review.
- The food plan is written before the drug escalation plan.

## Phase Structure

1. **Base phase:** calorie deficit, high protein, steps/cardio, training retention, pump/hydration support.
2. **Glucose phase:** add glucose tools only if [[Glucose & Insulin Sensitivity Protocol]] gates are met.
3. **Mitochondrial phase:** add lower-sympathetic metabolic tools when adherence is good but output needs support.
4. **Stimulant/thyroid phase:** reserved for later or harder segments with BP/HR/sleep guardrails.
5. **Diet break/refeed:** planned recovery windows, not emotional cheat days. Use them to restore training output and reduce fatigue.

## Escalation Rules

- Do not escalate because of one stalled week. Check adherence, water, sodium, sleep, constipation, and training stress first.
- Only add one new stressor at a time so side effects are traceable.
- Stimulants and thyroid are not combined with poor sleep, uncontrolled BP/HR, dehydration, or panic dieting.
- SGLT2 tools do not belong in dehydrating, ketogenic, illness, or diuretic-heavy windows.
- Hand off to [[Final Shred Deployment Protocol]] only if already lean and the risk/reward is written.

## Exit Criteria

End or diet-break when:

- The target BF/waist/photos are reached.
- Training performance, mood, sleep, libido, or injury risk deteriorates beyond the planned fatigue budget.
- BP/HR, lipids, renal/hepatic markers, glucose/ketone symptoms, or stimulant side effects breach stop rules.
- The cut has become adherence theater rather than measured fat loss.

## Monitoring

- Daily: bodyweight, steps/cardio, calories/protein, sleep, hunger, digestion, training readiness.
- Weekly: waist, photos, 7-day averages, performance trend, BP/HR trend, adherence review.
- Lab cadence: use [[Year-Round Shield]], [[Lipid Management Protocol]], [[Renal & Hepatic Support Protocol]], and [[Glucose & Insulin Sensitivity Protocol]].
- Schedule reference: [[Fat Loss Daily Schedule (Cut)]]

## Red Flags

Stop escalation and review for chest pain, fainting, severe palpitations, severe anxiety/insomnia, heat intolerance, tremor, unexplained shortness of breath, dehydration, ketone symptoms, severe GI distress, or rapid strength collapse.

## Stock Math

- Split the cut into phases and calculate each product by active phase days, not full campaign length.
- Keep stimulant/thyroid/final-shred stock separate from base cut stock.
- Count supportive stock: protein, electrolytes, glycerol, intra-workout, glucose strips if used, BP cuff batteries/consumables if tracked.
- Record reserve explicitly rather than hiding it in daily-dose assumptions.
