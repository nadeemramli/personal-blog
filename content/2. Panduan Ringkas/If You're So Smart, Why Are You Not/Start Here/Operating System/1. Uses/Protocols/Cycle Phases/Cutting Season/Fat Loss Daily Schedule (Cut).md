> [!abstract] The **execution clock** for the cut. [[Cut Protocol]] = *what to run*; [[Fat Loss Pathways (Theory)]] = *why it works*; **this note = when to take it.** Compounds are sequenced by half-life, receptor interaction, and circadian biology so the fat-mobilizers peak exactly when the body is primed to oxidize, and cortisol is down by the time you sleep on trenbolone.

> [!danger] Boundary & prerequisites
> Assumes the foundational shields are live — [[Glucose & Insulin Sensitivity Protocol]], [[Blood Pressure & Fluid Escalation Protocol]], [[Lipid Management Protocol]], [[Neuroprotection Protocol (Tren)]] — and current [[Bloodwork & Calculated Biomarkers|bloods]]. AAS/peptide *doses* live in [[Cut Protocol]] and [[Endocrine Design Rules]]; this note carries **timing** plus OTC/ancillary doses only. Read [Critical execution rules](#critical-execution-rules) before running.

## The fast: 16:8
| | Window |
|---|---|
| **Fast** | 20:00 → 12:00 (next day) |
| **Feed** | 12:00 → 20:00 |
| **Hard cut-off** | **20:00 sharp** — no calories or insulin-spiking agents after |

The fasted morning is the entire point: lipolysis (HSL) is gated by insulin, so the mobilization + GH window only works at floor-level insulin. Nothing caloric — including aminos or creamer in coffee — until the fast breaks at noon, *after* fasted cardio.

## Phase 1 — Fasted morning: mobilize, uncouple, oxidize (06:00–07:30)
Goal: open the fat-release faucet (GH/yohimbine), lock out glucocorticoid fat-retention (Oxandrolone), ignite the exercise-mimetics (MOTS-c / SLU-PP-332), and burn the freed FFAs through BAT + cardio.

**06:00 — the fasted spike (low-insulin zone)**

|---|---|
| **HGH** (full daily dose, Sub-Q) | Empty stomach → wave of lipolysis floods FFAs into blood. [[HGH (100iu-kit) — TCI\|product]] · dose in [[Cut Protocol]]. |
| **[[MOTS-C (100mg-box) — TCI\|MOTS-c]]** (Sub-Q) | Pairs with GH — accelerates glucose clearance into muscle + augments beta-oxidation. |
| **[[Oxandrolone (Anavar) (10mg×100tab) — kohohpharma\|Oxandrolone]]** — dose 1 | Glucocorticoid-receptor **antagonist**: blocks the cortisol spike the morning stimulants generate, preventing cortisol water-retention + protecting muscle during fasted cardio. |
| **[[SLU-PP-332 (0.5mg-ml ×30ml) — Uther\|SLU-PP-332]]** — dose 1 | Pan-ERR agonist → up-regulates mitochondrial-biogenesis transcription, switches cellular fuel preference to fat. |
| **Adrenergic stack** | [[Caffeine (200mg×250tab) — iHerb\|Caffeine]] + [[L-Tyrosine (500g) — iHerb\|L-Tyrosine]] + [[Coleus Forskohlii (400mg×60tab) — iHerb\|Coleus]] + [[Yohimbine (5mg×240tab) — iHerb\|Yohimbine]] + [[Mirabegron (50mg×30tab) — shopee-china\|Mirabegron]] 50 mg (**only dose of the day**). |

**06:15 — thermogenic trigger**
- **5–10 min cold shower / ice bath.** TRPM8 → BAT activation → UCP-1 uncoupling opens the heat **sink** for the FFAs just mobilized. This is the step that stops re-esterification.

**06:30–07:30 — fasted cardio (1 h)**
- Low-to-moderate steady-state. GH + yohimbine + mirabegron have mobilized; carnitine (saturated from yesterday's pre-workout) + cardarine route it to mitochondria → near-pure lipid fuel.
- **[[Cardarine (GW-501516) (10mg×50tab) — kohohpharma\|Cardarine]]** taken right before stepping on the machine. (Daily, consistent — never cardio-day-only; PPAR-δ is a steady-state gene effect.)

## Phase 3 — Feeding & volumizer window (12:00–20:00)

**12:00 — FIRST MEAL (fast-breaker)**
- High-protein whole food, **with real carbohydrate** — the diet is a *moderate/high-carb deficit*, not low-carb (low-carb under this androgen + cardio load is a muscle-loss recipe). Total intake is down for the deficit; carb *ratio* stays high to protect lean mass and training output.
- **[[Metformin-Empagliflozin FDC (500-5mg×300tab) — shopee-china\|Metformin/Empagliflozin]] — single daily dose (500:5 mg), with this meal.** Dosing the SGLT2 *with carbohydrate + the insulin response of a meal* is the core euglycemic-DKA mitigation — it spills the meal's glucose surplus while food on board keeps ketogenesis suppressed. **+1 L water.**
- **[[Retatrutide (60mg×10vial) — BFF-AMO\|Retatrutide]]** (E4D, Sub-Q) right before this meal on its scheduled days — GLP-1/GIP manage the meal's glucose; glucagon arm keeps hepatic lipid oxidation firing.

**16:00 — pre-workout: androgen, saturation, pump (training days only)**

## Phase 4 — Nighttime cut-off & cortisol shield

**19:30 — LAST MEAL**
- Final food of the day — protein + carbs, same moderate/high-carb logic.
- _(No second SGLT2 dose — see the once-daily change below. If overnight fasting glucose later proves stubbornly high on a single dose, a small second dose **with this meal** can be reconsidered, but never fasted.)_

**20:00 — HARD CUT-OFF** — water / non-caloric fluids only from here.

**21:30 — HPA-axis de-escalation**
- **[[Phosphatidylserine (100mg×120cap) — iHerb\|Phosphatidylserine]] 200 mg** *(wishlist — to source; 2 caps, occasional post-heavy-workout / pre-bed tool)* **or [[Ashwagandha (600mg×120tab) — iHerb\|Ashwagandha]]** (in stock) — de-escalate HPA stress after hard training or a stimulant-heavy day so the CNS can shift into recovery. Reserve 400 mg for peak stress weeks; do not make 400–600 mg the casual daily baseline. Run one, not both.
- **No Mirabegron here** — its full 50 mg sits in the 06:00 slot; the ~50 h half-life means a second dose would stack and disrupt sleep architecture + spill off Beta-3 selectivity.

**22:30 — Sleep.**

## Critical execution rules

> [!danger] 1 · SGLT2 hydration mandate — non-negotiable
> SGLT2 dumps glucose **and** water through the kidneys, on top of Cardarine + Yohimbine + Nebivolol all pulling volume — and empagliflozin's diuresis lasts well beyond the single daily dose. **Drink 4–5 L water daily.** Dehydration here = acute blood thickening + kidney stress. See [[Blood Pressure & Fluid Escalation Protocol]].

> [!warning] 2 · Euglycemic DKA — risk now managed, not gone
> Two design choices keep this low: **(a)** SGLT2 is a **single daily dose taken with the carb-containing first meal** (never fasted), so food + insulin are on board to suppress ketogenesis exactly when the drug spills glucose; **(b)** the diet is **moderate/high-carb**, not low-carb — adequate glycogen and insulin are the main thing standing between SGLT2 and euglycemic DKA. Residual caution: empagliflozin's effect outlasts the dose, so some glucosuria persists into the next fasted-cardio morning. Keep ketone strips on hand; nausea, fruity breath, air hunger, or abnormal fatigue → stop, test ketones (normal glucose does **not** rule it out), seek care. Governed by [[Glucose & Insulin Sensitivity Protocol]].

> [!warning] 3 · Fasted means fasted
> The 06:00 HGH (and the whole mobilization stack) depends on floor-level insulin. **Zero calories** — no aminos, no creamer — until cardio is fully done and the fast breaks at 12:00.

> [!note] 4 · Daily fasting-glucose log
> Glucometer **every morning before shots.** If Metformin + Empagliflozin are working, fasting glucose should hold **under ~90–95 mg/dL** despite the daily GH + tren load. Persistent >100 → escalate per [[Glucose & Insulin Sensitivity Protocol]] or pull GH.

> [!tip] 5 · The standing-still fat-burn loop
> Oxandrolone (cortisol blocked → no lean-mass melt, no sub-Q water) + SLU-PP-332 & MOTS-c (double exercise-mimetic → mitochondrial density ↑, lipid burn at rest) means the stack keeps oxidizing fat even at your desk. That loop is *why* the morning timing matters — you're setting the all-day metabolic tone in the first 90 minutes.
