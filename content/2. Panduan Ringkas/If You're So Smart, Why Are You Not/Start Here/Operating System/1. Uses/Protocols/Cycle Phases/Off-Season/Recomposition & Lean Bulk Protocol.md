# Recomposition & Lean Bulk Protocol

> [!abstract] One chemistry, two calorie modes.
> **Recompose** and **lean bulk** run the *same* compounds, support, and timing — the **only** dial that changes is calories. **Recomp eats at maintenance** (hold weight, slowly trade fat for muscle, sharpen partitioning); **lean bulk eats at a small surplus** (add muscle slowly, cap fat gain). Recomp is the **off-season → cut bridge**: it dries you out and restores insulin sensitivity so the cut starts from a cleaner base. Replaces the old scaffold *Lean Bulk Protocol*.

> [!info] Theory behind this protocol
> The mass mechanism — stimulus→mTORC1→MPS, the three muscle compartments, the myostatin + osmolality governors, and the GH/insulin Akt arm — lives in [[Muscle Building Pathways (Theory)]]. This note is the praxis.

> [!danger] Boundary
> Harm-reduction infrastructure, **not a prescription**. Test, EQ, Tren, Anadrol, Masteron, HGH/secretagogues, insulin-sensitivity drugs (Metformin/SGLT2), GLP-1/GIP/GCGR agonists (Retatrutide), ARBs, and β-blockers each require their owner-protocol safety gates and **clinician-reviewed bloodwork**. Every cycle start is gated on a panel ([[Bloodwork & Calculated Biomarkers]]). Deployment timing, entry/exit, and monitoring live in [[Recomposition & Lean Bulk Deployment Protocol]].

## The two modes — only calories change

| Mode | Calories | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recompose** | **Maintenance (±0)** | Hold bodyweight; slowly trade fat → muscle; restore insulin sensitivity and dryness | Bridge into a cut; consolidation block after a lean bulk; "hold and harden" windows |
| **Lean Bulk** | **Small surplus (+200–500 kcal, ≈0.25–0.5%/wk)** | Add muscle slowly, partition the surplus into tissue, cap fat ≤ ~15% | Off-season growth block |

Everything below — compounds, doses, guardrails, support — is **identical across both modes**. You only move the calorie dial.

### Calorie framework (scale to current weight)
- **Recomp:** sit at effective TDEE. At 91 kg that is ~2,100–2,300 base **+250 kcal** from 8k steps EOD → **~2,650**; scale down at lower bodyweight. Net zero across the week.
- **Lean bulk:** **+200–500 kcal** over that maintenance, targeting **~0.25–0.5%/wk** gain. If gain runs faster, it is fat → tighten, or hand to [[Off-Season Mini-Cut & Accelerator Tiers|mini-cut]].
- **Protein anchor:** ~200 g (≈2.2 g/kg) in both modes. Fats ≥45 g (joint/hormone support on Test). Carbs cycled around training.
- Live numbers per run: [[2026 Q4 — Lean Bulk 1]]. Goal-state calories: [[FFMI Trajectory & Calorie Targets]].

**Logic of the dose choices (as supplied):** 500 mg Test is a proven anabolic ceiling for a base with predictable aromatisation. EQ at ~half the Test dose gives the EPO/vascularity/endurance bump while acting as a *gentle* passive E₂ stabiliser rather than a sledgehammer (keep it low — its anti-estrogenic metabolites are individual). Masteron's high SHBG affinity frees up more of the 500 mg Test even at 100 mg, and its DHT-derived antagonism gives a SERM-like shield at the breast against the Anadrol/Tren estrogen+progesterone flare — without drying joints or wrecking lipids at the low dose. Keeping EQ + Mast low holds E₂ in the sweet spot (needed for HGH growth signals and neuro/vascular protection) and scales back the Test+EQ+Anadrol fluid/BP/viscosity load so the Metformin/Empa pair can manage intramuscular hydration without overloading the kidneys. The energy + protection layer (Methylene Blue, P5P, ARB, glucose guardrails) is what lets this fuller base run without bloating, prolactin gyno, or runaway BP.

> [!info] Tren + Anadrol synergy — and its short fuse
> **Anadrol** drives non-AR growth: cell hydration, glycogen, protein synthesis, extreme fullness and pumps. **Tren** binds the AR hard and *partitions* — it drives the Anadrol-fuelled calorie influx into muscle instead of fat, and adds CNS drive/aggression. Together: unparalleled raw strength and a dense, dry-yet-full look. **The trap:** Anadrol stimulates estrogen receptors directly (no aromatase) while Tren (19-nor) elevates progesterone/prolactin — both estrogenic *and* progestogenic pathways run hot, so gyno + water-retention risk is exponential unless ancillaries (P5P, SERM/dopamine cover) are dialled. **The cost:** Anadrol is C17-aa (liver + acute lipid hit), Tren raises BP, night sweats, wrecks sleep. **Restrict this pair to short blocks (≈4–6 wk)** even though the base runs 16–20 wk.

> [!warning] Hidden trap — EQ/Anadrol estrogen × MK-677/Tren prolactin
> EQ raises E₂ baseline (on top of 500 mg Test) and Anadrol stimulates estrogen receptors directly, **while** MK-677 (ghrelin-agonist) and Tren (19-nor) both spike prolactin. Elevated E₂ sensitises breast tissue; a simultaneous prolactin spike is a strong gyno + lethargy + soft-fluid trigger. **P5P (50–100 mg/day) is non-negotiable** for the whole run — keep a dopamine agonist / SERM on hand as backup.

## Metabolic guardrails — Metformin + Empagliflozin

> [!tip] Timing: take the 500/5 mg FDC tablet at **~2:00 PM with the first (largest, carb-heavy) meal** — not fasted.
> - **GI buffer:** Metformin on an empty stomach causes cramping/diarrhoea; the rice+protein meal buffers it.
> - **Carb-spike match:** Metformin suppresses hepatic gluconeogenesis + raises muscle insulin sensitivity (AMPK) — dropping it at 2 PM means it's fully active as the lunch carbs land, forcing them into muscle glycogen rather than fat.
> - **Pre-workout window:** Empagliflozin peaks ~1.5–2 h post-dose, Metformin ~2–3 h. Taken at 2 PM, by the 6:30 PM session you're at peak insulin sensitivity, glycogen-loaded, with the SGLT2 "safety valve" active under heavy weights.
> - **Avoid fasted (7 AM):** SGLT2 + fasted cardio = accelerated dehydration/electrolyte loss; and AMPK active during a deep fast can down-regulate mTOR and flatten muscle. You want AMPK active *with nutrients present*, not during a 7-hour fast.

> [!question] "Won't Metformin's AMPK blunt mTOR for my workout?"
> Theoretically yes — AMPK phosphorylates TSC2/Raptor and brakes mTORC1. **In this stack it's neutralised:** (1) Test/Tren/IGF-1 lock the hypertrophic switch ON and easily overpower a modest 500 mg AMPK signal; (2) AMPK massively up-regulates GLUT4 — by 6:30 PM muscles are glycogen-hyper-saturated, and that mechanical-tension/volume signal outweighs the microscopic dip in protein synthesis; (3) Metformin's plasma half-life is ~4–6 h, so the acute AMPK spike is already declining by the time you train and enter the growth window, while the loaded glycogen remains. Net: keeping insulin receptors pristine so your carbs reach muscle beats the minor mTOR blunting.

> [!caution] Hydration & hypoglycaemia (SGLT2 rules)
> Empagliflozin spills glucose (and water + sodium) through urine. **Scale up water aggressively 2 PM → bed**, and add a sodium/potassium electrolyte source to the intra/post-workout shake to prevent cramping and flat muscles. Training late (6:30–8:30 PM) on Anadrol/Tren + a glucose-disposal effect can crash blood sugar around 9:30 PM — keep rapid carbs in the post-workout meal to avoid cold sweats/shakiness/cognitive fog during the night work block.

> [!danger] Flags & conflicts — read before deploying
> 1. **Test 500 mg/wk conflicts with [[Endocrine Design Rules]] (Rule 2).** Your own zones put **350 = sweet spot**, **400–450 = lean-bulk base**, and **500 = "Danger-mid / blast ramp."** A recomp/lean-bulk is the *clean* phase — 500 sits high for it. Default lean-bulk per the rules is **Test 450 + EQ/Primo lateral**; treat 500 as a deliberate blast-ramp choice, gated on bloods.
> 2. **This stack breaks Rule 1 ("continuous Test + ONE rotating compound").** Test + EQ + Masteron + Anadrol + Tren is a multi-lateral blast-grade base, not a "clean" recomp — so this protocol is effectively a protected blast run at maintenance/small-surplus calories. Keep Tren ≤8 wk and Anadrol ≤6–8 wk per [[Endocrine Design Rules]] (Rule 3); route the oral/harsh on-off windows through [[Oral and Extreme Deployment Protocol]] and Tren neuro-cover through [[Neuroprotection Protocol (Tren)]].
> 3. **Not in catalog:** **Masteron (Drostanolone)** and **Anadrol (Oxymetholone)** aren't in your stock yet — **source them before running this**. In-inventory analogs if you need to start without them: **[[Proviron (25mg×50tab) — kohohpharma\|Proviron]]** covers Masteron's DHT/SHBG/anti-gyno role; **[[Oxandrolone (Anavar) (10mg×100tab) — kohohpharma\|Anavar]]** is your owned oral substitute for Anadrol.
> 4. **Proviron vs EQ:** if you sub Proviron for Masteron, per Rule 2b drop it when EQ is in the stack (no double aromatase competitor).
> 5. **Tren Ace 25–35 mg/wk** reads low for the acetate ester (often dosed per-pin/EOD); kept as supplied — confirm intended weekly total at build time.
> All doses **provisional until bloodwork confirms**.
