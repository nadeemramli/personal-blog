# Mini-Cut & Accelerator Tiers

> [!abstract] Pipeline 3 (Deployment) decision model. The [[Cut Protocol]] says *what* a cut stack contains; this note says *how hard to push it*, set by **tier of work = how fat or how lean you are.**
> The rule that prevents over-medicating: at high BF you do **less**, not more — a light mini-cut restores insulin sensitivity first; the heavy tools are earned by leanness, not spent on fat. Used by [[2026 Q3 Cut to 15%]].

> [!important] The mini-cut principle
> A mini-cut is **aggressive, brief (2–4 wks), and hyper-efficient at restoring insulin sensitivity without crashing training.** Tiers are *how much chemistry* you layer onto that, not how long you starve.

---

## Tier 1 — Light / "we're so fat" (entry point for high-BF cuts, e.g. 26%)

The default opener. Strip fat with lifestyle + glucose management before reaching for harsher tools.

- **Lifestyle:** +10k steps/day · deficit-or-maintenance kcal (hold or **raise** protein).
- **Thyroid:** [[Levothyroxine (T4) (50mcg×100tab) — shopee-china\|T4]] **100 mcg** + [[Cytolin (25mcg×100tab) — kohohpharma\|T3]] **25 mcg**.
- **Glucose clearing:** **SGLT2 inhibitor** (Empagliflozin / Ertugliflozin) + **Metformin** + [[Retatrutide (60mg×10vial) — BFF-AMO|Retatrutide]].
  - *Why:* Empagliflozin dumps glucose via urine; Metformin lifts peripheral muscle insulin sensitivity + cuts hepatic glucose output. Powerful for off-season mini-aggressive-cut glucose management.
- **Year-round "plus" already running:** [[Cardarine (GW-501516) (10mg×50tab) — kohohpharma\|Cardarine]] + injectable [[L-Carnitine (1000g) — Shopee|L-Carnitine]] (these are standing ancillaries, hence "plus").

> [!note] Off-season mini aggressive cut uses this same Tier 1 glucose stack (Metformin + SGLT2 + Retatrutide) — it's the bridge between bulk blocks when 0.5%/wk gain turns to fat. See [[Recomposition & Lean Bulk Protocol|Lean Bulk]].

## Tier 2 — Medium (as you approach lean, ~16–17% and below)

Layer mitochondrial/metabolic optimizers **onto** the Tier 1 base.

- [[SLU-PP-332 (0.5mg-ml ×30ml) — Uther|SLU-PP-332]] — ERR agonist; mimics endurance-exercise cellular signaling.
- [[MOTS-C (100mg-box) — TCI|MOTS-C]] — mitochondrial-derived peptide; metabolic flexibility, prevents diet-induced insulin resistance. *(INDEXA-funded)*
- **Methylene Blue** — electron cycler in the ETC; ↑ATP, clears metabolic waste under oxidative stress.

---

## Tier 3 — Heavy / "The Stack" (ONLY post-lean-bulk, getting truly shredded)

> [!warning] Not a high-BF tool. Trigger condition: you've **lean-bulked 20+ weeks without much fat gain** and want to get really shredded for a while before bulking back. Never deployed to dig out of high body fat.

- Blood-glucose stack (Tier 1 glucose) — **first 2–4 weeks only**.
- **Full fasted AM routine:** [[Yohimbine (5mg×240tab) — iHerb|Yohimbine]] + [[Caffeine (200mg×250tab) — iHerb|Caffeine]] + [[L-Tyrosine (500g) — iHerb\|Tyrosine]] + [[Creatine Monohydrate (1000g) — Shopee\|Creatine]] + [[Coleus Forskohlii (400mg×60tab) — iHerb|Coleus Forskohlii]].
- [[Cardarine (GW-501516) (10mg×50tab) — kohohpharma\|Cardarine]] · [[L-Carnitine (1000g) — Shopee|L-Carnitine]] · [[MOTS-C (100mg-box) — TCI|MOTS-C]] · [[SLU-PP-332 (0.5mg-ml ×30ml) — Uther|SLU-PP-332]] · Methylene Blue.
- **Thyroid Stack + [[HGH (100iu-kit) — TCI\|HGH]]**.
- [[Clenbuterol (40mcg×100tab) — kohohpharma|Clenbuterol]] **40 mcg fasted ×4/day**.

## The tier decision (P3 trigger)

```
how lean are you?
  >~20% (fat)      -> TIER 1   (lifestyle + glucose; restore sensitivity first)
  ~16-17%          -> TIER 1 -> TIER 2  (add mito/metabolic optimizers)
  already lean,     
  post 20wk bulk   -> TIER 3   (the full shred stack)  [rare]
```

> [!note] Cross-pipeline note
> Tier choice is downstream of [[Cut Protocol\|P2]] (BF% sets the season and the tier). [[Year-Round Shield\|P4]] stays constant underneath every tier and tightens as Tier 2/3 add cardiovascular load. Endocrine ([[Endocrine Design Rules\|P1]]) is held flat during a cut unless bloods force a change.
