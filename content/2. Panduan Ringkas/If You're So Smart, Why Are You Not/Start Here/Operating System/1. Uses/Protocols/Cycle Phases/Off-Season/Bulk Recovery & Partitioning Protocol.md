# Bulk Recovery & Partitioning Protocol

> [!abstract] Owns the recovery and partitioning layer inside a bulk.
> This is where sleep-timed GH tools, appetite tools, glucose gates, and food execution are managed so a surplus becomes productive tissue instead of mostly fat/water.

> [!info] Theory: [[Muscle Building Pathways (Theory)]] explains *why* partitioning + the GH/insulin Akt arm matter — the mTORC1 chain and the three muscle compartments this protocol is trying to fill.

> [!danger] Boundary
> HGH, GH secretagogues, insulin, GLP-1/GIP/GCGR agonists, and glucose drugs require clinician review and owner-protocol safety gates. This note is a deployment governor.

## Deployment Clock

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Growth block where recovery, sleep, appetite, food tolerance, or glucose tolerance is the limiting factor. |
| Duration logic | Runs with the growth block. It is not automatically year-round. |
| Deployment logic | Night/sleep-timed GH tools live here; glucose tools enter only if food/GH markers justify them. |
| Stock math | Block demand, often paired with endocrine calendar and food budget. |

## Entry Criteria

Use this protocol when:

- Calories are high enough that recovery, digestion, appetite, or sleep timing has become the bottleneck.
- Weight gain is intended and measured, not accidental overeating.
- Training progression, protein, carbs, steps, and sleep are logged.
- Fasting/post-meal glucose and BP/HR are known before GH/glucose escalation.

## Decision Framework

1. **Food first.** No surplus, no bulk protocol. Food quality and training progression are the foundation.
2. **Sleep before chemistry.** If sleep is poor, fix schedule, caffeine timing, apnea risk, and stress before adding sleep/GH tools.
3. **MK-677 vs HGH/CJC lane.** These are recovery/sleep/GH-axis decisions, not automatic bulk decorations. Before-sleep timing belongs here when the goal is appetite/recovery/sleep architecture.
4. **Glucose gate.** If GH/MK-677/food pushes fasting glucose, post-meal glucose, edema, or lethargy, hand off to [[Glucose & Insulin Sensitivity Protocol]].
5. **Retatrutide constraint.** In a bulk, retatrutide is a partitioning/appetite-control tool only if it does not sabotage calorie intake, protein intake, or training.
6. **Insulin hard gate.** [[Humalog insulin lispro (1500IU) — anabolicpharmacist]] is clinician-only/high-risk. It is not operational in this protocol without a separate medical plan, glucose monitoring, hypoglycemia rescue plan, carb plan, and supervision.

## Exit Criteria

Exit or downshift when:

- Weight gain exceeds target and waist rises faster than performance.
- Fasting/post-meal glucose worsens.
- Edema, numbness/tingling, BP rise, snoring/apnea signs, or lethargy appears.
- Appetite is either too high to control or too suppressed to hit the surplus.
- The bulk should hand off to [[Off-Season Mini-Cut Protocol]].

## Monitoring

- Daily: bodyweight, appetite, sleep quality, digestion, training readiness, edema.
- Weekly: waist, photos, lifts, average calories/protein/carbs, steps.
- Glucose: fasting and selected post-meal checks when GH/MK-677, retatrutide, or glucose tools are active.
- Safety: BP/HR, CMP/electrolytes, renal markers, lipids, and symptoms through owner protocols.

## Red Flags

Stop and review for hypoglycemia symptoms, severe edema, shortness of breath, chest pain, uncontrolled BP, rapid glucose deterioration, severe GI intolerance, sleep apnea signs, numbness/tingling that worsens, or injection-site/systemic reaction.

## Stock Math

- GH/secretagogue tools: `active_block_days x planned_units`; separate INDEXA-funded stock from personal stock.
- Retatrutide: count total mg, titration waste, and appetite effect.
- Glucose tools: only count days where glucose gate is active.
- Food inventory: protein, intra-workout, eggs, carbs, electrolytes, and digestion supports count as part of the block.
- Insulin lane: no stock projection unless the separate medical/supervised plan exists.
