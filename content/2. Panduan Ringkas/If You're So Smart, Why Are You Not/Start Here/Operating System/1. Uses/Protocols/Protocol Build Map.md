# Protocol Build Map

> [!abstract] This is the protocol construction layer between [[Product List]] and the live cycle notes.
> Product notes say **what exists**. Protocol notes say **when/why it is used**. Cycle notes say **when it is actually deployed**. Ledger notes say **how much to buy**.

> [!danger] Boundary
> This map is harm-reduction infrastructure, not a prescription. For prescription drugs, PEDs, peptides, insulin, thyroid drugs, diuretics, SERMs, and stimulants, the protocol note must contain entry criteria, exit criteria, monitoring, red flags, and clinician-review assumptions before it is treated as operational.

## Operating Rule

Each product should carry a `protocols` frontmatter list. That list answers: "which protocol note owns the decision to use this?"

Do not duplicate product logic across series:

- Hair and beard products are owned by [[Part 2.0 - The Stack and the Routine]] and [[Part 3.0 - What Actually Matters For The Beard]].
- Skin products are owned by [[Part 2.0 - The Face Stack and the Routine]], [[Part 2.1 - The Internal Stack and the Procedures]], and [[Part 3.0 - Body, Neck and Hands]].
- Cognitive products are owned by [[Part 4.1 - The Natural Stack]], [[Part 4.2 - The Enhanced Stack]], and [[Part 4.3 - The Hybrid Stack]].
- Sleep products are owned by [[Part 3.0 - Natural and OTC Support]] and [[Part 3.1 - The Pharmacological Reset]].

## Deployment Refinement

Cutting is not one protocol. It is one family with different deployment clocks.

| Protocol | Trigger | Duration logic | Deployment logic | Stock math |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Off-Season Mini-Cut Protocol]] | Bulk is drifting: weight gain > target, waist/BF rising, digestion or insulin sensitivity worsening | Short, aggressive 2-4 week reset | Mostly Tier 1 glucose/lifestyle; avoid burning the harsh tools while still high-BF | Small pulse demand, repeated during long off-seasons |
| [[Cutting Cycle Deployment Protocol]] | Planned 8-20 week cut, e.g. moving from high BF toward the 15-18% range | Longer campaign with low/refeed/diet-break structure | Starts conservative, escalates by BF and bloodwork; tools are staged, not all started day 1 | Campaign demand plus reserve |
| [[Final Shred Deployment Protocol]] | Already lean after productive bulk; short push to very sharp condition | Short terminal block | Tier 2 to Tier 3 only when earned; highest monitoring burden | Rare, high-intensity pulse demand |
| [[Bulk Recovery & Partitioning Protocol]] | Growth block / lean bulk where recovery, sleep, appetite, partitioning, and glucose tolerance are the constraints | Runs with the growth block, not automatically year-round | MK-677 or HGH/CJC-style sleep/GH tools live here; glucose tools may enter if GH/food pushes markers | Block demand, often paired with endocrine calendar |

This is the key adjustment: the **same compound can be a different decision** depending on phase. Metformin + SGLT2 can be an off-season insulin-sensitivity reset, an early long-cut glucose tool, or absent from final-shred if insulin sensitivity is already restored.
