# Neuroprotection Protocol (Tren)

> [!danger] Boundary — read first
> This is **harm-reduction documentation, not a prescription**, and the evidence base is **mechanistic + anecdotal, not settled human clinical data**. Trenbolone neurotoxicity in humans is not well-characterised; Memantine neuroprotection here is **extrapolated** from NMDA/excitotoxicity pharmacology. The first-line neuroprotection is always **keeping Tren low and short** (≤70mg/wk, ≤8wk) — Memantine is an adjunct, not a license to push Tren. Run past a clinician.

## Why (rationale)

Trenbolone is a 19-nor **progestin + potent androgen** associated anecdotally with "tren brain" — insomnia, anxiety, irritability/aggression, low mood, night sweats, and subjective cognitive fog. Mechanistically the concern is **glutamatergic excitotoxicity + neuroinflammation**: excessive tonic NMDA-receptor activation drives Ca²⁺ overload and neuronal stress.

**Memantine** is an **uncompetitive, low-affinity NMDA antagonist**: it blocks the *excitotoxic tonic* over-activation while sparing *physiological phasic* signalling — i.e. it protects without blunting learning/memory the way a strong NMDA blocker (ketamine, DXM) would. Its **long half-life (~60–100 h)** means **every-other-day or every-3rd-day dosing maintains steady protective levels** — and infrequent low dosing is exactly what avoids over-blocking NMDA.

## Protocol

> [!info] Active ONLY while Trenbolone is running (the cut's ≤8-wk Tren block). Off Tren → off this.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Agent** | [[Memantine Momenta (5mg×10tab) — Equicare Ventures\|Memantine]] (Momenta generic = cheapest, RM0.041/mg) |
| **Dose** | **5 mg start**, EOD or E3D; may go **10 mg** EOD/E3D if well-tolerated |
| **Frequency** | **Every other day (EOD)** or **every 3rd day (E3D)** — the ~60–100h half-life sustains levels between doses |
| **Window** | runs with the Tren block only (this cut: ≤8 wks, alongside [[Trenbolone Acetate Acro (100mg-ml ×10ml) — beligaspharmacy Intl\|Tren]]) |
| **Timing** | evening-flexible; mild sedation/dizziness possible → not pre-driving until you know your response |

> [!note] Start at 5 mg E3D, assess (memantine can cause dizziness/sedation/mild confusion if pushed). Step to EOD or 10 mg only if clean. Do **not** chase the Alzheimer's 20mg target — that's a different goal; neuroprotective adjunct dosing is deliberately low.

## Adjuncts you already own (stack these year-round, not just on Tren)
These hit the same excitotoxicity/neuroinflammation axis and are safe daily:
- [[Magnesium Glycinate (210mg×240tab) — iHerb\|Magnesium Glycinate]] — Mg²⁺ is the **natural voltage-dependent NMDA channel block**; synergistic with memantine.
- [[NAC (600mg×180tab) — iHerb\|NAC]] — glutathione precursor + modulates the cystine–glutamate antiporter (lowers excess synaptic glutamate).
- [[Fish Oil (Omega-3) (1100mg×240softgel) — iHerb\|Omega-3 (DHA)]] — membrane integrity + anti-neuroinflammatory.
- [[L-Theanine (200mg×240tab) — iHerb\|L-Theanine]] — glutamate/GABA balance, calms the "tren brain" edge.
- [[Creatine Monohydrate (1000g) — Shopee\|Creatine]] — brain bioenergetic buffer (neuroprotective under metabolic stress).
- [[Taurine (1000mg×100cap) — iHerb\|Taurine]] — inhibitory neuromodulator (GABA/glycine-ergic), calms excitotoxicity + BP-supportive. 1–3g/day.

> [!warning] Do NOT double up NMDA antagonists. Memantine + **dextromethorphan** (cough syrup), ketamine, or other NMDA blockers = additive blockade (dissociation, confusion). Also: [[Glycine (1000mg×100tab) — iHerb\|Glycine]] is an NMDA **co-agonist** (potentiates the receptor) — fine for sleep, just don't think of it as neuroprotective here.

## Monitoring
- Track the "tren brain" markers in the log: **sleep quality, mood, anxiety, aggression, cognitive clarity**. If they spike, the answer is **drop Tren dose/duration first**, not more memantine.
- Memantine is renally cleared — your hydration (already ≥4–5 L on the cut) and renal panel cover this.
