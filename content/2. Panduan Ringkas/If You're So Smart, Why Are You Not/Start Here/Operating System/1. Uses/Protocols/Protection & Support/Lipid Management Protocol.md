# Lipid Management Protocol

> [!abstract] Owns the lipid layer for enhanced phases: statin base, ezetimibe add-on, fish oil support, oral-steroid damage control, and lab timing.
> Product owners include [[Rosuvastatin (10mg×28tab) — shopee-china]], [[Ezetimibe (10mg×10tab) — indiamart]], [[Ezetimibe Zetiheal (10mg×10tab) — Equicare Ventures]], [[Ezetimibe Ezedoc (10mg×15tab) — Equicare Ventures]], [[Fish Oil (Omega-3) (1100mg×240softgel) — iHerb]], [[NAC (600mg×180tab) — iHerb]], [[CoQ10 (400mg×180tab) — iHerb]], and [[Aspirin (100mg×60tab) — shopee-china]].

> [!danger] Boundary
> This is a risk-control protocol, not a license to run harsher compounds. Statins, ezetimibe, aspirin, and oral AAS windows require clinician review when used as medical risk management.

## What This Protocol Owns

- Baseline and follow-up lipid labs during blast, cruise, cut, and oral windows.
- Deciding when [[Ezetimibe (10mg×10tab) — indiamart]] or alternate ezetimibe offers become add-ons rather than wishlist clutter.
- Separating lipid control from liver support: liver enzymes route to [[Renal & Hepatic Support Protocol]], but statin/ezetimibe safety sits here.
- Oral-steroid risk response for [[Oxandrolone (Anavar) (10mg×100tab) — kohohpharma]] and other harsh/time-boxed tools in [[Oral and Extreme Deployment Protocol]].

## Entry Criteria

Use this protocol when:

- A blast, oral AAS phase, tren phase, high-androgen phase, or final-shred phase is planned.
- LDL-C, non-HDL-C, ApoB, triglycerides, HDL-C, or hs-CRP are outside the agreed risk range.
- A lipid medication or aspirin is being added, paused, or compared by stock math.
- Muscle pain, dark urine, unexplained weakness, or liver-enzyme drift appears while lipid drugs are active.

## Required Baseline

- Lipid panel with LDL-C, HDL-C, triglycerides, non-HDL-C.
- ApoB if available; Lp(a) once if never measured.
- ALT/AST, bilirubin, creatinine/eGFR, CK only if muscle symptoms or high-risk context.
- BP/HR and hematocrit because vascular risk is not only cholesterol.

## Decision Ladder

1. **Remove avoidable damage.** Time-box or remove oral AAS, uncontrolled thyroid/stimulant abuse, poor sleep, alcohol, and dirty surplus before adding complexity.
2. **Diet floor.** Soluble fiber, leaner food choices, calorie control, and omega-3 intake are the base layer.
3. **Statin lane.** [[Rosuvastatin (10mg×28tab) — shopee-china]] is the primary LDL/ApoB lever when the medical lane is justified.
4. **Ezetimibe add-on.** Ezetimibe belongs when LDL/ApoB remains above target or oral-steroid damage is expected to be severe. It is an add-on/comparison lane, not a separate protocol.
5. **Fish oil support.** [[Fish Oil (Omega-3) (1100mg×240softgel) — iHerb]] sits in the support layer, especially when triglycerides or inflammatory tone are the issue.
6. **CoQ10/NAC support.** [[CoQ10 (400mg×180tab) — iHerb]] and [[NAC (600mg×180tab) — iHerb]] are support tools; they do not replace lipid labs.
7. **Aspirin gate.** [[Aspirin (100mg×60tab) — shopee-china]] is not a generic "health" supplement. It requires bleeding-risk review, BP control, and a reason.

## Oral-Steroid Rule

If an oral window is being considered, the lipid plan must be written before the oral starts:

- Baseline lipid panel and liver markers.
- Planned duration and stop condition in [[Oral and Extreme Deployment Protocol]].
- Follow-up lipid panel after the window.
- A decision on whether the oral is worth the HDL/LDL/ApoB cost.

## Monitoring

- **Every 8-12 weeks during active enhanced phases:** lipid panel, ApoB if available, ALT/AST.
- **After oral windows:** repeat lipids after the planned washout interval used by the system.
- **Symptom-triggered:** CK and medical review if severe muscle pain, weakness, dark urine, fever, or malaise appears.
- **Cross-check:** BP via [[Blood Pressure & Fluid Escalation Protocol]], hepatic markers via [[Renal & Hepatic Support Protocol]].

## Red Flags

Stop and seek review for dark urine with muscle pain, severe unexplained weakness, jaundice, severe abdominal pain, persistent liver-enzyme elevation, unusual bleeding/bruising on aspirin, or chest pain/neurological symptoms.

## Stock Math

- **Rosuvastatin:** `daily_units x 365 x years_covered`; it is a year-round base if active.
- **Ezetimibe:** `daily_units x add_on_days`; track each vendor offer by total tablets and landed cost.
- **Fish oil:** project by softgels/day and EPA/DHA target if recorded; do not compare only by capsule count.
- **Aspirin:** project separately because the decision is risk-based, not automatically tied to lipid drug use.

## Reference Anchors
