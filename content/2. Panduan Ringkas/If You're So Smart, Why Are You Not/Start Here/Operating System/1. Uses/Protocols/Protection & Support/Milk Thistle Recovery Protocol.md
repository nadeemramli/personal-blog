# Milk Thistle Recovery Protocol

> [!abstract] Owns the milk thistle / silymarin layer.
> Product owner: [[Silymarin Complex (300mg×360cap) — iHerb]]. This protocol is a recovery/off-cycle protocol, not an active oral-steroid support protocol.

> [!danger] Boundary
> During active 17-alpha-alkylated oral AAS windows, do **not** use milk thistle as the primary liver support. Active oral windows are owned by [[Oral and Extreme Deployment Protocol]] and [[Renal & Hepatic Support Protocol]], with [[TUDCA (250mg×60tab) — iHerb|TUDCA]] + [[NAC (600mg×180tab) — iHerb|NAC]] as the main support tools.

## Core Logic

Milk thistle is only useful if the product actually delivers silymarin/silybin. Raw or unstandardized milk thistle powder is low-confidence because oral silymarin has poor absorption. The quality floor is:

- standardized milk thistle extract at **70-80% silymarin**, or
- higher-tier **silybin/phospholipid phytosome** products when budget and need justify it.

The chosen source, [[Silymarin Complex (300mg×360cap) — iHerb]], meets the basic quality floor: **300mg milk thistle extract standardized to 80% silymarin per capsule**. It is good enough for recovery use, but it is **not** a Siliphos/phytosome-grade absorption upgrade.

## Deployment Rule

| Phase | Use Milk Thistle? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Active oral AAS / cholestatic stress window | **No** | Keep support focused on bile-flow/glutathione tools: TUDCA + NAC. Do not add extra hepatobiliary traffic during the insult. |
| Immediate post-oral recovery | **Yes** | Use as hepatocyte recovery / antioxidant support after the oral compound has stopped. |
| Off-cycle liver marker recovery | **Yes, if markers/symptoms justify** | Useful when ALT/AST/GGT are elevated or after a planned liver-taxing block. |
| Year-round healthy baseline | **Usually no** | Not needed as a casual daily supplement if labs are normal and no liver stressor is active. |

## Dosing

| Tier | Dose | Timing | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Standard recovery | 300mg/day | 1 capsule with a fat-containing meal | Default for post-oral recovery. |
| Higher recovery | 600mg/day | 1 cap twice daily with meals | Short block if liver markers are elevated or recovery need is higher. |
| Upgrade tier | Silybin-phospholipid / phytosome product | With meals | Use only if a future product is specifically bought for higher absorption. |

Run length:

- **2-4 weeks** after an oral/hepatotoxic window.
- Extend only if labs are improving but not normalized and the stressor has stopped.
- Do not use a supplement to justify continuing the liver stressor.

## What It Does Not Replace

- [[TUDCA (250mg×60tab) — iHerb|TUDCA]] during oral-heavy blocks.
- [[NAC (600mg×180tab) — iHerb|NAC]] / glutathione support.
- CMP/liver-marker monitoring.
- Stopping the hepatotoxic compound when red flags appear.

| Active target | Silymarin/silybin amount per serving is clear |

| Caution | Piperine-containing formulas can change medication exposure |

## Safety / Interaction Notes

- Avoid or use clinician review with gallbladder/bile duct obstruction concerns.
- Use caution with diabetes medications, anticoagulants/antiplatelets, and drugs heavily metabolized by liver pathways.
- Watch for GI upset, bloating, nausea, allergic reactions, or issues in people allergic to Asteraceae/Compositae plants such as ragweed, chrysanthemum, marigold, or daisy.
- Piperine in the chosen formula is useful for absorption but increases interaction complexity.

## Monitoring

Before and after recovery block:

- ALT, AST, GGT, ALP, bilirubin.
- Symptoms: fatigue, dark urine, pale stool, itching, right-upper-quadrant pain, nausea.
- Context: oral AAS stop date, alcohol, acetaminophen, new supplements/medications, training damage/CK if AST is disproportionately high.
