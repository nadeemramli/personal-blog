# Year-Round Shield

> [!abstract] Pipeline 4 (Health & Longevity). The **constant floor** — it never cycles. Runs through every blast, cut, and cruise to neutralize the inherent side effects of the performance compounds.
> Arguably the most important pipeline: the other three are only sustainable because this one holds. **Daily morning dosing is the anchor.** Already present as individual product notes; this assembles them into one named protocol.

> [!important] Watch metrics (the P4 triggers)
> **Blood pressure `<120/80`** (daily reading) · **lipid panel** (every ~8 wks → [[Bloodwork & Calculated Biomarkers]]). If either drifts, the Shield tightens — it does not loosen.

## The standing stack (daily AM anchor)

| Compound | Job |
|---|---|
| [[Rosuvastatin (10mg×28tab) — shopee-china|Rosuvastatin]] | Inhibits HMG-CoA reductase → forces LDL down, protects endothelium from oral-steroid HDL crush (e.g. [[Oxandrolone (Anavar) (10mg×100tab) — kohohpharma\|Anavar]]) |
| [[Irbesartan (150mg×120tab) — shopee-china|Irbesartan]] (or Telmisartan) — ARB | Gold-standard BP med for enhanced athletes; protects kidneys + against LV hypertrophy from high blood volume / EQ / [[MK-677 (10mg×50tab) — kohohpharma|MK-677]] |
| [[Tadalafil (10mg×100tab) — shopee-china|Tadalafil]] (low daily) | PDE5 inhibitor → ↑nitric oxide, ↓BP, better pumps, endothelial protection |
| [[Retatrutide (60mg×10vial) — BFF-AMO|Retatrutide]] (GLP-1/GIP/GCGR) | Appetite suppression, insulin sensitivity, metabolic rate during fat loss *(INDEXA-funded)* |
| [[L-Carnitine (1000g) — Shopee|L-Carnitine]] (daily loaded) | ↑androgen-receptor density; transports long-chain fats into mitochondria |
| [[Aspirin (100mg×60tab) — shopee-china|Aspirin]] (baby dose, 100mg) | Irreversibly inhibits platelet COX-1 → blocks thromboxane → ↓clot risk / blood viscosity for each platelet's 7–10 day life |
| [[Nebivolol (2.5mg×100tab) — Jigar Healthcare|Nebivolol]] (2.5mg, low) | β1-selective blocker w/ NO-vasodilation — resting HR + BP control (esp. against stimulant fat-loss load). Watch for bradycardia; blunts clen/yohimbine thermogenesis |
| [[Ezetimibe (10mg×10tab) — indiamart|Ezetimibe]] (10mg) | Cholesterol-absorption inhibitor — LDL add-on to [[Rosuvastatin (10mg×28tab) — shopee-china|Rosuvastatin]] when statin alone doesn't hit target (orals crush HDL) |

> [!note] Why these don't cycle
> Platelets lack a nucleus, so aspirin's COX-1 block lasts the platelet's whole life — consistency *is* the mechanism. Same logic for statin/ARB/PDE5: endothelial and renal protection is a steady-state job, not a phase.
