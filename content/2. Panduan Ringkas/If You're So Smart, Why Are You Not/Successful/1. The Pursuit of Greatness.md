What is greatness? and why do we need to pursuit it?

Success is a manifestation of greatness, most parents want their child to be successful, but rarely we see and heard parents pushing their child to be great.

Great has been a topic that sounds a bit narcissistic,  it sounds far-fetched and too delusional, not grounded to reality. It's almost sounding like people who pursuing greatness are only people with big dreams, with lucks and privileges. But pursuing success don't sound far-fetching instead, more grounding than great?

I beg to think differently cause I believe pursuing greatness are much achievable than success itself. Success requires an external factor that beyond our control, it requires luck. Great itself  is dependent on the person definition of great, and is completely in control.

Referring to Xenophon's Memoralibilia for Socrates, in Practical Virtue, Xenophon wrote that Socrates pushes people to be good at what they do—whether it’s running a household or leading an army. Virtue isn’t just moral; it’s competence. This is interesting to me cause I was thinking that, is doing the work at most competence will be consider great, or achieving the outcome as per our expectation is consider great?

Let's break it down how Great, Success and Competence/Virtue, plays in modern reality.

Music, Rick Rubin. 

Standard, Success  = Impact/Magnitude of the Work, Competency, Challenging Status Quo (Original, Innovation).  

Standard = Exposure, Taste


A theme here that I want to focus is that,

Being able to live a little further than what the reality is
Being able to vision how it supposed to be
Build up a Standard that disqualify and qualify the work
Work Ethics to meet up the Standard - Virtue

I believe pursuing greatness is all the above. Being able to shape the world the way that you want it to be according to your life's potential.
