Part 1.2 left one thread hanging deliberately. Financial Wealth's question (*what is your definition of enough?*) is the most important on the entire scoreboard, and it is the one you cannot answer with a number. This article is how you answer it, and it is the destination the whole series has been pointing at. It is an extension of Financial Wealth, not a separate idea: the financial coordinate from [[Wealth Part 2.0 - Financial Freedom Is a Coordinate|Part 2.0]] is the tool that locates "enough," and a located "enough" is what releases the capacity the other four wealths have been waiting on. This is the keystone. Pull it and the whole board holds.

## Enough is not a number

Ask most people what "enough" is and they name a figure: a salary, a net worth, a FIRE number. The figure never holds, because the moment they approach it, the figure moves. This is the treadmill from Part 1.2, and it is not a discipline problem. It is a *definition* problem. A target that is a single number gives expectations nothing to push against, so they expand to fill whatever you earn.

==Enough defined as a number is a target that runs away from you. Enough defined as a coordinate is a position you can actually stand on.==

## Freedom is a coordinate

This is the framework from [[Wealth Part 2.0 - Financial Freedom Is a Coordinate|Part 2.0]], and it is the engine of this whole article, so it is worth stating cleanly. Freedom is not one number on one axis. It is a position on two:

- **What you are protecting:** the layers of your life that actually matter to you, from the non-negotiable floor up. In the framework these are the four layers of spending: the **Shield** (essentials), the **Engine** (what maintains you as the primary asset: health, training, the things you genuinely live for), the **Catalyst** (your working capital), and the **Multiplier** (long-term compounding bets).
- **For how long you can protect it:** your runway, measured in the stages from paycheck-to-paycheck up through a year or more of your real life fully funded.

Plot yourself on those two axes and you have a *coordinate*, not a wish. The full mechanics (the layers, the runway stages, the worked examples, and the compounding engine under the Multiplier) live in [[Wealth Part 2.0 - Financial Freedom Is a Coordinate|Part 2.0]] and [[Wealth Part 2.1 - The Beauty of Compounding|Part 2.1]]; read them as the companions to this article. What matters here is what the coordinate lets you do.

## Reading enough off the coordinate

"Enough" is simply a specific coordinate: the point at which the layers you actually live for are funded, and protected for a runway long enough that a shock (a layoff, a bad year) becomes an inconvenience rather than an existential event. That is it. It is personal, because the layers are yours, and it is *bounded*, because a coordinate does not run away the way a number does.

Notice what this does to the treadmill. Once the layers that matter are secured for a real runway, additional income stops being "need" and becomes genuine surplus. (This is exactly the surplus [[Wealth Part 2.1 - The Beauty of Compounding|Part 2.1]] unlocks once the Compounding Baseline is hit.) You have located the edge of enough, and everything past it is optional rather than compulsory. The chase ends not because you stopped earning but because you finally drew the line that a single number could never draw.

> [!important] The definition
> Enough is the coordinate where what you actually live for is funded and protected for long enough that a financial shock is an inconvenience, not a catastrophe. It is bounded, personal, and reachable, which is exactly what a runaway number is not.

## Why this is the keystone of the scoreboard

Here is why this article closes the series rather than sitting inside Financial Wealth as a footnote. An *undefined* enough gives Financial Wealth an infinite claim on your three scarcest inputs: time, attention, and money. If there is no ceiling on the financial number, money will always have a reason to take the next hour, the next ounce of focus, the next ringgit, and the other four wealths get whatever is left, which over a life is close to nothing. That is the mechanism by which a single-number scoreboard quietly bankrupts a life even as the number goes up.

Defining enough does the opposite. It caps the one wealth that would otherwise consume the others, and the surplus it frees is real and redeployable. ==A defined "enough" is not a limit on your life; it is the mechanism that funds the rest of it.== This is precisely what statement 5 of the Financial quiz was pointing at all along: *using financial wealth as a tool to build the other types of wealth.* You cannot use money as a tool for Time, Social, Mental, and Physical wealth until you have first decided how much of it is enough. Financial coordination is what makes that decision possible, which is why it is the keystone of the entire board.

## The redeployment

So the operating move is concrete. Locate your coordinate (which layers, what runway). Mark where "enough" sits. Then take the capacity that sits *beyond* enough (the surplus hours, attention, and money that you are no longer compelled to throw at a runaway number) and deliberately redeploy it into whichever wealth your Part 1.2 scores said was lowest.

- Surplus *money* buys back time (the [[Wealth Part 1.2 - The Five Wealths|Time Wealth]] buffers), or funds the gym and the food (Physical), or the trip with the people who matter (Social).
- Surplus *attention* goes to the relationships and the purpose that the chase was crowding out.
- Surplus *time* is the raw material every other wealth is built from.

Then re-score in 90 days and read whether the redeployment moved the number you aimed at. That short loop (locate the coordinate, define enough, redeploy the surplus into your lowest wealth, re-score) is the whole operating system. It is not five-way juggling. It is one disciplined act (declaring enough on the wealth that would otherwise eat the rest) repeated as your life changes.

Do that, and you stop waiting to arrive financially before you are allowed to live. You define the coordinate that counts as arrived, and then you spend the rest on the four wealths that were the point all along.

## Part 2.2 Takeaways

> [!check] Key concepts to internalize
>
> - **Enough is a coordinate, not a number:** A number runs away from you; a coordinate is a position you can stand on.
> - **Two axes:** What you protect (the four layers) and for how long (runway). Plot both and you have a real coordinate.
> - **Enough, defined:** The point where what you live for is funded and protected long enough that a shock is an inconvenience, not a catastrophe.
> - **The keystone:** An undefined enough gives Financial Wealth an infinite claim on your time, attention, and money, starving the other four.
> - **Enough funds the rest:** Defining it caps one wealth and frees redeployable surplus into the others. This is statement 5 made real.
> - **The loop:** Locate the coordinate, define enough, redeploy the surplus into your lowest wealth, re-score in 90 days.

## Your Coordination Task List

Turn Financial Wealth into the tool it's supposed to be.

1. **Plot your coordinate** — list your four layers (Shield, Engine, Catalyst, Multiplier) and your current runway, using [[Wealth Part 2.0 - Financial Freedom Is a Coordinate|the Coordinate framework]].
2. **Mark "enough"** — define the coordinate where what you live for is funded for a runway that makes a shock survivable.
3. **Find your surplus** — name the money, time, and attention that sit beyond enough and are currently going to the chase.
4. **Redeploy into your lowest wealth** — point that surplus at whichever wealth scored lowest in [[Wealth Part 1.2 - The Five Wealths|Part 1.2]].
5. **Schedule the 90-day re-score** — retake the five quizzes, and check whether the redeployment moved the number you aimed at.
