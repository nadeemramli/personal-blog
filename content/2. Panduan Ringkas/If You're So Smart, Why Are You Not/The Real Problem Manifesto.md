I’ve read somewhere about _"If You're So Smart, Why Are You Unhappy?"_ or _"Why are you not happy?"_—something along those lines.

The interesting part of this axiom is that it challenges you to actually figure out the REAL problem, but it also challenges you to realize that the solution doesn’t come through "thinking" alone.

What happens with ideas is that they should give you praxis—practical applications—as well as thought-provoking philosophy. The balance between the two is great.

It’s not enough to solve a problem through thinking, no matter how hard you try. Some would say the core of the problem was the "thought" itself.
