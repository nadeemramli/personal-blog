### **Aesthetic Components & Benchmarks**  
*(Focus: Visual Physique Excellence)*  

| **Component**       | **Definition**                          | **Practical Benchmarks** (Elite/Above Avg)       |  
|----------------------|-----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|  
| **1. Size**          | Muscle mass relative to frame           | Men: 18"+ arms, 2x BW squat; Women: 15"+ arms, 1.5x BW squat |  
| **2. Symmetry**      | Balanced muscle development             | No >10% imbalance (e.g., left vs. right quad measurement) |  
| **3. Proportion**    | Harmonious ratios (e.g., shoulder:waist)| Men: Waist <45% shoulder width; Women: Hip:waist ~1.4:1 |  
| **4. Conditioning**  | Low body fat, striations, vascularity    | Men: 6–8% BF (visible abs); Stage-ready: 3–5% BF |  
| **5. Posing**        | Ability to showcase physique            | Judges score flow, transitions, and highlighting strengths (bodybuilding criteria) |  

---

### **Functionality Components & Benchmarks**  
*(Focus: Physical Performance Excellence)*  

| **Component**       | **Definition**                          | **Practical Benchmarks** (Elite/Above Avg)       |  
|----------------------|-----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|  
| **1. Strength**      | Max force production                    | Men: 1.5x BW bench, 2x BW deadlift; Women: 1x BW bench, 1.5x BW deadlift |  
| **2. Endurance**     | Sustained effort (cardio/muscular)      | 1.5-mile run: <10:30 (men), <12:00 (women); 60+ push-ups in 2 mins |  
| **3. Mobility**      | Joint ROM + stability                   | Sit-and-reach >15cm past toes; single-leg balance >60s eyes closed |  
| **4. Agility**       | Rapid direction change + reaction       | 5-10-5 shuttle: <4.5s (men), <5.0s (women); ruler drop test <15cm |  
| **5. Coordination**  | Skillful movement integration           | 50+ consecutive tennis wall rallies; 10m precision dribble (soccer) |  
| **6. Power**         | Explosive force (speed + strength)      | Vertical jump: 24"+ (men), 18"+ (women); broad jump >8' (men), >6.5' (women) |  

---

### **Key Notes**  
- **Aesthetic Standards**: Rooted in bodybuilding (e.g., Mr. Olympia judging criteria).  
- **Functional Standards**: Based on sport/combat metrics (NFL Combine, military fitness tests).  
- **Gender Adjustments**: Women’s benchmarks are ~60–80% of men’s due to physiological differences.  

**Example Athlete Profiles**:  
- **Aesthetic (Bodybuilder)**: 8% BF, 20" arms, flawless posing routine.  
- **Functional (MMA Fighter)**: 1.75x BW deadlift, 5:00 mile, 5.0s 5-10-5 shuttle.
