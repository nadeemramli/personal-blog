> Part 1.0 set out *what* to measure and *why*. This article is the practical manual for *how*. The structure below is the plan; the protocol detail, Malaysian device/lab specifics, and worked examples get filled in next.

## Planned contents

**The principle: trend over snapshot.** A single reading is almost meaningless. The job is to build a logged baseline and read the direction of travel, the same diagnostic logic as the [[Part 2.1 - Continuous Monitoring|Performance Enhancement monitoring chapter]].

**Body composition and structure**
- **FFMI**: the formula, where to get an honest body-fat % (DEXA, calipers, the limits of bioimpedance), and how to interpret it against the ~25 natural ceiling. Cross-link the full treatment in [[Part 1.1 - What Actually Matters|Fit]].
- **DEXA**: what the scan actually gives you (fat, lean mass, *and* bone mineral density in one pass), realistic Malaysian cost and cadence, where to book it (Sunway Medical, Columbia Asia, BP Healthcare).
- **Grip strength**: buying a hand dynamometer, the test protocol, age/sex norms, and logging it like any other lift.

**Cardiovascular**
- **Resting heart rate and HRV**: Whoop vs Apple Watch vs Oura, why a sleeping measurement beats a clinic snapshot, and reading multi-week trends instead of agonising over one night.
- **Blood pressure**: why an arm cuff beats a wrist cuff, the measurement protocol (seated, rested, same time daily, average of readings), logging into Apple Health / Whoop, and the target ranges.
- **Vascular function**: pulse-wave-velocity and flow-mediated-dilation testing, "vascular age" devices, where to get it, and the full ECG-is-not-a-vascular-test clarification from Part 1.0.
- **VEGF**: the specialised assay, and an honest "you can almost certainly skip this."

**Metabolic**
- **Fasting glucose and HbA1c** from a blood draw; the finger-prick meter for spot checks.
- **Continuous glucose monitor**: the arm patch (Freestyle Libre and similar), how to wear it in 1–2 week bursts, reading time-in-range, and using it to find *your* personal food spikes rather than living on it forever.

**Reproductive**
- **Semen analysis** for fertility: where, cost, what the report means (count, motility, morphology).
- **Nighttime erections**: home strain-gauge devices vs a clinical rigidity test, and how to read it as the vascular early-warning system it is.

**Biological age**
- **Epigenetic clocks** (DunedinPACE and absolute-age clocks via TruDiagnostic-style methylation tests) and **telomere length** tests: what they cost, how noisy they are, and a frank verdict on whether they're worth it yet for a normal person.

**The blood panel**
- The core panel to actually request (metabolic, full lipids, hormones, hs-CRP and inflammation, organ function), the Malaysian lab options (BP Healthcare, Gribbles, Pathlab), and the 3–6 month cadence.

**The minimum viable dashboard**
- A tiered table: the cheap essentials everyone should track, the mid-tier worth adding, and the exotic tier for the curious-and-wealthy. The whole point is *fewer, cheaper, repeatable.*
