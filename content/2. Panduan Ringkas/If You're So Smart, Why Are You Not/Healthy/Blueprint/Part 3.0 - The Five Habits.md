## Planned contents

**The philosophy first.** The best protocol is the one you'll actually keep, so it has to be small. The aim is to guide you to do *fewer* things for your health, built as habits rather than as a shopping list of therapies. This is the 90/10 rule from [[Part 4.0 - Pharmacology|Fit]] applied to the whole body: the structural basics are 90% of the result.

**The five habits.**

1. **Sleep: the #1 longevity drug.** It underpins every other healthy behaviour (exercise, nutrition, emotional resilience). Build your life around it. Cross-link the [[Part 1.0 - The Architecture of Sleep|Sleep series]] for the full system; this is the summary.
2. **Nutrition: every calorie must fight for its life.** The key move: never let your mind make eating decisions in the moment. Build and rely on food *systems* instead (this hands straight off to [[Part 3.1 - Building Systems and Breaking Bad Habits|Part 3.1]]). Cross-link the [[Nutrition|Nutrition note]].
3. **Exercise: about 6 hours a week is ideal,** spanning strength, cardio, flexibility, and balance. If that's impossible, even 20 minutes a day is a large win. Cross-link [[Part 1.1 - What Actually Matters|Fit]] (strength and body composition) and [[Part 1.0 - What Athleticism Actually Is|Athletic]] (cardio and the engine).
4. **Family, friendship, community: love and be loved.** Connection is not soft; it is one of the better-evidenced predictors of how long you live. This is the bridge into [[Part 4.0 - Mental and Emotional Wellbeing|Part 4.0]].
5. **Things to avoid.** Fast food, junk food, smoking, vaping, excessive alcohol, compulsive social media, and anything addictive. The framing: *addiction makes you a prisoner*, and "none is easier than some."

**The promise, honestly stated.** Implement the basics and you will feel better than you can remember, but you'll also feel the friction against your current habits, social obligations, and lifestyle. That friction is normal and is the subject of the next article.
