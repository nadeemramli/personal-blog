## The two courses

A build prioritises gaining strength and muscle. A cut prioritises losing fat while retaining as much training performance as possible. If neither goal has a clear reason or your routine is in flux, a period of maintenance gives you a baseline from which to decide.

## Where you stand

Look at weight and waist trends, performance on familiar lifts, and whether you can sustain your routine. Fat-free mass index expresses estimated fat-free mass relative to height. Use it as a rough description. The frequently cited cutoff comes from a study of male athletes and cannot establish an individual ceiling. [Kouri et al., FFMI study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7496846/)

## Give the course an end

Write down why this phase comes first, what progress would look like, and when you will review it. A build is going somewhere if performance is improving and the changes in weight and waist fit the goal. A cut is worth reassessing if training deteriorates or you can no longer sustain the routine. Decide from several observations under similar conditions.
