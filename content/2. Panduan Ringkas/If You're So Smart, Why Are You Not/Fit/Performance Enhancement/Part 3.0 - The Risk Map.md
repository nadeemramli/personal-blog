## Cardiovascular and blood effects

In the prospective HAARLEM cohort, blood pressure and hematocrit rose and lipid measures shifted in an unfavourable direction during androgen use. A separate observational study of long-term users found associations with impaired heart function and coronary atherosclerosis. These studies document important risks. Their designs cannot rank causes of death or predict an individual's outcome. [HAARLEM prospective study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35014715/) · [Baggish et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28533317/)

## Reproductive and other effects

Exogenous androgens can suppress reproductive hormone signalling and sperm production. Recovery varies. Liver injury, particularly with certain oral anabolic steroids, is another documented concern. Clinicians also need the full substance history when assessing kidney findings or metabolic changes. [HAARLEM reproductive follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33550376/) · [Endocrine Society scientific statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4026349/) · [Primary-care testing guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10962511/)

When a proposed safeguard claims to reduce harm, ask whether a study measured an improvement in health outcomes or only a change in a marker.
