## What protection means

Cardiovascular risk, reproductive function, and organ health each need their own assessment. Treating a diagnosed condition can be appropriate on its own merits. The available evidence on anabolic steroid harms does not establish a universal preventive regimen that offsets those harms. [Endocrine Society scientific statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4026349/) · [HAARLEM prospective study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35014715/)

## Start with the exposure history

Bring a complete list of substances to the appointment. For each proposed treatment, ask what condition it addresses, what benefit is supported in people like you, what adverse effects or interactions matter, and how follow-up will work. Aspirin illustrates the trade-off. Its benefit for primary cardiovascular prevention depends on the person's risk profile, and it can increase bleeding. [USPSTF aspirin recommendation](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/document/RecommendationStatementFinal/aspirin-to-prevent-cardiovascular-disease-preventive-medication)
