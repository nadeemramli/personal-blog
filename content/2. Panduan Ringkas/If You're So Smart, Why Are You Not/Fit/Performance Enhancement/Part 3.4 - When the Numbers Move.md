## Read the result in context

A lab report is one observation. Bring the current report, earlier results, symptoms, and an honest account of medications and supplements. Ask whether the finding needs confirmation, what might explain it, and how quickly it needs attention. In people with high muscle mass, creatinine-based kidney estimates can require careful interpretation. [Primary-care testing guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10962511/)

## Questions for the appointment

- Is this change meaningful in my situation?
- Could the sample, recent activity, illness, or a medication affect the result?
- Which finding needs prompt follow-up and who will arrange it?
- Which symptoms should lead me to seek urgent care?

For chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or stroke symptoms, seek urgent medical assessment. A previous normal panel cannot rule out an acute problem. [American Heart Association home monitoring guidance](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/high-blood-pressure/understanding-blood-pressure-readings/monitoring-your-blood-pressure-at-home)
