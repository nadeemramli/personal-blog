## What suppression did

External androgens can suppress the signals that drive testicular testosterone and sperm production. In the HAARLEM cohort, sperm production generally recovered more slowly than serum testosterone, and some participants still had abnormalities at the end of follow-up. An individual's course depends on their history and needs clinical evaluation. [HAARLEM reproductive follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33550376/)

## When recovery is the goal

Tell a clinician about the substances used, when they were last taken, symptoms, and whether you are trying to conceive. A clinician can assess whether hormone testing, semen analysis, or referral is appropriate. Evidence for treating withdrawal or accelerating recovery after nonmedical use is limited. Persistent symptoms or fertility concerns deserve follow-up even if one result has returned to range. [Clinical review of AAS cessation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6517163/)
