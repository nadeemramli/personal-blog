## Four questions for each review

- **Signal.** What has changed across repeated measurements?
- **Food.** Does your current routine support the goal you set?
- **Training.** Can you still perform the work the program calls for?
- **Health.** Are there symptoms or findings to discuss with a clinician?

Write down what you changed and why. When the decision concerns training or food, a small adjustment is easier to evaluate than an overhaul.

## Keep the records usable

For a clinical appointment, bring symptoms, recent results, and a complete list of medications and supplements. Ask how each result should be interpreted and what follow-up it needs. Monitoring can reveal changes associated with anabolic steroid use, including changes in blood pressure, lipids, and red-cell concentration. A reassuring result on one measure cannot settle the wider risk.
