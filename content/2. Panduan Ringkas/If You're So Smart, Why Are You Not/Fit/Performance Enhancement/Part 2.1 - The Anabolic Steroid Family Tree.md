## Two maps of the same territory

[[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Part 2.0]] mapped the body's anabolic machinery by **mechanism**: fifteen pathways, the eight receptor routes that compounds work and the seven that food, training and the body's own signals pull. This article maps the same compounds by **lineage** — the chemical family each one descends from. They're two maps of the same territory, and you want both: the pathway map tells you *what a compound does*, the family map tells you *what to expect from it before you've even looked it up*.

Derek's framework is the cleanest version of the lineage map. Almost every commonly used anabolic steroid falls into one of three families:[^1]

- **Testosterone and its derivatives**
- **Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) and its derivatives**
- **Nandrolone (19-Nortestosterone) and its derivatives**

Testosterone is the ultimate parent of the whole tree — both DHT and Nandrolone are derived from it. Compounds within a family tend to share attributes, so once you know the family, you already know most of what to expect (with a few important exceptions we'll flag). ==The family predicts the behaviour; the pathway explains it.==

## How anabolics actually enhance performance

Before the families, the bird's-eye view of *how* any of these compounds build muscle or improve performance. Derek lists four broad mechanisms[^1] — and each lines up directly with a pathway from [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Part 2.0]]:

- **Androgen-receptor activation**, plus aromatization, [5α-reduction](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/do-androgen-receptors-downregulate-from-continuous-steroid-use/), and downstream metabolites that drive size and strength → the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|AR and estrogen pathways]].
- **Antagonism of glucocorticoid receptors**, inhibiting protein breakdown and sparing muscle → the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|glucocorticoid pathways]].
- **Psychoactive effects in the brain** that drive harder training (and therefore more growth).
- **Stimulation of erythropoietin (EPO)**, raising red blood cells and aerobic performance — useful for output, but the exact mechanism behind the [[Part 3.2 - Continuous Monitoring|hematocrit creep]] you have to monitor.

Keep these four in mind: most of what separates one family from another is *which* of these it leans on, and how cleanly.

## The third map: genomic vs non-genomic, class 1 vs class 2

The family tells you what to expect. Two further splits tell you *how* a compound acts and *why* a stack pairs the way it does. Both come from the same Anabolic Round Table material the series leans on elsewhere (the Trensparent episode cited in [[Part 1.2 - Course Design|Part 1.2]], segment "Genomic vs Non-Genomic Steroid Pathways").

**Genomic versus non-genomic action.** The classic route is **genomic**: the androgen binds the nuclear androgen receptor, the complex moves into the nucleus and drives transcription of new contractile protein. It is slow (hours to days), it is what [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Pathway 1]] describes, and it is the mechanism the dose-chasers are overdosing. The **non-genomic** route is everything androgens do *without* transcription: binding membrane receptors and triggering rapid signalling (calcium flux, kinase cascades, nitric-oxide release), shifting neurotransmission, and the psychoactive effects that make a session feel different within an hour of a dose. Non-genomic effects are why strength and aggression can rise before any new tissue exists, why orals "kick in" in days, and why the brain-side costs (mood, sleep, aggression on the 19-nors) are a real part of the bill, not a side story. A stack that only counts genomic effects under-counts both the benefit and the cost.

**Class 1 versus class 2 binding.** This split sorts anabolics by how strongly they bind the androgen receptor itself:

| Class | What defines it | Examples in this series' language | Why it matters for pairing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 1** | Strong androgen-receptor binders; most of their anabolism is genomic, through the AR | Testosterone, Primobolan-class, Masteron-class, Trenbolone-class, Nandrolone | Two class 1 compounds compete for the same receptors; stacking them adds side effects faster than it adds anabolism |
| **Class 2** | Weak androgen-receptor binders that are nonetheless strongly anabolic through *other* routes (estrogenic signalling, non-genomic action, red-cell and glycogen effects) | Dianabol, Anadrol-class | A class 2 compound adds a *different* mechanism on top of a class 1 base, which is why the classic pairing is one of each rather than two of the same |

Read this series' lean-bulk stack through that lens and it falls out exactly: a class 1 base and a class 1 dial that do *not* compete (the dial is doing estrogen work, not AR work), one class 1 19-nor at the minimum for the progesterone and glucocorticoid side, and one class 2 oral pre-workout for the estrogenic and non-genomic side. The cut keeps the class 1 anchor and swaps the oral for a dry DHT-derivative whose value is mostly non-genomic (strength and hardness in a deficit). ==Family predicts behaviour; genomic versus non-genomic predicts timing and the brain-side cost; class predicts what can be paired without paying twice.==

## Family 1 — Testosterone & its derivatives

Testosterone is the [parent hormone](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/how-many-milligrams-of-testosterone-do-men-naturally-produce/) of this branch and the root of the entire tree. The performance-relevant members:[^1]

- **Testosterone** — the [base of most protocols](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/testosterone-dosage-for-bodybuilding/)
- **Boldenone (Equipoise)**
- **Dianabol (Methandrostenolone)** — Derek's deep-dive sits inside his [Arnold cycle breakdown](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/arnold-schwarzeneggers-steroid-cycle/)
- **Halotestin (Fluoxymesterone)**
- **Turinabol (Chlorodehydromethyltestosterone)**

**Character of the family.** These are the broad-spectrum compounds — they touch anabolic *and* androgenic functions, interact with aromatase, and have strong effects on red blood cells and energy systems. They produce a middle-ground level of hypertrophy, neurological effect, and aerobic enhancement: anabolic, but not wildly so. They behave roughly the way balanced endogenous steroidogenesis would.[^1]

**Aromatization is the defining trait.** Testosterone aromatizes to [estradiol](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/sensitive-assay-estradiol-test/); Dianabol converts to methylestradiol. Boldenone is *mildly* estrogenic but much less than the other two (the data is genuinely murky). Because Testosterone, Dianabol, and Boldenone all aromatize and have broad activity, ==they are the only three compounds in this family that can serve as a "[test base](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/testosterone-dosage-for-bodybuilding/)"== — the estrogenic, full-spectrum foundation a course is built on.

**The two exceptions.** [Halotestin](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/the-highest-steroid-dosage-ever-well-tolerated/) and Turinabol do *not* aromatize. They were designed to strip out estrogenic activity and mass, and lean instead toward pure protein expression and the neurological/strength end of the spectrum — meaning they behave far more like DHT derivatives than like their own family. A useful early lesson that the tree has exceptions in every branch.

## Family 2 — DHT & its derivatives

DHT is the 5α-reduced metabolite of Testosterone — more androgenic than Testosterone, and responsible for male maturation at puberty. The performance-relevant derivatives:[^1]

- **Masteron (Drostanolone)**
- **Primobolan (Metenolone)** — featured in Derek's "[highest steroid dosage ever well tolerated](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/the-highest-steroid-dosage-ever-well-tolerated/)"
- **Anavar (Oxandrolone)** — [Derek's breakdown](https://youtu.be/Z2bHe562IpA?t=598)
- **Anadrol (Oxymetholone)** — [dosage & results](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/anadrol-dosage-results-comparison/)
- **Winstrol (Stanozolol)** — [Derek's breakdown](https://youtu.be/gxmfSauPxTo)
- **Proviron (Mesterolone)**
- **Superdrol (Methasterone)** — [Derek's breakdown](https://youtu.be/7MGquMdLAUQ)
- **DHB (Dihydroboldenone / 1-Testosterone)** — [Derek's overview](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/dhb-dihydroboldenone/)

**The big misconception.** People assume DHT derivatives must be brutally androgenic. The opposite is usually true: ==DHT derivatives are almost all *more tissue-selective* than Testosterone.==[^1] They are not substrates for aromatase, they don't act as agonists of a dozen satellite receptors the way 19-nors do, and they don't 5α-reduce into something more androgenic. That selectivity strips out the unpredictable activity of the other two families.

**What that means in practice.** No water retention, no progestogenic side effects — just relatively pure muscle growth and proportionally higher strength gains. This is exactly why DHT derivatives get mislabelled "weak": ==what people read as weakness is actually the *absence of side effects*.==[^1] They're dry strength builders with reliable, predictable activity, and they're the cosmetic tools (a drier, harder look) of the tree. The four most commonly leveraged are **Primobolan, Masteron, Anavar, and Winstrol.** Proviron is the outlier used more for [androgen-replacement / SHBG effects](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/the-main-secondary-reasons-to-use-a-certain-performance-enhancing-drug/) than mass — see the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|SHBG pathway in Part 2.0]].

**The exception: Anadrol.** Despite being a DHT derivative, [Anadrol](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/anadrol-dosage-results-comparison/) behaves like a 19-nor — it drives pronounced gains in sheer (watery) mass and acts as an estrogen-receptor agonist. Proof that the family label is a strong default, not a law.

## Family 3 — 19-Nortestosterone (Nandrolone) & its derivatives

The last family. The parent is Nandrolone (19-Nortestosterone); the members:[^1]

- **Nandrolone** — "[Deca](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/deca-only-for-hrt/)" is nandrolone with a decanoate ester, "NPP" the same hormone with a phenylpropionate ester (same drug, different release speed)
- **Trenbolone (Trienolone)** — [Derek's dosage write-up](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/trenbolone-dosage/)
- **Trestolone (MENT)** — [Derek's experiment](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/ment-trestolone-hair-loss-experiment/)

**Character of the family.** Very anabolic, but defined by **progestogenic activity** — they're agonists of the progesterone receptor (the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|PR pathway]]), along with a web of satellite receptor interactions that make them less predictable and more demanding to manage.[^1]

- **Nandrolone** converts to a weak estrogen (Estrone, ~10× weaker than estradiol), is mildly estrogenic as an [ERα agonist](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/15276617/) in its own right, and 5α-reduces to *less* androgenic DHN — so it's much less androgenic and estrogenic than Testosterone, and prized for joint/connective-tissue support.[^5]
- **Trenbolone** doesn't aromatize but is a potent **glucocorticoid-receptor antagonist** — it suppresses glucocorticoid expression and blocks cortisol from binding skeletal-muscle receptors, which is *why* it so dramatically outperforms other steroids in a calorie deficit (the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|cortisol-blocking pathways]]).[^4]
- **Trestolone (MENT)** is the odd one: it *does* aromatize, behaving like a Testosterone/19-nor hybrid, which gives it some promise as a test-base alternative.

> [!danger] The 19-nor suppression warning
> ==The 19-nors are the most suppressive family of the entire tree.== Even minuscule trace amounts keep your [HPTA](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/deca-only-for-hrt/) shut down — a single nandrolone injection can suppress you for *months* regardless of what you do.[^1] Derek's conclusion is the prudent one: reserve 19-nors until you've already decided you'll be [blasting and cruising](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/testosterone-dosage-for-bodybuilding/) for the long haul. This is the [[Part 1.0 - The Decision|Part 1]] lifetime-commitment question made chemically concrete.

19-nors also have unique GH/IGF-1 interactions ([[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Pathways 7 & 8]]) that DHT derivatives lack — another reason they reward an experienced hand and punish a careless one.

## The myth-buster: all of them retain similar nitrogen

This is the single most clarifying point in Derek's article, and it reframes the whole "which steroid is strongest?" question. Classic nitrogen-balance studies found that the most potent compounds from *every* family produce **roughly the same nitrogen retention** — on the order of a daily gain of 60–75 g of lean body mass for the strong injectables, with oral 17-alkyl derivatives inducing a dose-dependent effect of the same order.[^1][^3]

In other words: ==the actual contractile tissue you build is similar across compounds. What differs is everything *around* it.== If Dianabol adds five pounds of water in a week, that doesn't make it a better muscle-builder than Anavar — it makes it a wetter one. The destination (muscle gained) is comparable; the *journey* (water, estrogen, progestogenic effects, neurological feel, organ strain) is what varies wildly between compounds.[^1]

> [!important] Why this matters for the whole series
> This is the chemical proof of [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Part 2.0]]'s "choose by the job, not the hype" rule. Since raw muscle-building is roughly equivalent, you select a compound for its **side-effect and secondary-mechanism profile** — its job — not for a mythical potency ranking. And it's why "more / harsher" so rarely beats "moderate / multi-pathway."

*The [[Part 2.1 - The Anabolic Steroid Family Tree#The myth-buster: all of them retain similar nitrogen|myth-buster]] sits at the heart of this article: every family — Testosterone, DHT, 19-nor — retains roughly the same nitrogen, which means the raw muscle built is comparable across compounds. What actually differs is the bill you pay above that shared floor: water, estrogen, progestogenic effects, suppression. The three families all descend from one parent and climb in a fixed learning order (Test → DHT → 19-nor), with the most suppressive family gated at the top. Use this reference to place any compound in its lineage and predict its behaviour before you've looked anything else up.*

## The family tree in motion: wet vs. dry is a setting, not a property

Compound selection gets oversimplified into "wet" (mass plus water) versus "dry" (hard and lean) compounds. In reality, ==**"wetness" is not an inherent property of a steroid**; it is a manageable manifestation of aromatisation and of stimulation of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS).== (This section moved here from the old Cycle Design page in November 2026; it is the family tree read as a dial.)

- **DHT derivatives as aromatase modulators.** The "dry" compounds, especially **Primobolan and Masteron**, act as selective and reversible modulators of aromatase. Layering a DHT derivative onto an aromatising "wet" base (testosterone, nandrolone) lets you *modulate the estrogenic output* without the systemic lipid strain of a suicidal aromatase inhibitor like exemestane. The dry compound becomes your estrogen-management tool ([[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Pathway 3]]), not just a cosmetic one.
- **"The dose is the poison" with wet compounds.** Testosterone, nandrolone and Anadrol offer the highest anabolic incentive, but the side-effect profile is dose-driven. Nandrolone at a low level provides genuine joint and synovial-fluid support; escalated far enough it triggers the "bloat monster": excessive RAAS activation and progestogenic activity. Same compound, opposite outcome, set by the dose.
- **Marketability by design.** For athletes who need a lean, dry look year-round, the intelligent design is a **testosterone base plus a DHT derivative** to modulate conversion: cosmetic crispness maintained even through a high-intensity build course.

> [!note] This is the family tree in motion
> Re-read the three families above with this lens: the DHT family isn't "weak," it is the *dial* you use to keep an aromatising base in range. Wet/dry isn't which compound you picked; it is how you tuned the ratio. The water itself is [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Pathway 6]]'s territory and the electrolytes behind it are [[Part 3.6 - Electrolyte Management|Part 3.6]]'s.

## The learning ladder: Test → DHT → 19-nor

Derek's recommended order of education is also the sane order of *use*, and it dovetails with [[Part 1.0 - The Decision|Part 1]]'s "don't waste blasts" and [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Part 2.0]]'s "lowest effective dose":[^1]

1. **Master the Testosterone family first.** Understand why a test base anchors nearly every protocol,[^2] and how its aromatization and 5α-reduction create a self-balancing act of androgenic and estrogenic activity (with [neuro- and cardioprotection](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/sensitive-assay-estradiol-test/) from estrogen). For most people, testosterone alone is the [first course](https://moreplatesmoredates.com/testosterone-dosage-for-bodybuilding/).
2. **Add DHT derivatives only once you've fully leveraged Testosterone.** They're the next rung: dry strength and cosmetic refinement on subsequent courses.
3. **Reach for 19-nors last** — only if your goals genuinely can't be met with Test + DHT derivatives, or you can't tolerate those, *and* you've accepted blast-and-cruise. Their suppression and satellite complexity demand an experienced hand.

> [!tip] The whole ladder in one sentence
> Climb one family at a time, exhaust the cheapest/safest lever before adding the next, and never skip to the most suppressive family just because it's the most hyped.

**Bioidentical first, exotic last.** The same ladder, read across hormone classes rather than steroid families: the antiquated "testosterone and Dianabol" first course is a high-risk shotgun that often causes premature HPTA shutdown and systemic shock. The professional entry prioritises the lowest effective dose and the three bioidentical hormones the body already makes and regulates, **testosterone, growth hormone and (for the full-time athlete only) insulin**, maximised before any exotic metabolite is reached for. A modest testosterone base taken in frequent small injections (the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|pin-frequency]] principle, for stable androgen and estrogen levels), a low pharmaceutical GH dose for the androgen-to-IGF-1 synergy, and only then, cautiously, insulin for partitioning, *before* trenbolone or nandrolone ever enter. The point of the hierarchy is to minimise the exotic metabolic load on the liver and kidneys so that the first transition into an enhanced state is sustainable. The operating rules that enforce it (one compound at a time, bloods four weeks in, the 19-nor near-last) are in [[Part 1.3 - Running the Course|Part 1.3]]; the reserve test that precedes the first course is in [[Part 3.7 - Coming Off and PCT|Part 3.7]].

## How the families map onto the receptor pathways

This is where the two maps connect — useful for re-reading [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|Part 2.0]] with the lineage in mind:

- **Testosterone family → AR + Estrogen pathways.** Broad AR activation plus aromatization makes this family the natural home of the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|AR (Pathway 1) and ER (Pathway 3)]] levers — and the reason "manage estrogen, don't crush it" lives here.
- **DHT family → SHBG + the "restraint" pathways.** Non-aromatizing and tissue-selective, these are the [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|SHBG (Pathway 2)]] tool (Proviron, Masteron) and the dryness/cosmetic side of estrogen management — they *reduce* estrogenic load rather than add it.
- **19-Nor family → Progesterone + Glucocorticoid pathways.** Progestogenic by nature (Pathway 4) and, in Trenbolone's case, the strongest [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|glucocorticoid-blocking (Pathways 5 & 6)]] tool there is — with the prolactin and suppression costs to match.

## Part 2.1 Takeaways

> [!check] Key concepts to internalize
> 
> - **Three families, one parent.** Testosterone, DHT, and 19-Nortestosterone — all descend from testosterone, and the family predicts most of a compound's behaviour.
> - **Testosterone family = broad-spectrum base.** Aromatizing, RBC-driving, middle-ground; only Test, Dbol, and Boldenone can anchor a course. (Halo & Turinabol are exceptions that act DHT-like.)
> - **DHT family = dry, tissue-selective strength.** No aromatization, no water, no progestogenic sides; "weak" is a misread of "few side effects." (Anadrol is the 19-nor-like exception.)
> - **19-Nor family = max anabolism, max baggage.** Progestogenic, prolactin-raising, GH/IGF-1-interacting, and the *most suppressive* family — reserve for the blast-and-cruise-committed.
> - **All families retain similar nitrogen.** Raw muscle built is comparable; side effects and secondary mechanisms are what differ — the chemical basis for "choose by the job, not the hype."
> - **Climb the ladder: Test → DHT → 19-nor**, lowest effective dose, one family at a time.

## Your Family-Tree Task List

1. **Place every compound you're considering into its family** before anything else — it tells you aromatization, water, progestogenic risk, and suppression at a glance.
2. **Confirm you have a valid test base** (Test, Dbol, or Boldenone — or MENT) anchoring any plan; most protocols need one.
3. **If you're eyeing a 19-nor,** re-read the suppression warning and the [[Part 1.0 - The Decision|Part 1]] commitment question. Have you actually accepted blast-and-cruise?
4. **Cross-reference each compound's family with its [[Part 2.0 - The Anabolic Pathways|pathway job]].** Two compounds in the same family doing the same job = redundancy = risk with no reward.
5. **Read Derek's per-compound page** (linked above) for anything you're seriously considering — the family gives the gist; the deep-dive gives the detail.
6. **Honour the ladder.** If you haven't exhausted Testosterone, you have no business in the DHT or 19-nor families yet.

[^3]: Nitrogen-balance equivalence across steroid families: the classic metabolic-balance literature finding that potent injectable 17β-esters (e.g., nandrolone phenylpropionate, nandrolone decanoate, methenolone enanthate) and oral 17-alkyl derivatives produce comparable nitrogen retention at equivalent doses; the differences between compounds lie in their androgenic, estrogenic and progestogenic side activity, their receptor binding and their half-lives, not in the size of the anabolic signal itself. See the classic metabolic-balance reviews: Kochakian CD (ed.) (1976), *Anabolic-Androgenic Steroids*, Handbook of Experimental Pharmacology vol. 43, Springer; Kruskemper HL (1968), *Anabolic Steroids*, Academic Press.

[^5]: Nandrolone's receptor profile (weaker androgenic activity after 5α-reduction to dihydronandrolone, mild estrogen-receptor activity of its own): Bergink EW, Janssen PS, Turpijn EW, van der Vies J (1985), "Comparison of the receptor binding properties of nandrolone and testosterone under in vitro and in vivo conditions," *Journal of Steroid Biochemistry* 22(6):831–836; the ERα-agonism paper linked inline; Derek (More Plates More Dates), his nandrolone write-up, linked inline above.
