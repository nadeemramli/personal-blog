## Three families

Testosterone-related, dihydrotestosterone-related, and nandrolone-related compounds are ways of grouping anabolic-androgenic steroids by chemical structure. The grouping is useful when reading about pharmacology. Real-world harms are harder to assign to a family because people can use multiple substances and products may differ from their labels. [HAARLEM cohort baseline study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31663164/) · [Endocrine Society scientific statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4026349/)

## What the labels miss

Bodybuilding descriptions such as “wet” and “dry” describe an appearance. They cannot tell you what a compound is doing to blood pressure, lipid levels, or reproductive function. A useful comparison names the outcome, the population studied, and what else participants were taking. [HAARLEM cardiovascular findings](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35014715/)
