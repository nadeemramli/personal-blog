## Appearance, performance, and health

A fuller muscle, a lower scale weight, and better performance measure different things. A short-term visual change can reflect fluid shifts. It gives little information about what happened to fat mass or whether someone is healthier.

## What the drug categories tell us

Prescription treatments for metabolic or endocrine disease are evaluated for specific indications and patient groups. Their effects in those settings cannot be assumed to carry over to bodybuilding. Liothyronine carries a boxed warning against use for weight loss. Insulin misuse can cause severe hypoglycemia, and diuretic misuse can disrupt fluid and electrolyte balance. [Liothyronine prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5222f13d-7222-4845-b863-49e7558f5b1a) · [Endocrine Society scientific statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4026349/)

When a claim rests on a cell or animal experiment, look for a human study that measured the outcome being promised.
