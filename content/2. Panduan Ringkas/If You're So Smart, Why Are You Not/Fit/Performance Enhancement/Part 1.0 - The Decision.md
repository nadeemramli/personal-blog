## Where this series picks up

If you want to get stronger, look better, or perform better, start with the framework you already have. Pick a goal, train for it, eat in a way that supports it, and measure whether you are moving toward it. If progress has stalled, ask what changed in your training, sleep, or food before assuming you have reached a biological ceiling.

## The one-way door

Anabolic-androgenic steroids can change physique and performance. They can also suppress reproductive function and are associated with cardiovascular harm. Recovery varies, and sperm production can take longer to recover than testosterone. A training milestone cannot tell you how your health will respond. [Endocrine Society scientific statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4026349/) · [HAARLEM reproductive follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33550376/)

Write down the result you want, why it matters, and which costs would make it a bad trade. Include health, fertility, and the time you want to spend thinking about your physique. Keep the same criteria when progress is slow and when it is going well.
