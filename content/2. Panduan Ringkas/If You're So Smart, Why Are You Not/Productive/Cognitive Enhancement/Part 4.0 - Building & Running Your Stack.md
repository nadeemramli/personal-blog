## The base stack: ten ingredients, by job

Here's a comprehensive focus-and-cognition stack, each ingredient tagged to its pillar so you can see it's not random — it's one tool per job from Part 3.

| Ingredient | Pillar (from [[Part 1.0 - The Cognitive Architecture\|Part 1.0]]) | What it does here |
|---|---|---|
| **L-Tyrosine** | Executive Function (dopamine fuel) | Raw precursor for dopamine under stress/high output |
| **Caffeine** | Executive Function (stimulant) | The base arousal driver |
| **L-Theanine** | Flexibility / State Control | Removes caffeine's jitter; the calm half of the signal-to-noise balance |
| **Alpha-GPC** *(or CDP-choline)* | Memory (choline fuel) | Raw material for acetylcholine |
| **Huperzine-A** | Memory (the brake) | Slows acetylcholine breakdown — must be cycled |
| **ALCAR** (Acetyl-L-Carnitine) | Memory + mitochondrial energy | Acetyl donor for acetylcholine; buffers mental fatigue |
| **Uridine monophosphate** | Preservation (structural) | Synaptic membrane building block ([[Part 3.1 - Neural Preservation\|Part 3.1]]) |

| **Ginkgo Biloba** | Blood flow | Cerebral circulation — delivers everything else |

> [!note] On Alpha-GPC vs CDP-choline
> Given the [[Part 3.0 - Cognitive Performance|alpha-GPC stroke/TMAO signal]], the conservative build swaps in **CDP-choline (citicoline)** as the choline source — it also supplies uridine precursors, so it partially overlaps with the uridine line. If you keep alpha-GPC, don't run it at the top dose every day indefinitely. This matters more for you than most, given the [[Part 4.0 - Protection|cardiovascular load you already carry on cycle]].

## Dosage optimisation: getting each one right

This is where most pre-made blends quietly fail — they fix the ratios for *convenience*, not for *clinical optimum*. Here's each ingredient against its evidence-backed daily target:

| Ingredient | Common blend dose | Clinical / optimal target | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L-Tyrosine** | 1,000–2,000 mg | 1,000–2,000 mg | **Perfect.** Scales cleanly. |
| **Alpha-GPC** | 600 mg of "50%" = 300 mg active | 300–600 mg active | **Check the math** — "50%" means a 600 mg cap yields ~300 mg active. One serving hits baseline, two hits the ceiling. |
| **ALCAR** | 500–1,000 mg | 500–2,000 mg | **Solid.** ~1,000 mg is the noticeable-effect sweet spot. |
| **Uridine** | 200–400 mg | 150–300 mg | **Optimal**, especially paired with a choline source. |
| **Ginkgo** | 180–360 mg | 120–240 mg | **Good.** 120–180 mg is plenty; more is safe but unnecessary. |
| **Bacopa** | 165–330 mg | ~300 mg (at 50% bacosides) | **Underdosed at one serving.** Needs ~300 mg+ to actually work for memory.[^1] |
| **Caffeine** | 150–300 mg | 100–200 mg | **The bottleneck** — see below. |
| **L-Theanine** | 100–200 mg | ~200 mg (2:1 vs caffeine) | **Usually under-ratioed** in blends. |
| **Huperzine-A** | 100–200 mcg | 100–200 mcg | **Right dose, must cycle** — long half-life, accumulates. |
| **Saffron** | 15–30 mg | ~30 mg (0.3% safranal) | Effective; standalone bottles are often *much* higher (~88 mg), so one cap is plenty. |

> [!check] The two fixes that matter most
> 1. **The caffeine:theanine ratio.** The golden ratio for clean focus is **2:1 theanine-to-caffeine** (e.g. 200 mg theanine / 100 mg caffeine).[^2] Most blends invert this, leaving you over-stimulated. If your blend runs 150 mg caffeine to 100 mg theanine, just add a standalone 100 mg theanine cap. Cheapest, highest-impact tweak in the whole stack.
> 2. **Cycle the Huperzine-A.** Its long half-life means daily use accumulates into headaches and muscle twitches. Run it intermittently (e.g. weekdays on, weekends off), not continuously.

## The pre-made trap: why one scoop forces a compromise

Pre-made blends are genuinely convenient and often well-formulated. Their structural flaw is that **the ingredients are locked together in one ratio**, so to get a clinical dose of the herbal components you're forced to take everything else at that multiple too:

> [!warning] The caffeine handcuff
> In a typical blend, the *only* way to hit the optimal **Bacopa (300 mg)** and **Tyrosine (2 g)** doses is to take **two servings** — which drags your **caffeine to ~300 mg** (three-plus cups of coffee in one hit). For most people that means jitters and a hard crash. ==You end up over-caffeinated just to properly dose the herbs.== The blend can't separate the stimulant from the structure. A DIY stack can — which is the entire case for building it yourself, and it's especially relevant if you want to use the focus components in the *afternoon* or on *rest days* without wrecking your sleep.

## Synergy and redundancy: what to add, what to skip

Once your base is comprehensive, *adding more is not automatically better* — the same inverted-U and overload logic from Part 3 applies. Here's how the common "should I add X?" candidates actually fit:

### Synergistic — genuinely worth adding

- **Lion's Mane (dual-extract) → high value.** Adds a *different* mechanism (NGF/neuroplasticity, [[Part 3.1 - Neural Preservation|Part 3.1]]) with no overlap on your neurotransmitter compounds. Pairs with Bacopa for long-term structural support. Slow-acting — judge it over months.
- **Creatine monohydrate → yes, but drop the dose.** A brain-energy (ATP) buffer that reduces mental fatigue, *especially* under sleep deprivation — and you're likely already taking it for the gym. ==The cognitive dose is 3–5 g daily, not 20 g.== The 20 g you'd see is a short athletic *loading* protocol; for daily cognitive/maintenance use, 3–5 g is the sweet spot and the rest just becomes waste. Synergises with ALCAR on mitochondrial energy.
- **Phosphatidylserine (PS) → situational synergy.** A structural phospholipid that supports cell-membrane fluidity and helps blunt cortisol spikes — a nice "smoother" alongside L-theanine against caffeine's edge, and it supports the membrane-building that makes uridine work.

### Redundant or risky — skip

- **DMAE → skip.** It's another route to raising acetylcholine — which you're *already* hitting hard with alpha-GPC (fuel) and Huperzine-A (brake). Stacking DMAE on top risks **cholinergic overload**: depressive mood dips, jaw clenching, muscle stiffness, heavy brain fog. Pure redundancy with downside. (This is the same overload warning from [[Part 3.0 - Cognitive Performance|Part 3.0]].)
- **Pregnenolone / DHEA → cautionary, and *not* a casual add.** Potent hormones that shift your endocrine baseline ([[Part 3.1 - Neural Preservation|Part 3.1]]). Don't drop them into a daily stack "for focus." If you're on cycle and suspect the neurosteroid-depletion fog, that's a **test-first, supervised** correction — not a stack ingredient. If forced to choose one for cognition, pregnenolone is the cleaner pick (DHEA converts downstream to androgens/oestrogens).

### The special case — Rhodiola Rosea (a "swing weapon," not a daily driver)

Rhodiola is neither cleanly synergistic nor redundant — it's **high synergy with high over-stimulation risk**, because it interacts with three things you're already taking.

> [!caution] Rhodiola is mechanistically synergistic but practically redundant in its *goals*
> Rhodiola is a mild **MAO inhibitor** — it slows the *breakdown* of dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin. **Saffron** is a *reuptake* inhibitor — it blocks their *recycling*. They attack the same target from opposite ends (one stops the woodchipper, the other plugs the vacuum), so combining them — on top of **L-Tyrosine** (the raw material) and **caffeine** (the stimulant) — means you're maxing out the *entire dopamine life cycle* at once. For a naturally normal-dopamine brain, that can tip into **hyper-noradrenergic** territory: racing heart, cold hands, irritability, "wired-but-tired." So: use it as a **swing weapon for high-stress, low-sleep days**, start low (**100–250 mg**), and choose a **3% rosavins / 1% salidroside** extract (rosavins are calmer; high-salidroside is jittery).

## Timing: building the stack around your day

This is where it gets genuinely clever, and it's the upgrade from a *static* stack (everything fighting for dominance at 9 AM) to a *dynamic* one that follows your day's energy demands. The model assumes your real schedule: **deep work 9–5, train at 6 PM, sleep ~11 PM.**

```
[ 9 AM – 5 PM ]  🚀  CORE STACK + SAFFRON   → office focus, mood, output
                     (Tyrosine, choline, caffeine+theanine 2:1,
                      ALCAR, uridine, bacopa, ginkgo, saffron)
[ 5 PM – 6 PM ]  📉  THE TRANSITION         → caffeine fades, CNS fatigue rises
[ 5:30 PM ]      ⚡  RHODIOLA (pre-workout)  → crushes CNS fatigue for training
[ 6 PM – 8 PM ]      TRAIN                    → drive + dopamine for the session
[ with dinner /  🌙  L-THEANINE (extra)      → handbrake on lingering norepinephrine
  pre-bed ]                                    → protects sleep
```

> [!check] Why splitting Saffron (AM) and Rhodiola (PM) is mechanistically smart
> - **Saffron in the morning** anchors mood and focus through the workday. By 6 PM its acute peak has passed, but its long half-life keeps a smooth baseline lift running in the background.
> - **Rhodiola at ~5:30 PM** is a *transitional bridge*: it counters the central-nervous-system fatigue of a long mental day and the fading-caffeine dip, so you walk into training with the dopamine/norepinephrine drive to recruit muscle fibres after eight hours at a desk. It also supports cellular ATP and glycogen — pairing with your creatine.
> - **Staggering them** captures the mechanism synergy (Saffron's lingering reuptake-block + Rhodiola's MAO-inhibition) **without** the chaotic jittery peak you'd get swallowing both at once.

> [!danger] The sleep caveat on an evening Rhodiola
> Rhodiola keeps norepinephrine active — and norepinephrine is exactly the chemical that *wakes the brain up*. Take a potent extract at 5:30–6 PM and you can be physically exhausted from training but mentally racing at 11 PM. Two defences: (1) keep the pre-workout dose **small (100–200 mg)** and rosavin-dominant; (2) ==take your L-theanine *post-workout / pre-bed*== — it raises GABA and acts as the handbrake that turns off the lingering norepinephrine push, letting you transition into deep sleep. This connects straight to the [[Part 2.1 - The Pre-Sleep Protocol|Sleep series' pre-sleep protocol]].

## The cost model: DIY vs pre-made, true monthly burn

Now the part you actually asked for — costed properly, the same way the [[Part 5.1 - Cost|PE finale]] frames it: **the real question isn't "what's the upfront price," it's "what's the true monthly burn rate."** Because DIY bottles empty at wildly different rates, the big upfront number is misleading.

### Step 1 — The upfront DIY cash-out (illustrative RM, 10 ingredients)

| Ingredient | Pack (illustrative) | Pack price (RM) | Cost / day |
|---|---|---|---|
| L-Tyrosine | 500 g powder | 114 | 0.23 |
| Alpha-GPC | 120 caps | 102 *(or ~73 higher-capacity)* | 1.70 *(→ 0.61)* |
| ALCAR | 180 caps | 78 | 0.43 |
| Uridine | 60 caps | 78 | 1.29–2.58 |
| Caffeine | 250 caps | 49 | 0.20 |
| L-Theanine | 120 caps | 49 | 0.41 |
| Huperzine-A | 240 caps | 82 | 0.34 |
| Ginkgo | 240 caps | 61 | 0.25 |
| Bacopa | 120 caps | 69 | 0.58 |
| Saffron | 240 caps | 82 | 0.34 |
| **Upfront total** | | **~RM 760** | |

### Step 2 — Why upfront ≠ monthly: component lifespans

The trick is that a 500 g tub of tyrosine lasts **~16 months** while uridine and alpha-GPC empty in **~2 months**. So the *recurring* cost is far below the cash-out:

- L-Tyrosine powder → ~500 days  ·  Huperzine / Ginkgo / Saffron → ~8 months  ·  Caffeine → ~8 months
- ALCAR → ~6 months  ·  L-Theanine / Bacopa → ~4 months  ·  **Alpha-GPC & Uridine → ~2 months (your fast-movers)**

==The only things you restock often are Alpha-GPC and Uridine.== Everything else is a long-tail buy you make once or twice a year.

### Step 3 — The five procurement pathways

Comparing a typical pre-made formula (≈RM 240 for a 30-serving tub) against the DIY routes:

| Pathway | Upfront | **True monthly burn** | The verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Pre-made, 1 serving/day** | ~RM 240 | **RM 240** | The convenience benchmark. One scoop. |
| **2. Pre-made, 2 servings/day** | ~RM 480 | **RM 480** | Expensive, and forces ~300 mg caffeine daily. |
| **3. DIY base** | ~RM 760 | **RM 183** | Saves ~RM 57/mo vs pre-made 1×; full dose control. |
| **4. DIY + higher-capacity Alpha-GPC** | ~RM 734 | **RM 150** | **The financial sweet spot** — the cheaper choline bottle drops monthly choline spend dramatically. |
| **5. DIY, cheaper Alpha-GPC + double-dosed** | ~RM 734 | **RM 178** | **The value champion** — *double* the clinical potency (2 g tyrosine, 1 g ALCAR, 240 mg ginkgo) for *less than one serving* of the pre-made formula. |

> [!check] The mathematical takeaways
> - **The Alpha-GPC bottle choice is the single biggest cost lever** — switching to a higher-capacity pack roughly halves your recurring choline spend.
> - **Pathway 5 is the standout:** for **less than the monthly cost of a single pre-made serving (RM 240)**, you get a *double-strength* stack with superior Bacopa and Saffron dosing — and you can keep caffeine clean at 100 mg while running theanine at 200 mg. It "wins" on cost efficiency *and* neural output.
> - **The cost is the trade for hassle.** DIY means **~9–11 capsules + a scoop of powder every morning** and managing ~10 bottles. The pre-made route is one scoop. That convenience is the entire thing you're paying the premium for.

> [!tip] The honest cost hierarchy (same as the PE series)
> For context, the [[Part 5.1 - Cost|PE finale]] makes the point that ==the gear is the *cheap* recurring part of a sensible protocol== — food, gym, and fast-moving consumables dominate the real budget. A cognitive stack is similar: at ~RM 150–240/month it sits *below* your food and training spend, and the highest-ROI "purchase" — fixing [[Part 2.0 - The Behavioral Base|sleep and your dopamine baseline]] — costs nothing at all.

## Putting it all together

A complete picture of the finished, dynamic, Tier-2 protocol — assuming you've passed the [[Part 2.0 - The Behavioral Base|gate]] and have [[Part 1.1 - Measuring Cognition|baseline measurements]]:

> [!check] The assembled protocol
> **Morning (with breakfast, start of work):** L-Tyrosine · choline source (CDP-choline preferred, or alpha-GPC not-at-max) · caffeine 100 mg + L-theanine 200 mg (the 2:1) · ALCAR · uridine · Bacopa · Ginkgo · Saffron · *(+ Lion's Mane and 3–5 g creatine as the synergistic adds)* · Huperzine-A **on weekdays only**.
> **Late afternoon (~5:30 PM, training days, optional):** Rhodiola 100–200 mg (rosavin-dominant) as the pre-workout swing weapon.
> **Evening (post-workout / pre-bed):** extra L-theanine as the norepinephrine handbrake; phosphatidylserine if using it.
> **Skip entirely:** DMAE (redundant/overload). **Hands off unless tested:** pregnenolone/DHEA.
> **Reserve for genuine high-stakes days only:** the Tier-1 tools from [[Part 3.0 - Cognitive Performance|Part 3.0]] — never as daily drivers.
> **Always:** change one variable at a time and watch your [[Part 1.1 - Measuring Cognition|deep-work minutes, task latency, and weekly PRs]]. A stack that doesn't move the numbers is just expensive urine.

## Part 4 Takeaways

> [!check] Key concepts to internalise
>
> - **Build in Tier 2.** A daily stack should be the safe, legal, sustainable layer; Tier 1 is for rare high-stakes days only.
> - **Dose for the clinical optimum, not convenience.** Fix the **2:1 theanine-to-caffeine** ratio, hit Bacopa at ~300 mg, and **cycle Huperzine-A.**
> - **The pre-made trap:** one locked ratio forces you to over-caffeinate just to dose the herbs. DIY separates the stimulant from the structure.
> - **Add Lion's Mane, creatine (3–5 g), and PS** — synergistic. **Skip DMAE** (cholinergic overload). **Rhodiola is a swing weapon**, not a daily driver. **Pregnenolone/DHEA are test-first hormones**, not stack adds.
> - **Time it dynamically:** Saffron AM, Rhodiola pre-workout, L-theanine pre-bed — and protect sleep from evening Rhodiola.
> - **Cost is about *monthly burn*, not upfront.** DIY runs ~RM 150–185/month (the Alpha-GPC bottle choice is the biggest lever); the double-dosed DIY beats a single pre-made serving on both potency and price. The convenience premium of pre-made buys you one scoop instead of ten bottles.

## Your Build Task List

1. **Audit your current intake against the dosage table.** Fix the theanine:caffeine ratio first — it's the cheapest win.
2. **Decide DIY vs pre-made** using *monthly burn*, not the scary upfront number, and your honest tolerance for swallowing 10 capsules a day.
3. **Set the choline source.** Default to CDP-choline given the [[Part 3.0 - Cognitive Performance|alpha-GPC stroke signal]] and your on-cycle cardiovascular load.
4. **Build the timing schedule** around your real day (work → train → sleep). Put theanine pre-bed if you run an evening Rhodiola.
5. **Add only Lion's Mane + 3–5 g creatine** to start (the clean synergistic pair). Resist piling on more.
6. **Run it as a single-variable experiment** and judge it on the [[Part 1.1 - Measuring Cognition|numbers]] over 3–4 weeks, not on vibes.
