## Coverage legend

- **Solid** = full article(s) exist and are published (`draft: false`).
- **Stub** = the home exists but is a scaffold (`draft: true`) waiting to be fleshed out.
- **Scattered** = the topic is only touched incidentally inside other articles, with no dedicated home.
- **Gap** = essentially absent and in-scope (this is where we missed out).
- **Out of scope** = deliberately not our competency.

## Where we actually missed out (the real gaps, in-scope)

In rough priority order (most load-bearing first):

1. **Gut Health / Nutrition.** The single biggest hole. `Healthy/Nutrition` is a stub, yet it's the "fuel lever" the Blueprint hub names and the macro foundation Fit 5.0/5.1 already reference. Building it unblocks several finished series. (Built 2026-05-28 per the Rich-section note below; this item is now closed.)
2. **Learning & Skill Acquisition.** ~~The folder is empty.~~ **Built 2026-05-30 — COMPLETE 7-article series** in `Successful/Learning and Skill Acquisition/` (Parts 1.0/2.0/2.1/3.0/3.1/4.0/5.0, ~25k words). The "software that runs on the hardware" that Cognitive Enhancement 4.0 promised. Two-layer foundation (physiological + cognitive map), the 4-step method (history → narrative → root → practice), deliberate-practice depth, day-shape + NSDR, Obsidian+Excalidraw system, and a learning-specific router into the [[Part 4.0 - The Escalation Protocol|Cognitive 3-tier escalation]] (Daily Baseline / Heavy Learning Day / Sprint with Noopept-or-Aniracetam SWAP). This item is now closed.
3. **Hormones & Endocrine (natural / Healthy angle).** The enhanced angle is deep in PE, but the non-enhanced reader has no Healthy-section home since the old hormones file was absorbed. Worth a Blueprint article or section.
4. **The "small organs" Healthy cluster: Vision, Hearing, and a consolidated Tools article (light / cold / heat).** All three are either a passing mention or absent. They could be one "the rest of the dashboard" Blueprint article rather than three.
5. **Social / Confidence.** Only planned (Appearance 6.0). The signaling side belongs in Appearance; the inner-game / behavior side belongs in Successful.
6. **Pain / injury management.** Inflammation-as-biomarker is fine; what's thin is practical musculoskeletal pain, joints, and injury, which fits Fit / Athletic alongside Mobility.

**Deliberately out of scope** (your call, confirmed by the content): Immune System & Disease, clinical Addiction & Substance Use, and Hypnosis specifically.
