# Content Vault Agent Guide

Before working in content, read ../pa-control/AGENTS.md and its required governance files.

## Purpose

The content folder is the first frontier for Nadeem's writing series: public philosophy, education, models, essays, and selected evidence-backed artifacts. It is authoritative for published prose, not for current private finance, health, biomarker, PED, or business records.

## Rules

1. Preserve the series' conceptual and editorial consistency.
2. Private operating data belongs in ../operating-system after migration.
3. Business/project records belong in ../build-blog/build-vault.
4. Career decisions and capability learning belong in ../career-path/career-path, governed by PA Control.
5. Do not publish private amounts, biomarkers, symptoms, purchasing records, credentials, PED details, or regulated-health claims without explicit approval and appropriate evidence.
6. Content may explain Operating System principles and use sanitized examples; it must not become the live database.
7. The private Operating System was migrated and became canonical on 2026-07-12. Content should retain only public principles and sanitized examples; do not recreate private operational records here.
8. Publishing, material thesis changes, and use of private evidence always require approval.
